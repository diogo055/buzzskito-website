- **A lastmod must be a true date.** Blog posts are listed with their publish date from `lib/constants.ts`. City and service pages share `LAST_CONTENT_UPDATE`; leave it unless they are actually rewritten.
- **`/_next/` is not blocked in `app/robots.ts`** (it was until Oct 11, 2026). It holds the CSS, the JavaScript and the address of almost every image; blocking it made Google render pages unstyled and kept images out of Google Images and away from Pinterest's crawler. Do not add it back.

- **Page keeper (Oct 20, 2026, `lib/page-keeper.ts`, inline in `app/layout.tsx` head):** Google sometimes stores a blank or "Application error" render of a page and folds it into other Next.js sites showing the same screen ("Duplicate without user-selected canonical", a casino or cinema as canonical; 7 of 105 fresh guides on Oct 20).
