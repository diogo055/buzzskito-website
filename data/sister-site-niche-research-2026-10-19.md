# Sister-site niche research (Oct 19, 2026)

Owner's question: which adjacent niche makes the most money (Mediavine + Amazon) as a second site linked from
buzzskito.ca, ranked by speed to the first $10,000 and by growth. Ahrefs US data, ~1,900 units. Volumes are
Ahrefs estimates (on our own site US impressions run ~0.26 per Ahrefs search): use them to compare niches, not
as forecasts. "Prize" = Ahrefs traffic potential (traffic of the #1 page) summed over the sample searches.

Weed-name and "possum"/"mealybugs"/"creeping charlie" searches have big volume but tiny traffic potential:
Google answers them on its own page (pictures, knowledge panel), so few clicks reach any site.

## Independent niche sites are the ones that collapsed (Ahrefs, Oct 19)

leafyplace.com 1,937 visits/mo (DR 47), smartgardenguide.com ~0 (DR 48), plantophiles.com 266 (DR 52),
birdwatchinghq.com 5,290 (DR 52), birdfeederhub.com 265 (DR 28), lawnphix.com 2,419 (DR 21).
Still winning: big brands (gardeningknowhow.com 220K, DR 81; lawnstarter.com 208K, DR 75; epicgardening.com
105K, DR 74; wildlife-removal.com 23K) and, in pest, real companies (bettertermite, Fox Pest:
200k-comparables.md). Same pattern as the pest collapses since Google's Sep 2023 update
(comparables-research.md): sites with a real business or expert behind them keep their traffic.

## Conclusions given to the owner

1. Fastest $10K and lowest risk: put the adjacent PEST topics on buzzskito.ca, which already has a real licensed
   company behind it: pests on pets (fleas, ticks, ear mites, lice; vet topics like worms and hot spots stay out),
   houseplant pests (fungus gnats, spider mites, mealybugs, thrips, aphids), garden insects, moisture bugs,
   wildlife droppings (raccoon poop is already in the droppings family).
2. Best separate site, later: home moisture & mold (year-round, easy long tail, high-ticket Amazon, links from
   our moisture-pest pages are relevant), then lawn & weeds (very easy, seasonal, US audience; Google answers
   many weed names itself). Skip birds (hardest; niche sites there collapsed).
3. A new site realistically needs 18-30 months to its first $10K: a new domain earns trust slowly, Mediavine
   requires its own traffic minimum per site (sources disagree on 50K sessions or ~$5K a year in ad revenue),
   and every page for it is taken from BuzzSkito's weekly Claude allowance. A link from buzzskito.ca helps a
   little (Ahrefs DR 1.9; the Semrush Authority Score of 22 is a different scale). Launch it only after the
   Dec 15 checkpoint proves the engine, with a real person or business behind it.
