# Amazon boxes that need the owner's decision (verified during the Oct 23 converter push)

The upgrade agents never changed an Amazon element: product name, button text, `search=`, `asin=` or tag. There
was one exception, the rat poison page, where box wording was corrected to match the labels. Its attributes are
unchanged, and an independent checker verified the new wording.

Everything below was verified by an independent checker. Health Canada's live registry refused connections all
evening, so registration facts come from Health Canada's own registry export of 3 Oct 2026 (archived copy) and from
label files. Recheck any item at https://pest-control.canada.ca/pesticide-registry/en/product-search.html before
acting.

**Why it matters:** the site belongs to a licensed operator. Canada's Pest Control Products Act (s. 6(1)) bars
using an unregistered pest control product, and Health Canada's advertising rules (DIR2016-01) bar "safe" claims.
A box that recommends an unregistered product for pest use, or calls a product "registered" or "legal" when it is
not, is the liability the site rules warn about. Fixing the wording of a box keeps the link and the income.

## B. Product facts (wrong specs or names)

- best-wasp-nest-spray-canada: boxes call Raid 7 a "foam" (lines 157-158, 177, 197-234, 423, 435). It is a 5.7 m jet spray.
- best-spider-spray-canada: "Konk OnGuard" does not exist (lines 161-163, 227, 314, 335). Konk is Acuity's brand and OnGuard is UR-CAN's.
- best-raccoon-live-trap-canada: "X-Large, ~32-inch" is wrong. Havahart's X-Large (1081) is 42 inches, and the Duke pick (HD Large 1112) is 28 inches.
- motion-activated-sprinkler-canada: boxes say "9V", but Orbit lists 4 AA. "Yard Enforcer Pro" does not exist.
- mosquito-repellent-guide-ontario-2026: Natrapel AwardRow says "Odourless", but the maker lists a fragrance.
- best-battery-backpack-sprayer-canada: AwardRow (line 170) says lithium. PetraTools says the HD4000 is a 12V 8Ah lead-acid. Pump-sprayer and patio-heater boxes repeat claims the checkers cut from the page text (pump lines 122-123, 151-156, 189, 234, 282; patio 138, 213).
- best-steam-cleaner-for-pest-control-canada: Vapamore "continuous fill" is false. Its manual says to cool down before removing the water tank cap.
- best-fruit-fly-trap-canada: boxes say to buy a TERRO "refill lure", but TERRO says "We do not sell refills" (lines 169, 195).
- best-indoor-fly-mosquito-trap-canada: the 1/4-acre DynaTrap is an outdoor trap, but the page sells it as ~600 sq ft indoors.
- best-rat-trap-canada: Victor M201 "Metal Pedal" is officially discontinued (Victor). best-mouse-trap-canada: the M250S "5 seconds / 100 kills" claim is not on Victor's page.
