## 5. What a US reader needs that a Canadian page omits

### US species and ranges (our biggest untapped differentiator)

Nobody in the top 15 does this properly. We name only genera plus *Ae. aegypti* / *Ae. albopictus*. A US reader needs:

- **Culex pipiens** (northern house mosquito) — northern US WNV vector, dusk into night.
- **Culex quinquefasciatus** (southern house mosquito) — the southern US counterpart.
- **Culex tarsalis** — **the western US WNV vector.** Named by nobody in the top 15. Open goal.
- **Aedes albopictus** (Asian tiger) — aggressive **daytime** biter, established across the Southeast and pushing north.
- **Aedes aegypti** — Gulf Coast, Florida, south Texas, Arizona; daytime biter.
- **Aedes sollicitans** — eastern salt-marsh mosquito; explains coastal/tidal-cycle biting.
- **Psorophora ciliata** ("gallinipper") — Gulf and Southeast; the outsized-bite question.
- **CDC potential-range framing:** CDC's estimated range maps for *Ae. aegypti* and *Ae. albopictus* use "county-level records, historical records, and suitable climate variables" to predict whether they could survive "if introduced to an area during the months when mosquitoes are locally active." **Carry CDC's caveat:** the maps "do not represent risk for spread of disease," and populations "may be found in an area that is not shaded on the maps and may not be found in all shaded areas."

### US seasonality and regions

Our page's only seasonal statement is *"in much of North America that is roughly June through August"* — and our only geography is **Oakville** and **Vaughan, Ontario**. A US reader gets nothing. Needed: the four-region month bands (above), a peak/worst-month answer (**July–August** nationally, shifting May–September by region; Gulf Coast/Florida Feb–Nov; Alaska a compressed June–July), and the fact that **southern and coastal states run far longer seasons than the GTA's May–September**.

### US agencies

We cite **one** CDC link, in a table footnote, and mention "EPA/Health Canada–recognized" in passing. US readers and AI Overviews weight `.gov` and `.edu`. Additive citations available:

- **CDC** — Mosquitoes hub; potential-range-of-Aedes toolkit; the WNV vector-species list (1999–2016).
- **EPA** — the repellent-search tool for registered active ingredients (DEET, picaridin, IR3535, OLE/PMD).
- **AMCA** (American Mosquito Control Association) — Integrated Mosquito Management. **Cited by both #3 and #4; we cite it zero times.**
- **State extension services** — none cited. Obvious picks: University of Florida IFAS, Texas A&M AgriLife Extension, Rutgers Center for Vector Biology (NJ).

### Units

Every threshold on our page is **Celsius-first**: "10 C (50 F)", "24–28 C (75–82 F)", "35 C (95 F)", "-2 C", and wind "16 km/h (10 mph)". Terminix leads with "50 degrees Fahrenheit." A US reader scanning for **50°F** finds it in parentheses, and extraction systems favour the primary unit.
