How each channel works, in short:

1. **Missing press links.** Drafts 01 and 02. After that, Google Alerts for "BuzzSkito", "Buzzskito" and "Nick Dudlak" (Alex sets them up once, two minutes, free) so every future mention gets a polite one-line request within a week. One email per mention, no follow-up: it is a favour.
2. **"Newer numbers" emails.** Ontario hiking, dog, cottage, camping, school and university pages about ticks often carry old numbers (two checked today, a hiking blog and a school lesson plan, both rely on 2019 sources). We have source-cited 2024-2025 figures on free pages with a "Cite this page" block (`/ticks-in-ontario-statistics`, `/lyme-disease-canada-statistics`), real-photo ID pages (`/blog/what-ticks-look-like-ontario`, `/blog/bugs-that-look-like-ticks`), the `/tools/bug-bite-identifier` and the US state tick pages (`/learn/ticks-by-state`). Each email names their page, gives one or two facts, offers one link, asks nothing else. US .gov/.edu library guides almost never link to companies (checked one today: only .gov and .edu links), so US effort goes to channel 3 and the spring data push instead. Cockroach, mouse and bed bug pages (tenant groups, university housing pages): test 10 emails in January before doing more.
3. **Journalist-request platforms.** Checked live today:
   - **HARO** (helpareporter.com): free daily query emails. Bought by Featured.com in April 2025 after Cision's Connectively shut down on Dec 9, 2024.
   - **Source of Sources** (sourceofsources.com): free, up to three emails a day, strict "only reply if you have something useful" rule.
   - **Qwoted**: free Basic plan = 2 pitches a month with a 2-hour delay; Pro is US$1,188 a year for 35 a month. Use the 2 free pitches on the best-fitting big outlets.
   - **Featured.com** (now also shows the Connectively name): free tier with a small daily allowance; paid from US$39 a month.
   - SourceBottle (free, mostly Australian outlets) and MentionMatch (the old Help a B2B Writer, business-to-business topics only) are not a fit now. Terkel became Featured in 2023.
   - Alex answers as a **mosquito and tick specialist**: ticks, mosquitoes, bites, Lyme and West Nile numbers, yards and outdoor pests, what professional treatment involves and costs. For mice, roaches and bed bugs, only identification questions our guides answer, said plainly ("BuzzSkito publishes free pest ID guides"), never as an exterminator of those pests. This keeps the brand's "GTA tick experts" identity.
   - No paid plan until the free ones have run through March. Expect few tick questions in winter and many from March.
4. **Seasonal news pitches.** Our proven channel: four stories in nine days in May 2026. Angles we own: fall ticks (adult blacklegged ticks most active in September-October; active any day above 4°C, per Public Health Ontario), Lyme counts (Canada 5,809 in 2024 and a preliminary 7,105 in 2025; Ontario 2,369 in 2024, the most of any province, per PHAC), tick season start in spring, West Nile in August. Spring US push: localized "ticks in [state]" pitches to US local outlets in March with the state pages (CDC county data, maps, real photos). We have **no original winter data on mice, cockroaches or bed bugs**, so we do not pitch those as news: borrowed numbers presented as ours would be dishonest and journalists would cite the original source anyway.
5. **Podcasts and local radio.** Zoomer Radio AM740's "The Garden Show with Charlie Dobbin" (Saturdays 9-10 am, takes calls, has guests) and the "Canada's Local Gardener" podcast both fit a spring tick-and-mosquito segment. Pitch in late January or February. Weak for links, good for GTA homeowners (the $1,222 customer).
6. **Photo and chart credit.** Most photos on our guides belong to other people (public domain or Creative Commons), so we cannot ask for credit on them. What is ours: the GTA pressure map, data-page charts and any real photos Alex takes. Idea for spring: Alex photographs real ticks on a card with a ruler, mosquito larvae in a cup, a typical GTA yard; we publish them under CC BY (free to reuse with a credit link). Small, but it compounds.
7. **Listings and associations.** Draft 10 (free listings) and draft 06 (SPMAO). Their links are mostly nofollow, so they barely move the US guides, but they make the business look established to Google, Bing, Apple Maps and AI assistants, which helps the service side most. Paid options for later (owner's call): SPMAO membership, Mississauga Board of Trade, Landscape Ontario (LawnSavers' biggest link source, per the July kit).

**Never (Google's spam policies, and our own rules):** paying for a link or a "link insertion"; giving free service, products or money in exchange for a link (that counts as a paid link); link swaps; private blog networks; guest posts written to place links, AI-written or not; fake people or invented experts; blog-comment or forum spam; paid directory bundles or "citation services"; adding our own site to Wikipedia (conflict of interest, and the links are nofollow); "scholarship" pages; replying to the spam sellers who will email after any outreach. Any sponsorship we ever pay for must carry rel="sponsored". Every claim in a pitch comes from our pages and their public sources; nothing says our service prevents disease; no "Health Canada approved"; no "safe for kids and pets" promises.

## 5. How we measure, and how it feeds the monthly goal check

**Monthly count of real linking sites** (Claude, about 1,000 Ahrefs units a month; the plan has 200,000 and resets on the 26th):
1. Ahrefs backlinks-stats for buzzskito.ca (50 units).
2. Ahrefs all-backlinks, one row per domain, new since the last check (first_seen_link on or after the 1st of last month), with page, target, DR and followed or not (about 6 units a row).
3. Classify each new domain with the rules below, check new "real" ones in their page code (is the link there, is it followed), and add them to the link log.
4. Cross-check: Alex opens Search Console, Links, "Top linking sites" once a month and reads out the total (Google's own view, 2 minutes). Ahrefs misses some real links (it has not counted CityNews), and Search Console's links report has no API.

**Spam rules (do not count):** .shop and .site link farms; pages named like "all/524/9.html"; anchors that sell SEO; AI prompt-copy sites such as parse.gl; scrapers that copy our text. **Real (count):** a person or organization chose to mention BuzzSkito or our content: news, blogs, organizations, schools, our own business listings.

**What we report each month:** new real linking sites (and the running total), how many are followed, how many from DR 20+ sites, against the cautious and realistic lines in section 3.

**Did the links move rankings?** For each page that gains a real followed link: Search Console average position and impressions for the 28 days before the link, against days 15-42 after it, compared with 5 similar pages (same type and topic, similar starting position) that gained no link. With a handful of links this is a signal, not proof.
