## Evidence

### 1. Method

1. **Candidate sweep.** Every pest family in the brief, in two ways: discovery calls (Ahrefs matching terms for "bug/bugs", "look like", "spider" and "bites", filtered to US volume ≥ 4,000-15,000 and KD ≤ 22-25) and 122 named searches checked one by one (spider species, household bugs, flies, ants, wasps, rodents, droppings, fleas, ticks, mites, caterpillars, cockroaches, garden pests, look-alikes). Then TP for the 60 finalists, KD and TP for 10 more, and results-page checks for 6.
2. **Coverage check.** Each candidate was checked against the 186 live guides, the 531 articles in `lib/article-index.json` and the 228 planned pages not yet live in `plan.src.json` (including side targets). Live targets were excluded unless the page ranks badly; those went to the upgrade list.
3. **Score** (section 3). It ranks; it does not forecast.
4. **Upgrades.** For 50 pages, every query with its impressions and position (Search Console, query view filtered by page). Gain = impressions at positions 4-15 × top-3 click-through − clicks they already get. Frozen pages, pages retrofitted since Oct 16 and product-review pages were set aside.

### 3. Scoring (all 49 candidates)

- **B (demand)** = √(TP × 0.3 × cluster volume): the geometric mean of what the #1 page earns and 30% of the searches. A big Ahrefs volume with a tiny TP (misattributed, or answered on the results page) cannot dominate.
- **R (chance of ranking)** by KD of the main search: 0-5 → 1.0; 6-10 → 0.8; 11-15 → 0.6; 16-20 → 0.45; 21-25 → 0.3; above 25 → 0.12.
- **F (format and results page):** 1.0 for identification, bites, signs, look-alike and comparison pages with small sites or forums in the top 10; 0.8 for curiosity species; 0.7 for how-to pages, non-insects and mixed intent; 0.5 if it would compete with our own live page; 0.3 for a mostly non-North-American topic.
- **C (our cluster):** 1.2 cockroaches and ticks, 1.1 spiders, bed bugs, fleas and ants, 1.0 household and mice, 0.9 wasps and US flies (no US pages yet).
- **M (Canada searches it too):** 1.15 if the pest is common in Canada and none of our Canadian pages already serves that search, else 1.0 (so the US editions, drain flies, horse fly, june bug and bed bug eggs get 1.0).
- **S (season, for "right now"):** 1.0 holds in winter or is in season now; 0.95 spring; 0.9 summer-fall with some winter interest; 0.85 summer page that is new or planned for Feb; 0.7 summer page already planned for December (moving it up adds little).
- **Score** = B × R × F × C × M × S. **Model clicks** = score × 1%: a rough in-season month at maturity at positions 5-8 (about 0.26 Search Console impressions per Ahrefs search, measured in `path-to-50k.md`, × 0.6-1.3% click-through, folded into TP). Seasonality was judged, not pulled (monthly volume history costs units).
