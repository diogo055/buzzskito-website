# What wins per page, the mosquito search map, and the 10 strongest new pages (Nov 5, 2026)

The owner asked two things on Nov 5:
- "Is the mosquito universe massive, or do we just have a lot of mosquito pages? What's our most powerful type of page?"
- "What is the combination of 10 pages that is literally the most powerful that we can make right now... don't repeat anything we have."

All the numbers are Google Search Console web clicks unless marked otherwise. Scripts: session scratchpad `mosquito-analysis/` (`analyze_power.py`, `mosquito_universe.py`). The page list is `scripts/site-map/`.

## 1. Clicks per page by type (last 28 days, Oct 8-Nov 4)

"Mature" means the page had Search Console impressions by the end of August. A hit is a page with 30+ clicks in 28 days.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bites & stings | 31 | **39** | 67 | 59% | 6,739 |

| Service, city & home | 177 | 0 | 0.6 | 0% | 18 |

This is the same order as the Oct 26 measurement (bites 45, identification 33, droppings 14), so it holds.

**Pages that first showed in Google in October (257 pages, their first weeks, in winter):**
- Identification starts best. Bugs that look like cockroaches got 117 clicks in 28 days, bugs that look like fleas 102, tiny brown bugs 92, tiny black bugs 72.
- Droppings and signs come next: animal droppings 62, rat droppings 25.
- October's bite pages are small so far (median 5.5), because most bites are summer searches.

| Mosquitoes | 86 | 2 (15 in August) | 15.6% | 22.1% |

**Mosquitoes are big because we have many pages, not because each page is strong:**
- Six pages bring 60% of mosquito clicks, and 24 of 86 had none in the last 28 days.
- Mature mosquito pages fell from about 118 clicks a day in August to about 57 now.
- All other information pages rose from about 30 a day in July to about 124.
- Inside mosquitoes the type rule holds: bite pages have a median of 33; the 35 product pages 2; the 20 remedy pages 1.

**Bed bugs and ticks are saturated:** about 80 pages each and low per-page yield. Build there only for a new, proven search.

**Reading it:**
- We have a page for nearly every big mosquito search. The gap is position and click rate, not missing pages.
- **mosquito larvae:** 42,700 impressions at position 9.6, on one page.
- **how long do mosquito bites last:** 42,600 impressions with all its variants. `why-do-mosquito-bites-itch` already carries that search in its title ("How Long Do Mosquito Bites Last & Itch? Timeline") and sits at position 8. A second page would compete with it, so this is push work (push queue #19).
- **Timing and biology** searches get 0.2-0.3% click rates: Google answers them in the results.

**Our own numbers can't see searches we have no page for.** The Google Trends and autocomplete pass for those is in `mosquito-trends-2026-11-05.md`.

**The December mosquito batch is the weakest page type.** 11 species and disease pages are held (section 6 of the God plan log has the list): Culex, Anopheles, floodwater mosquitoes, gallinipper, yellow fever mosquito, dengue, EEE, chikungunya, La Crosse, Jamestown Canyon and St. Louis encephalitis.
- Species pages have a median of 1 click.
- Health searches go to the CDC and health systems.
- `types-of-mosquitoes` and `mosquito-borne-diseases` cover them as sections instead.
- Kept: `asian-tiger-mosquito` and `skeeter-syndrome`.
