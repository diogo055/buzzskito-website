## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- Orkin gives two sizes (5-16 mm and 5-25 mm). UC IPM: about 3/4 inch; Penn State and Colorado State: about 5/8
  inch; UF/IFAS: the ringlegged earwig is 1/2 to 2/3 inch.
- Orkin: earwigs mainly eat live sprouts and decaying plants. Colorado State: their main food is insects, including
  aphids; UC IPM: important predators of aphids.
- Orkin: "earwig larvae". UC IPM: immature earwigs look like adults except smaller and wingless.
- Orkin: pinches "rarely break skin" (unsourced). UC IPM: they can bite if trapped in clothing or sat upon;
  Colorado State: a mildly painful bite when sat upon or handled.
- NCAP: 20-50 eggs. Sources differ (UC IPM: 30 or more; Penn State: 30 to 55 in the first batch; Wisconsin: 50-90),
  so our page gives the figures by source.
