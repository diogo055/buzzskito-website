## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- PetMD: flea dirt "itself is not harmful". CDC (About Flea-borne Typhus, Dec 11, 2025): infected flea dirt rubbed into a
  bite or wound, breathed in or rubbed into the eyes spreads typhus; CDC About Fleas: germs spread when infected flea feces
  ("flea dirt") are scratched into an open wound.
- VCA: cat scratch disease reaches people through the cat's saliva in a wound. CDC: usually a scratch, the bacteria
  passing when it is contaminated with flea feces; bites and licks also can.
- All three: "digested blood". UF: relatively undigested blood; Ohio State: the excess blood adults take in beyond what
  they need to digest; Texas A&M: partially digested.
- PetMD and Leon Valley treat a red smear as the answer. Tennessee: bed bug feces also run red when moistened.
- PetMD's "crevices of your dog's ears" is in no source opened (Ohio State, UMN: neck, tail base, belly...). Left out.
