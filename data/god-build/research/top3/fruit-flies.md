## What they get wrong, and what our page says instead
- Food Network "from larvae to adult within a week": Kentucky says egg to adult in about a week; Texas A&M as few as 8
  days; Wisconsin 8 to 10 days.
- Food Network "wash produce to remove eggs": Kentucky says to cut away and discard cracked or damaged parts in case
  eggs or larvae are in the wound, and to eat, discard or refrigerate ripe produce.
- Bleach: Healthline wipes trash cans with it and many readers pour it down drains; Iowa State says there is no
  benefit to treating drains with bleach or ammonia, and our page says so.
- The Kitchn trap-only approach: Kentucky says the problem continues no matter how often insecticides are applied
  unless breeding sites are removed; Texas A&M calls traps and sprays temporary relief.
- Iowa State gives fruit flies as 1/4 inch long; Kentucky and Texas A&M give about 1/8 inch, which our page uses.
