## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- Mouse Blocker: mice avoid ultrasound and never get used to it. UC IPM and Nebraska G1105: ultrasound is directional, does
  not get behind objects, fades with distance; mice soon get used to repeated sounds; little evidence any device works.
- Mouse Blocker, Fox: live traps and release. CDC: no live traps (fright makes rodents urinate); Nebraska: do not relocate.
- Mouse Blocker, bettertermite: a dead mouse smells for weeks. Cornell IPM: days for a mouse, weeks for a rat.
- Scotts: bait as the popular fix, no word on odor. UC IPM: rodenticide often leaves mice dying inside the structure;
  Nebraska: where a poisoned mouse dies cannot be predicted; going outside for water is hopeful speculation.
- Scotts, Mouse Blocker, Fox: a dime-sized hole. CDC: the width of a pencil, 1/4 inch; Nebraska: slightly over 1/4 inch.
- ClearDefense: glue boards every 8 to 12 feet. CDC: do not use glue traps.
