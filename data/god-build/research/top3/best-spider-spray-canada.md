## For the owner: affiliate lines that look wrong (left untouched)
- **"Konk OnGuard Residual Surface Spray"** (Best Alternative award, comparison-table row, two buttons, search
  "konk onguard residual insecticide spray"): the registry has no product by that name. KONK is Acuity Holdings'
  brand (its spider can is Konk Spider & Tick II, 32619); OnGuard is a separate brand of UR-CAN Inc. (for example
  OnGuard PNP Liquid Residual 32223). The search term may land on either.
- "Knock Down residual insecticide spray" is fine (Knock Down Spider Killer 29689 is current).
