## The three, taken apart
- **Wikipedia "Maggot"** (~1,000 words): "larva of a fly"; not a technical term, used for Brachycera, not mosquitoes or crane
  flies; uses: fishing bait, **maggot therapy** (Lucilia sericata), **forensic science** (time of death, drugs in tissue);
  myiasis; home advice from a pest-company blog. Photos: carcasses, myiasis. No size, no photo at home, no FAQ.
- **Britannica** (~700 words, Oct 28 reading): tapering, pale, legless, **mouth hooks**, 3 to 8 instars, first instar about
  0.08 inch; myiasis, **maggot therapy, forensics**, casu marzu. Drawing of the house fly life cycle.
- **Mental Floss** (~2,200 words, 2017): 15 facts: life cycle (adults about 10 days later), **hooked mouthparts** and
  outside digestion, heat in maggot masses, **forensics**, **wound healing and the FDA's 2004 clearance**, black soldier
  fly composting and feed, rat-tailed maggots, the bot fly. Links news and studies; one carcass photo.
- Terminix "baby flies": fly eggs "like small grains of rice", maggots 0.08-0.4 inch, egg to adult 7-14 days; no sources.
