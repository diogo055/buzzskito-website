## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- AI overview / Wikipedia: "1 to 11 mm" as the cellar spider's size. Penn State: long-bodied females 7-8 mm, males 6 mm;
  Clemson: short-bodied females 1/16 inch.
- AI overview: cellar spiders bite with harmless venom. Penn State: not known to bite people; UC Riverside: no reference to
  any pholcid bite causing a harmful reaction. Our page says neither "harmless bite" nor "cannot bite".
- Bug Out: fangs "not capable of piercing human skin" as fact. UC Riverside: uncate fangs like a recluse's, no scientific
  basis for "too short"; UC IPM: too short "in most cases". Our page gives both.
- bettertermite: "This single eye pair is found on all harvestmen". UC Riverside: "at most 2 eyes".
- Wikipedia, Australian Museum: a TV test as evidence. We cite Vetter, the Burke Museum and MDC instead.
- Reddit (#2 for vs harvestman): "Harvestmen are mites". UC Riverside, MDC: their own order, Opiliones.
- Not ours to fix: /blog/bugs-that-look-like-ticks gives harvestmen "a visible waist between two body sections" (MDC, UC
  Riverside: one body, no waist). Reported, not edited.
