## What they get wrong (and what our sources say)
- H&G: egg to adult "four to six weeks". UC IPM: 60 days above 75°F, up to 500 days below 50°F.
- H&G: silverfish rarely come in "without a transportation method". UC IPM: large numbers can invade
  new homes from surrounding wild areas; Missouri adds boxes, cartons and furniture.
- Orkin: "reproduce rapidly". Virginia Tech: populations build slowly; a large infestation usually
  means a building has been infested a while (Ohio State and Clemson agree).
- Aptive: silverfish chew "plastic packaging" (no source found for it); it lists cardboard as a
  target, while UMD Extension says newsprint, cardboard and brown wrapping paper are seldom attacked.
- All three: "pepper-like" droppings, unsourced. Kentucky (ENTFACT-614) uses "pepper-like specks"
  for COCKROACH droppings; the NPS IPM manual describes silverfish droppings as small, dark and
  visible to the eye, and UC IPM's damage photo shows fecal debris as lighter brown spots on paper.
