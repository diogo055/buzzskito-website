## What ours does that none of them do
1. **Every life stage in real, credited CDC photos** (eggs, maggot, pupa, a house fly emerging), plus two look-alikes photographed where people find them (a carpet beetle larva on a house floor, moth larvae in a bag of peanuts), and look-alike photo cards. Theirs: carcasses, a drawing, or nothing.
2. **A quick-ID table with sourced sizes in inches**: house fly and blow fly maggots against drain fly, fungus gnat, pantry moth, carpet beetle, mealworm, soldier fly and mosquito larvae; and a life-cycle table with hours and days per stage (Missouri, NC State, Texas A&M).
3. **The one-line test** (legless, no dark head capsule, pointed head end, blunt rear) and **"where did they come from?" by place**: trash can, kitchen, floor or ceiling (dead animal, forgotten rodent traps per Purdue), winter, dog droppings and compost.
4. **Extension-strength removal**: boiling water for cans (Virginia Cooperative Extension), a bleach rinse (NC State), sweep or vacuum indoors (Iowa State), weekly pickup and sealed bags (UC IPM); and the drain-fly exception (UMN: boiling water and bleach do not work in drains).
5. **Health at CDC, FDA and USDA strength**: myiasis is not common in the US (CDC); FDA defect levels; CDC's food-poisoning warning signs; USDA's late-October 2026 screwworm status, dated.

## What they get wrong or leave out (ours states it from the source)
- WebMD: a single "5-10 days" life cycle. Texas A&M: about 45 days at 60°F against 7 days at 95°F; Missouri: 6 days to 5 weeks.
- WebMD recommends diatomaceous earth, bug zappers and attracting birds and reptiles; none of the extension sources we opened recommends them for maggots. UC IPM: pesticides are not needed or recommended in most home situations; remove the breeding material.
- None of the three says that maggots on a floor usually mean an animal died in the structure (Texas A&M) or that they are wandering to pupate (Texas A&M, Missouri: up to 50 feet a day).
- Wikipedia's home advice rests on a pest company blog; ours rests on extension guides.
