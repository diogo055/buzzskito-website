## Corrected (false against the primary source)
- "Health Canada does not require PMRA registration for insect traps that work mechanically" (FAQ), "needs no PMRA
  registration" (Quick Answer bullet), "needs no registration under Canada's Pest Control Products Act" (body), "Physical
  devices ... do not require PMRA registration" (PMRA section), key-facts row "no PMRA registration required": the Pest Control
  Products Regulations put devices sold to attract or destroy flying insects in Schedule 1 (no general device exemption,
  s. 3(1)(a)); Schedule 2 items 5-6 exempt only CSA C22.2 No. 189 high-voltage insect killers. Health Canada has registered
  indoor light traps as DEVICE products: STEM Flying Insect Light Trap 35296 and Raid Essentials Flying Insect Light Trap 35297

  rooms), STEM 35801, PIC Flying Insect Light Trap 35911, Veyofly Electronic Fly Trap Indoor 36005 (2026-08-12), Zevo Flying
  Insect Trap 6.5 36155 (2026-10-14). KATCHY: 0 rows.
