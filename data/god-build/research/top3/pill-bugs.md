## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- Treehugger: eggs carried "two to three months until they hatch"; Boulder: eggs develop "two to three months" and babies stay in
  the pouch "three or four days". UF EENY630: eggs hatch after three to four weeks, young stay one to two more weeks; UC IPM: eggs
  hatch about 1 month after being produced, young stay about 2 weeks.
- Treehugger: "Average Lifespan in the Wild: 1.5 years" (unsourced). UC IPM and UF: adults can live 2 to 5 years; Kentucky: ~3.
- Wikipedia: they eat "wood supports in houses", making them a house pest (citation needed). Idaho CIS 1169: not harmful to people,
  pets, stored foods, houseplants, furniture or home structural timbers; MDC: they do no damage when they enter buildings.
- Treehugger: "the only crustaceans that have adapted to living completely on land": not in any source opened; not repeated.
