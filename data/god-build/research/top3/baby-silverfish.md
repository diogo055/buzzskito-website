## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- WebMD: the young reach adulthood in 4 to 6 weeks. NC State says the young darken to adult color in 4 to 6 weeks;
  becoming adult takes 60 days above 75°F to 500 days below 50°F (UC IPM), or 3 months to 3 years (Missouri).
- silverfishinfo: hatchlings 2 to 4 mm. UC IPM: about 1/16 inch (about 1.6 mm).
- silverfishinfo: 2 to 20 eggs a batch. UC IPM: clutches average 50 (1 to 200); Penn State: 1 to 3 at a time; NC
  State: over 100 in a lifetime; Virginia Tech: usually fewer than 100.
- Orkin: scales "after several months" (no source). UC IPM: about 12 days after the fourth molt; Penn State: after
  several molts.
- Dr. Killigan's: sells sprays for baby silverfish. UC IPM: light infestations need no insecticide, only less water and
  physical controls.
