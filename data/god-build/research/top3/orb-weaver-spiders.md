## What they get wrong or leave loose
- Riveredge: "more than 3,500 kinds" of orb weavers making up "about a quarter of spider species" is unsourced and
  does not add up; our page gives only UC IPM's count (about 200 species in North America).
- Google's AI answers call garden spiders "completely harmless" with venom that is "harmless to humans"; Penn State
  reports verified cross orbweaver bites with pain, swelling, redness and sometimes systemic symptoms for two days to
  three weeks. Our page says bites are rare and usually sting-like, and gives the exception.
- "Barn spider" is used for two different spiders: MDC uses it for the spotted orbweaver (Neoscona crucifera), UMN
  for a larger yellow-and-brown orb weaver (Charlotte's model). Our page says so instead of picking one.
