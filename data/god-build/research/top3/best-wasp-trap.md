## Corrected (false against the primary source)
- **"Traps are devices — no Health Canada PMRA registration needed".** It was in the Quick Answer bullet, the FAQ, the
  key facts, and the PMRA section heading and paragraph. It is false for baited traps, on three counts:
  - the Pest Control Products Act s.2 counts a product used as a means for "attracting" a pest;
  - Sterling's three registrations above;
  - the Pest Control Products Regulations, Schedule 1 item 2, cover "Devices that are manufactured, represented or sold
    as a means to attract or destroy flying insects", with exemptions in Schedule 2 only on conditions.
