## What they all lack (and what this page now adds)
- **What the evidence says about each tool.** New table "What the Evidence Says About Each Tick Removal Tool", from
  ILCOR's first aid systematic review (2021 draft): pet trials Duscher 2012 (Tick Twister 7 of 108 ticks with damaged
  mouthparts vs 20 of 108 for a lasso remover; twisting 37/337 vs pulling 60/190) and Zenner 2006 (Tick Twister fewer
  damaged mouthparts than Buster forceps or Adson forceps, pet owners); human study Akin Belli 2016 (tweezers 33 of 40
  intact vs 3 of 40 for a card remover); no study measured disease transmission; chemicals, heat and freezing detached
  no ticks. Plus PHAC's and CDC's current removal instructions.
- **A dog section** ("Best Tick Remover for Dogs", 105 impressions of dog searches) with the pet trial evidence.
- **Answer-first opening**: tweezers for people (PHAC/CDC), the Tick Twister hook for dogs and engorged ticks.
- **Buyer's checklist** and **three FAQs**: best tool for humans, do tools beat tweezers, should you twist.
- **Three photos** (all CDC, public domain): adult and nymph blacklegged ticks on a fingertip (lead and share image),
  engorged blacklegged tick, dog's ear with brown dog ticks.
- **Rule fixes**: the H1 said "Tested for Ontario" and the description "tools tested" (this company does not test
  products): now "Compared". Amazon prices removed ("About $7 on Amazon", "$5–$10", "(under $15)", Cost bullets now
  price tiers).
