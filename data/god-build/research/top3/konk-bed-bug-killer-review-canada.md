## Corrections made (against the approved label, PCP 31110, submission 2023-3451, Dec 21, 2023)
- "Made in Canada" / "Canadian-made" (hero, quick answer, table, key facts, body): no source shows where it is made. The
  label lists Acuity Holdings, Inc., 11627-178 Street, Edmonton AB; the oldest KONK registrations belong to Air Guard Control
  (Canada) Limited. Prose now says exactly that; the key-facts row "Made in: Canada" became "Label address".
- "Pyrethrin/pyrethroid-based": the label is pyrethrins 0.50% + piperonyl butoxide 5.00% (pyrethrins are not pyrethroids).
- "Short residual", "its residual fades over days", "some formulations claim residual activity": the label says "Insects
  must be contacted to be killed" and makes no egg or residual claim.
- "Exact actives ... can change between production runs": the registered formulation is fixed; FAQ rewritten from the label.
- Harris and Ortho Home Defense "check the listing": Harris has no Canadian product; Ortho's Canadian cans are Bed Bug B Gon
  (33722). EcoRaider "check the Canadian listing": not registered. CimeXa "sold through Canadian retail": not registered.
- CrossFire/Temprid "lean on the same pyrethroid chemistry": CrossFire's EPA label (1021-2776) is clothianidin, metofluthrin,
  PBO; Temprid pairs imidacloprid with beta-cyfluthrin and is registered here only as commercial-class SC and ReadySpray.
- "Safe-use directions", "use it safely", "make the product safe and legal", "passed that review": reworded (DIR2016-01).
