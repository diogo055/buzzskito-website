## The three (plus two)
- **NC State Extension, "Phorid Flies"** (Crawley and Bertone, 2021; about 2,000 words with captions): the best page.
  Eight Bertone photos (live adult, pinned specimen, wing, puparium, fruit fly, two fungus gnats, black fly), no
  table, no FAQ, no sources. Good on wing venation, the breeding-site list, the plumber's scope and smoke test, tape or
  a clear bowl over drains. Gaps: no room-by-room search, no dried drain trap, no vinegar-trap warning, no sizes for
  the look-alikes, odd size line ("2/64 to 1/4 inch").
- **University of Maryland Extension, "Phorid Fly"** (about 150 words, one Bugwood photo): size, humped back, the
  first-floor sewer-line warning, "remove or dry out the material". No look-alikes, no method to find the source.
- **Orkin Canada, "Phorid Humpbacked Flies"** (about 1,200 words with service copy): no photo of the fly in the
  text, no sources. Contradicts itself (700 vs 500 eggs a lifetime; a 25-day cycle vs two weeks to a month; larval
  stage 8-16 days vs 1-2 weeks) and calls disease a "high risk" with nothing to support it.
- Wikipedia "Phoridae": taxonomy, fire ant parasitoids, coffin flies; no home guidance; sections tagged for missing
  citations. Virginia Tech ENTO-433NP (revised Aug 28, 2026, about 800 words, 2 photos): strong indoor breeding list
  (drip pans, tile grout, damp sponges, broken pipes under slabs), tape sticky-side down over drains, "consult a plumber".

## What they get wrong (our page states it correctly)
- Orkin's "high risk" of disease: NC State calls larval infestations of people "fairly rare" and says the flies are
  mainly nuisance pests; Virginia Tech and Kentucky say adults do not bite and may carry microbes to food.
- MSU's Bug's Eye View says dark eyes alone separate phorids from fruit flies, but Texas A&M and Kentucky describe
  fruit flies with black eyes (dark-eyed fruit flies breed in drains): our test adds the hump and the scuttling run.
- Orkin's single "typical 25 days": Texas A&M gives 14 to 37 days egg to adult, Tennessee 11 days to four weeks,
  depending on temperature. We give the range and why it matters (sprays buy days, the source restocks in weeks).
