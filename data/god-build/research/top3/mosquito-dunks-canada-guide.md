## What none of the three has, and our page now does

### 1. The Canadian answer in the first screen, from Health Canada's registry (searched Oct 23)
- MOSQUITO DUNKS, Reg. No. 28888, DOMESTIC, registrant Summit Chemical Company.
- Registered 2008-05-08, expiry 2028-12-31.
- The only BTI briquet with a domestic registration.

### 2. The Canadian label quoted, not the U.S. "100 sq ft"
Label 2023-3665, dated 2023-07-28, read as a PDF from the registry:
- "Use one (1) Mosquito Dunk for up to 6 sq. metres water surface, regardless of depth".
- ¼ dunk for 0.1–3.0 m², ½ for 3.0–6.0 m².
- "Allow a minimum of 48 hours for control".
- Reapply "at 30 day intervals if mosquito larvae are detected".
- On-property water only; "DO NOT apply" to ditches, drains, rain gutters or catch basin sumps that flow off the property.
- "use them within one year from date of manufacture".

### 3. A dose-by-water-size table
"How much dunk? The Canadian label, by size of water", with everyday examples and Health Canada's bird bath advice: change the water "at least twice a week".

## What they get wrong or leave vague
- **The "100 square feet" figure** that every shopping result uses is the U.S. label. The Canadian label is 6 m², about 65 sq ft.
