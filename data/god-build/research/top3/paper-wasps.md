## The three, taken apart
1. **Wikipedia, "Paper wasp"** (~1,200 words of body). Species counts, nests, brood rearing, behavior. ~15 images,
   none of a nest on a house and none with a scale. No size of the wasp or the nest, no table, no FAQ, no first aid,
   nothing on removal or on telling one from a yellowjacket beyond a nest photo. Sting: "quite painful", anaphylaxis
   possible, no care advice.
2. **PestWorld, NPMA** (~800 words, ~20 question headings). Fact box: 5/8-3/4 inch, "brownish with yellow markings".
   No sources cited. Sting care adds NSAIDs, antihistamines and hydrocortisone with no medical source; removal is
   "do not attempt it yourself" plus a pro-finder box (trade association). Says "about 22" North American species.
3. **Cornell IPM** (~650 words). Good on the European vs northern paper wasp and the open "umbrella" nest; 4 photos,

## What they get wrong or leave out (our page states it from the source)
- PestWorld's 5/8-3/4 inch is narrower than MDC's 1/2-1 inch for the genus (UC IPM and Texas A&M: about 1 inch).
- PestWorld's sting medicines have no source: we give Mayo Clinic's first-aid steps without doses.
- "Do not remove it yourself" (PestWorld) vs extension: UC IPM, Kentucky and Penn State all describe owner removal of
  accessible paper wasp nests, and UC IPM/Kentucky say many nests need no treatment at all.
- Cornell's northern paper wasp size mismatch: the page gives only MDC's genus range.
