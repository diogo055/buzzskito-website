## What they (or the results page) get wrong

- Missouri Botanical Garden: 3/8-inch hardware cloth. ICWDM, Penn State and the North Carolina Wildlife Resources Commission give 1/4-inch mesh or smaller.
- Missouri Botanical Garden: "a sure-fire vole weapon is a garden cat". UC IPM: in most cases predators can't keep vole populations below damaging levels.
- Missouri Botanical Garden: unsourced "15 voles per acre can grow to 250 in four years". Left out; our numbers are UC IPM's.
- Will County Forest Preserve (result 5): "Like moles, voles are rodents". MDC's eastern mole field guide: the eastern mole is not a rodent.
- Google's AI Overview: "Voles can carry diseases like hantavirus" (citing a pest company). CDC's 2002 recommendations: none of the hantaviruses carried by voles and lemmings had been associated with human disease in the US; CDC today: the deer mouse spreads the most common hantavirus behind HPS.
