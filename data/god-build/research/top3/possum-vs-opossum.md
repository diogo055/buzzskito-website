  background; a brushtail); no table, no FAQ, no sizes; cites Merriam-Webster, Grammarly, Dictionary.com and a wildlife
  rehabber page. Says both animals "play dead" and that opossums benefit gardeners by eating "ticks".

## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- Mental Floss and Trinka: both animals "play dead". Only the opossum is documented doing it in the sources opened (PGC,
  National Zoo, WDFW); no Australian source opened describes it, so the page does not claim it for possums.
- Mental Floss, Trinka: opossums are tick and rat controllers. Hennessy and Hild (2021) found no ticks in 32 wild opossum
  stomachs and none in 23 published diet studies; the page says so and links /blog/do-chickens-eat-ticks.
- Trinka: opossums are "Didelphimorphia" as a family (it is the order; the family is Didelphidae) and Europeans "created"
  the word opossum (PGC: from the Algonquin apasum).
- Not in the top 3 but worth recording: UF/IFAS's own opossum fact sheet says opossums are "nearly immune to rabies". The plan
  bars that wording; the page uses CDC, Iowa HHS ("almost never carry rabies") and Minnesota DH (a species of concern).
