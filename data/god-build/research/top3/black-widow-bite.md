## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- WebMD says pain eases "about 8 to 12 hours" after the bite. StatPearls: recovery usually within 24-48 h; MedlinePlus:
  severe symptoms usually start to improve within 2 to 3 days, milder ones may last several weeks.
- WebMD says bites "can be fatal" in one section and that no US deaths have been reported in its FAQ. MedlinePlus: death in
  a healthy person is very rare; young children, very ill and older people may not survive. Poison Control and Monte 2011:
  no deaths in the reviewed US poison center cases.
- WebMD (Google snippet): muscle pain "usually within 8 hours". MedlinePlus: after 15 minutes to 1 hour; StatPearls:
  cramping usually within an hour.
- Cleveland Clinic: "about 2,600 bites" a year. StatPearls says 2,600 exposures a year (2000-2008-era data, Monte);
  2012-2022 counted 15,299 in 11 years (Kerns). Our page gives the dated totals, not a yearly rate.
- Cleveland Clinic gives no timing at all; Poison Control names no higher-risk groups. UC IPM: bites are quite rare even
  where the spiders are common (stated on our page).
