## What they give the searcher that we did not
- A long, skinny black bug answer (millipedes, earwigs, silverfish, ground or rove beetles). We had none.
- Short answers to "round black bug", "black bugs in my bedroom" and "are they harmful". Our facts existed but were spread out.

## What we give that they do not
- Location sorter plus crawls / jumps / flies table, sourced sizes, credited photos with scale bars, which ones bite
  (with CDC), the note that many "black" bugs are reddish brown up close, links to every sibling guide.
