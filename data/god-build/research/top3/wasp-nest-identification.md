## What they get wrong (ours states it from the source)
- Best Bees: wasps "never" reuse a nest. MDC: old yellowjacket nests are usually not used again; honey bees keep the same nest year to year (Missouri Extension), blue mud daubers move into old mud dauber nests (Purdue), carpenter bees reuse tunnels (Missouri Extension). Its table gives paper wasp colonies 20-30 insects: UC IPM says 15 to 200, Penn State a few dozen and over 200 at times. It also gives a ladder-spatula-trash-bag removal while its FAQ says leave nests alone.
- Moxie: a single queen "builds the entire nest". Penn State: the queen starts it and rears the first workers, who then build as the colony grows. Bald-faced hornet nests "usually 10-12 feet" up: unsourced; MDC says usually high in trees or in shrubs, occasionally on buildings.
- Mosquito Joe: hornets "up to 1 1/2 inch", unsourced. Penn State: European hornet workers up to about 1 inch (queens about 1.3); MDC: bald-faced hornet workers 1/2 to 3/4 inch. Its regional "hotspot" claims have no source.
