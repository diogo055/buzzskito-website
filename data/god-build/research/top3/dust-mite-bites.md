## The three, taken apart
- **Healthline, Mite bites** (updated Nov 18, 2024; PA-C reviewer): ~1,300 words; h2s: can dust mites bite, symptoms,
  what bites look like (chiggers, scabies, Demodex, bird and rodent, oak, straw itch mites), how do I know, treatment.
  No real bite photos in the text we could read; no table; 7 thin references; permethrin named with no dosing; a paid
  telehealth ad mid-article. Says dust mites "can trigger allergic reactions that can resemble bites".
- **doctronic.ai, Dust mite bites** (reviewed May 10, 2026): ~1,700 words, no sources at all, no photos (an author headshot),
  one unsourced table (dust mite reactions vs bed bug vs flea bites), 5 FAQs, links to prescription-drug sales pages
  (an antibiotic among them). Describes dust mite "welts" from histamine as if typical.
- **WebMD, What to know about mites** (reviewed Mar 15, 2026): ~1,800 words over 11 mite types; one stock dust mite photo;
  no table; 4 FAQs; scabies drug doses; says dust mites cannot harm people by biting or stinging (correct), but lists
  "dust mites" and "house dust mites" as two entries and files wheezing under "mite bite symptoms".
- MNT (#4, June 2023): ~1,200 words, one stock dust mite image, a 2-column dust mites vs bed bugs table, Amazon links;
  says a "bite-like rash" comes from mite feces proteins.

## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- Healthline ("allergic reactions that can resemble bites") and MNT ("bite-like rash"): University of Kentucky ENTFACT-649
  (rev. Mar 2026) says the belief that dust mites cause itching and bite-like reactions is untrue. We give Kentucky's line and
  the eczema link only at MedlinePlus and AAD strength.
- doctronic: dust mite "welts" with a typical duration and pattern, no source. No health source opened describes dust mite
  welts; MedlinePlus and NIAMS describe eczema as scaly patches that come and go.
- MNT: dust mites "thrive at 68-77 F"; Kentucky ENTFACT-646 puts their optimum at about 75-80 F and 70-80% humidity. (Not
  needed on our page; left out.)
- WebMD/Healthline give permethrin and other drug doses; our plan bars doses: scabies goes to a provider (CDC).
