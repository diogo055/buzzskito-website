## Corrected on the page (false against the primary source)
- Canadian Patriot Plus model 'MM4200' -> MM4202B; Executive 'MM3302' -> MM3302B (Woodstream Canada).
- Patriot Plus net 'MM3100NET' / 'Small (MM3100NET class)' -> MM4200NETN (mosquitomagnet.com net page); Executive
  'MM3300NET' -> MM3300NETN.
- Net interval 'once or twice a season' / 'replace 1-2x per season' -> every 21 days (maker).
- 'Health Canada status: Not a pesticide, no PCP registration needed' (3 places) -> devices sold to attract or destroy
  flying insects are in Schedule 1 of the Pest Control Products Regulations (exemptions in Schedule 2 only for
  CSA-certified high-voltage insect killers), and the Octenol attractant carries PCP Reg. No. 28440.
