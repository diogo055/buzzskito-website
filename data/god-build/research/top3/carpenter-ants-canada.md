  "COMMON SWEET-EATING ANTS", not carpenter ants. No current borax or boric acid product names carpenter ants.

  to make room for their nests"; "Piles of sawdust may mean you have carpenter ants."; baiting rules (plenty of stations,
  two baits, at least two weeks, no sprays while baiting). https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/pest-control-tips/ants.html

  pesticide use ... inside buildings"; exception for use "integral to a structural extermination"; pesticides may be used
  for "pests that can cause structural damage to the home".
