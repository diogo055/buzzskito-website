## What they get wrong (ours states it from the source)
- **Size.** Skedaddle's "six to eight inches in diameter" is the inner chamber: MDC gives a twig frame 12 to 20 inches across around a hollow 6 to 8 inches wide; PGC says about 12 by 16 inches; Maryland DNR 12-16 inches.
- **Hibernation.** Skedaddle: squirrels "spend the winter hibernating". PGC: tree squirrels never actually hibernate; UC IPM: they do not hibernate and are active year-round.
- **Height.** Woodland Trust "at least six metres", Birds & Blooms "20 to 25 or 35 feet": MDC says leaf nests average about 40 feet up but can be as low as 10 feet; PGC puts gray squirrel dens 40 to 60 feet up.
- **Young.** Birds & Blooms: young "fledge" (a bird word) at about 12 weeks. MDC: young first leave the nest at 6 to 7 weeks and are weaned about a week later; ICWDM: they explore outside the nest around weaning at 10 to 12 weeks.
- **When to leave nests alone.** Birds & Blooms' "March and October" is garbled: ICWDM says young may be present February through August; Humane World says babies are likely February through May and August through October.
- **Removal.** Skedaddle sells removal; none mentions that agencies say to wait for the young, or that state rules on trapping and moving squirrels differ (NY DEC: illegal to move an animal off your property; Nebraska: translocation illegal).
