## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- Penn State: "small (0.4–0.8 in, 1–2 mm)". 1 to 2 mm is about 0.04 to 0.08 inch (1/25 to 1/12 inch); UF says usually under
  1/16 inch, DCPH 1/25 to 1/13 inch. The AI answer above it repeats a mixed "1/32 to 1/8 inch".
- DCPH: "Booklice are not truce lice" (typo for true). UF ENY-225: none are parasites and few look like lice.
- Sources disagree on books: Penn State (2024) says booklice do not directly damage paper; Clemson (2004) and UF say they can damage
  damp books by eating mold and the starch in bindings. Our page gives both, attributed, and does not say "destroy".
- Life cycle figures differ by source (Penn State: under a month in summer, 60 eggs; DCPH: 2-3 months, up to 100 eggs; UF: 30-60
  days, 50-60 eggs); our page attributes each rather than picking one.
