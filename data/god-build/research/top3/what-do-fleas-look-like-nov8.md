## Why each wins
- PetsRadar: a vet answers in order: visible to the eye? fleas and eggs on pets, signs, where they come from, house,
  people, treatment. (Body did not load in WebFetch; headings from its jump menu.)
- WebMD: the whole topic on one page (2,300+ words): size (1/12 to 1/8 inch), "do fleas fly?", the four life stages
  with numbers, signs on dogs, cats and people, bites, allergy, treatment; 19 sources, 6 FAQs; a fresh vet review date.
- PetMD: the pet owner's version: sesame-seed size, "can you see fleas?", dry skin or fleas, a step-by-step "how to
  check your dog" with a flea comb, 4 FAQs (incl. "what can be mistaken for fleas on dogs?"). Stock photos only.
- Moxie: flea test, eggs (grain of salt) and larvae, where to look, white-sock test, look-alikes (bed bugs, ticks,
  head lice, carpet beetles) with similarities and differences.

## What they give that we did not
1. The young: eggs, larvae, pupae and the life cycle (WebMD, Moxie, PetMD, PetsRadar). We sent all of it to /blog/flea-eggs.
2. A how-to-check-your-pet routine with a flea comb (PetMD's H2, WebMD's comb-on-white-paper, PetsRadar).
3. Ticks and head lice as look-alikes (Moxie; PetMD's FAQ). We had only springtails and bed bugs.
4. A plain "how big is a flea" heading and an actual-size photo (we had only a placeholder for the life-size shot).
5. Answers to "can a pet have fleas you can't see?" (PetMD FAQ, PetsRadar).

## What we give that none of them do
- Real, credited magnified photos (CDC scale bar, museum specimen, fingertip), the comb anatomy, the cat vs dog vs
  rat flea table, flea dirt magnified, the jar checklist, CDC disease facts, no-pet sources of fleas, all sourced.
- None of the three shows a scale bar, a true side view or a comparison of flea species; none cites CDC directly.

## What the searcher needs first
The flea in one look: wingless, dark reddish brown, about 1/8 inch, flat side to side, jumps; and what that looks
like to the naked eye (a dark speck about half a grain of rice). Then: is the speck on my pet a flea?
