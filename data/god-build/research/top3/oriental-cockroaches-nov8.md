## The three, taken apart
- **Wikipedia "Oriental cockroach"** (~950 words): waterbug / black cockroach names; males 18-29 mm, females 20-27 mm; a
  German/Oriental/American table (size, temperature, nymph development, lifespan, flight); egg case 16-18 eggs; health;
  5 photos (colony, nymph, male, female). Sources mostly books and pest firms.
- **pestnet.com** (~250 words): one species; "up to an inch and a half" (Texas A&M: 1 to 1 1/4 inches); says males fly
  short distances (Penn State, UF: flightless); one photo that looks like an American cockroach; no sources.
- **A-Z Animals** (~850 words, 2022): question headings (what kind of roach is black, what they eat, poisonous, where they
  come from, why in the house, black bugs that are not roaches); says males glide and are larger than females (Texas A&M:
  females nearly 1 1/4 inches, males 1 inch); 4 stock photos; one source.
- **bettertermite** (~2,300 words): size chart of 5 roaches incl. **the wood cockroach** (males fly to lights); crickets
  only in its FAQ; 13 FAQs; quote pitches.

## What they give that we did not (before Nov 8)
1. **"Black cockroach" in the first sentence.** Our quick answer opened on "An Oriental cockroach is ...".
2. **The wood cockroach**: nearly black, males fly to porch lights, females short-winged with the abdomen exposed (Penn
   State 2026). The likeliest "black flying cockroach" outside the humid South, and a near twin of an Oriental female.
3. **Field crickets** as the other big black indoor bug in fall (bettertermite FAQ; UMD, MDC).
4. **Odor** as a sign (A-Z; Penn State says roaches leave a characteristic odor around an infestation).
5. Whether black roaches bite: our live /blog/do-cockroaches-bite page was not linked.

## What we give that they do not
Credited photos where people meet it (garage floor, sewer manhole at night, female with egg case, CDC male/female), a
9-row quick-ID table with sourced sizes, Turkestan / Florida woods / smokybrown look-alikes by region, health at the
sources' strength, moisture-first control from 6 extension services, 10 FAQs. Correct sizes and flight (they get both wrong).

## What the searcher needs first
Its name (usually an Oriental cockroach), the one test (short wings, cannot fly, damp and low in the house), whether
it is dangerous, and that moisture and drains come before sprays. "Black flying" searchers need the roach that does fly.
