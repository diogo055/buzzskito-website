## Accuracy fixes found while checking (each against an opened source)

### The Register misattribution
- The page credited The Register (Aug 17) with "delivery slipped from early summer to August". That article does not say it; searched for summer, June, July, August, delay and slipped.
- **Source of the slip:** SCMP, Jun 27, 2026: "Photon Matrix initially said it expected deliveries ... to begin early this summer, but the mass production timeline has been pushed back"; mass production was "delayed until August".
- **Fix:** corrected in all five places it appeared, three of which credited The Register.

### Production and shipping
- **The page said:** "Mass production began August 16" (as fact) and "Aug 2026: First shipments begin" (no source).
- **The sources say:**
  - The maker's Sep 20 FAQ: mass production is planned to begin in October 2026.
  - The FAQ also mentions a "current pre-production preparation phase".
  - We found no confirmed delivery by Oct 23.
- **Fix:** the CGTN report is kept as reported, the maker's later date is added, and the shipments row is removed.

### Laser class
- **The page said** "the manufacturer publishes no class" in about 15 places.
- **The source says:** the maker's FAQ now gives Class 4.
- **Fix:** reworded to "uncertified Class 4" or "no delivered unit's label". The historical "when this was written" passages are kept as history.

### The anti-counterfeit notice
- **The page said:**
  - It was dated Aug 9.
  - The store was the "only official channel".
- **The page itself shows:**
  - It is dated July 23, 2026.
  - It lists two official channels (store and Indiegogo).

### Refunds

- **The store's own policy adds:** returns within 30 days after delivery.

### Company

- **The store's terms and shipping policy say:** the store is operated by Changzhou Guangzhiju Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd, Xinbei District, Changzhou. Both are now shown.
