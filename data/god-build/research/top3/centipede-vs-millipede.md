## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- Carnegie: centipedes "prefer dry environments." MDC: they are almost always confined to damp, humid places and dry out
  easily (no waxy layer, breathing holes that cannot close); UC IPM: damp, dark places.
- Carnegie: the longest species of each is about 6 inches. MDC: the Amazonian giant centipede can exceed 12 inches.
- Carnegie: "up to 750 legs"; EBSCO: none over 500. Virginia Tech (2022): a female *Eumillipes persephone* has 1,306;
  the 750-leg *Illacme plenipes* was the previous record.
- Orkin shows no real animal at all (illustrations only) on an identification question.
- Sources to watch (not top 3, not used): UC IPM says even the largest millipedes have fewer than 100 legs (Kentucky:
  most species 30 to 300); Health Canada says house centipedes came from Mexico (Penn State, MDC: the Mediterranean).
