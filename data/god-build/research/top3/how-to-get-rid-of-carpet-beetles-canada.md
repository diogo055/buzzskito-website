## Claims corrected (primary sources)
- "Safer's Diatomaceous Earth is the one most widely stocked in Canada, with Knock Down and Doktor Doom Be Green as
  registered alternatives ... no resistance can develop and the powder does not expire" -> Safer's Ant & Crawling
  Insect Killer 32278 has carpet beetle directions; Doktor Doom 32561 lists carpet beetles; Knock Down 32665 lists
  only "beetles"; Safer's label: "Remains effective ... for as long as [it] is present."
- Pheromone-trap FAQ "check the packaging for a PCP registration number. If there is none, treat what you bought as
  a plain glue board" -> PCPA s. 2 ("attracting"), Regulations Schedule 1 item 2; no carpet beetle lure, trap or
  pheromone in the registry; Health Canada: "It's a violation of the Pest Control Products Act to buy and use
  unregistered pesticides."
- Verified and kept: mothballs are registered pesticides limited to sealed containers (Solvable 2528: "Place
  garments, woolens, furs, etc. in airtight trunk or chest ... Seal all joints and holes of the containers with
  adhesive tape").
