## What they get wrong (the page states the sourced version)
- Kärcher: avoid steaming mattresses. University of Minnesota Extension: steam is "particularly useful for carpeting,
  upholstery, mattress and box spring".

- Conair's page in the AI Overview pushes a garment steamer. UMN: a clothing steamer does not reach high enough temperatures.
