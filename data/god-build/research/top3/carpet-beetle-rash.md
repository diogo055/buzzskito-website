## What they get wrong, and what our page says instead
- Healthline: carpet beetles drawn to exhaled carbon dioxide. None of the extension or CDC sources opened says so;
  our page does not repeat it.
- Healthline and bettertermite: "most people are allergic to bed bugs" / "bed bugs bite everyone". CDC says bed bug
  bites affect each person differently and lists "no physical signs" among the possibilities; our page says the
  one-person pattern is a clue, not proof.
- Healthline: mothballs with no warning. Our page gives no product advice; control is left to the control page.
- bettertermite: "removing larvae stops the reactions" as a promise. Our page reports what the CDC case series did
  and saw (healed in 3 days with treatment plus cleaning), without promising an outcome.
- Pest blogs in the results say carpet beetles feed on the oils in your hair in bed. The CDC report says larvae never
  infest living animals; our FAQ says so.
