## The three, taken apart
- **Ohio State BYGL, "Concrete Mites" Are Out: Look Before You Sit!** (J. Boggs, May 29, 2020; bygl.osu.edu/node/1588): ~350 words;
  6 of its own photos (concrete mites on concrete and a light post, red velvet mites); covers only concrete mites (Balaustium) and red
  velvet mites; no size, table, FAQ or other red bugs; suggests a named pyrethroid product for spring events.
- **Alpha Home Pest Control, "Tiny Red Bugs aka Clover Mites - Are They Harmful?"** (Idaho; undated): ~370 words; no photo of any bug;
  clover mites only; "no larger than a pinhead" is its only size; no source, table or FAQ; ends in a free-inspection pitch.
- **Waynes, "Tiny Red Bugs Around Your Home"** (R. Wood; callwaynes.com/blog/what-is-this-little-red-bug/): ~2,000 words; no photo of
  any bug; a 4-suspect box (chiggers, bed bugs, spider mites, clover mites) and a 4-row table in millimeters, unsourced; 5 FAQs; one
  named source (UConn); Southeast seasons; a perimeter-treatment pitch.
- Also read: Bob Vila (~950 words, 2 stock photos, clover mites only, a hairspray tip, no sources); Leafy Place (~2,000 words,
  uncredited photos, 5 bugs, no sources, nothing on velvet or concrete mites).

## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- Waynes: chiggers "burrow into skin folds". Kentucky ENTFACT-637 and Cleveland Clinic: chiggers do not burrow.
- Waynes: bed bugs are "never bright red". UMN: young bed bugs are nearly colorless except after feeding; a fed bed bug turns purplish
  red; NYS IPM's photo shows a nymph's abdomen bright red after a meal.
- Waynes: fill the plant-free strip with pea gravel or rocks. UMN: bare soil 18-24 inches wide; landscape rock is not enough.
- Waynes, the AI answer and others make chiggers "the tiny red bug that bites". Kentucky and Cleveland Clinic: the biting larvae are
  yellow to orange and nearly invisible; the bright red adults do not bite.
- Bob Vila: hairspray as control; the AI answer: a named window cleaner. Neither is in a source opened (UMN: vacuum or a moist cloth).
