## What was wrong on our own page (corrected Oct 28)
- **Delta Dust "Commercial class in Canada: legal for licensed applicators"** (PMRA bullet) and "restricted to licensed
  commercial applicators" (FAQ): the only current registration, DeltaDust Powder 33495, is RESTRICTED class for black-tailed
  prairie dog burrows (fleas); 29862 is cancelled. Fixed, with Drione 15255 as the registered professional dust and a link
  to /blog/wasp-dust-canada. This matters: the page ranks 3.0-3.9 for "delta dust (canada)".
- **"Raid Max Wasp & Hornet Killer"** (FAQ and body): that registration (23299.04) is cancelled. The current Raid cans are
  Raid Wasp & Hornet Bug Killer 7 (29776, jet, up to 5.7 m) and Raid Max Wasp & Hornet Foam Bug Killer 2 (30746, foam, up to
  3 m). "All of which throw a 2-4 metre jet or foam" was wrong for Raid 7. Fixed.
- **Ground nest "DIY possible - with caution / treatable at night"** (decision table) and "treatable but riskier" (FAQ 1):
  13 domestic wasp products carry the exclusion "nests located underground or in voids". Now "label first", with Calgary's
  clear-bowl method or a professional.
- **"True Old World hornets are not established in Canada"** (FAQ 2): false. The Canadian Encyclopedia: "The European
  hornet is the only species of hornet established in Canada" (southern Ontario and Quebec woodlands). Fixed.
- **"Declared eradicated from British Columbia in December 2024"** (FAQ 2, body): the December 18, 2024 declaration was
  Washington State's (WSDA/USDA, for the U.S.); B.C.'s agriculture ministry said B.C. remains "Northern Giant Hornet-free",
  last found in the Fraser Valley in 2021 (Global News). Fixed.
- "Residential carbaryl uses ... not registered" reworded to the verified fact: no current domestic carbaryl product.
