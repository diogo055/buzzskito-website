## What they get wrong or leave unsupported (our page states it from the source)

- **UCLA's range:** "much of California" and "lower Oregon". CDC NIOSH gives limited distribution in New Mexico, Arizona and California. APHIS lists 13 states and Puerto Rico, with no Oregon.
- **Orkin, the sting:** the ant stings "until it is brushed off or until its victim dies" (no source). AAAAI: the ant pulls the stinger out, rotates and stings again, several stings in moments.
- **Orkin and Cleveland Clinic, the fluid:** they call it pus or pus-like. AAAAI: the cloudy white material is sterile and heals quickest if left alone.
- **Orkin, the timing:** bumps "fade in about an hour" and blisters come "within four hours", with no source. Cleveland Clinic: the bumps last several hours and blisters form after several hours.
- **Keep Kids Healthy, the range:** the 2016 state list is out of date. APHIS (Sept 2026): 367 million acres in 13 states and Puerto Rico.
- **Sources:** none of the three cites CDC. UCLA and Orkin cite nothing.
- **Cleveland Clinic disagrees with itself:** 7 to 10 days on its fire ant page, 3 to 7 days on its ant bite page. We give the fire ant page's figure.
