## Corrected on the page (false or unsupported against the primary source)
- "Low-power electric fencing for gardens and livestock is legal and widely used across Canada" (FAQ 7) and "a fully legal, permanent-deterrent option for Canadian gardeners (subject to local bylaws)" (table caption) -> Toronto Municipal Code § 447-1.2 A(4) (consolidation June 9, 2021): no fence used as a conductor of electricity unless on agricultural land raising livestock, not more than 12 volts, only to contain livestock, signs at not more than 12-metre intervals; Mississauga Fence By-law 0397-1978 s. 12 (amended to 0020-2026): "no person shall erect ... an electrical fence on any land", DC exception on agricultural land, 12-volt trickle charge, solely to contain animals, signs about every 15.25 m.
- "an electric fence is a device, not a pesticide, so it needs no Health Canada PCP registration" (FAQ 7, caption) -> stated only what the registry shows (no Zareba or Gallagher record). Not asserted either way, because PCP Regulations Schedule 1 item 3 covers devices that "repel pests by causing them physical discomfort by means of sound, touch or electromagnetic radiation", and the registry holds a shock-mat "ANIMAL REPELLENT" device (SCAT MAT POWER PAK 20897, 1989-2001). Owner question for Health Canada.
- "a safe, pulsed shock ... it startles, it does not injure" (FAQ 7) and "not a dangerous or injuring shock ... used safely ... will not harm a raccoon, a dog, or a curious child" (FAQ 10) -> pulses, a sharp jolt, treat it as a hazard; ICWDM "Use electric fences with care and install appropriate caution signs"; Zareba's kit ships with three warning signs.
- "Fence energizers sold through Canadian retailers and Amazon.ca carry CSA or cUL certification" and "can void home-insurance considerations" (FAQ 8) -> ESA (Ontario): approval by an accredited certification or field evaluation agency before a product is used, sold, displayed or advertised; check the listing and ask the seller; certifiers include CSA, UL, Intertek.
- "trains them off in a night or two" (Quick Answer) -> removed (unsourced); heights given as 10-15 cm and 30 cm (Iowa State: 4-6 in and 12 in).
