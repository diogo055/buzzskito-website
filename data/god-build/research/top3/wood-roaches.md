## What they get wrong (our page states it correctly)
- bettertermite: American cockroach "up to 1.5 inches". Penn State says adults reach up to 2 inches; Minnesota gives
  1 1/2 to 2 inches.
- bettertermite: wood roaches indoors "die within 48-72 hours". No source we opened gives hours: Penn State says within
  a few days, Virginia Tech within days.
- ClearDefense and bettertermite give one line on chemical treatment. The extension sources disagree, and our page
  says so: Penn State, pesticides not necessary or recommended; Virginia Tech, perimeter treatments generally
  unnecessary; Iowa State, an outdoor barrier is a last resort and spraying firewood does no good; Minnesota, treat
  around doors and windows only if larger numbers are entering.
- None of the three says that a female wood roach or a nymph is the one mistaken for an Oriental cockroach, or gives
  the check (Minnesota: the Oriental lacks the light bands near the head).
