## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- AI answer: "native to the American Southwest and Mexico". Montana: from Washington, Idaho and Montana south to the Mexican border,
  west of the Central Plains; Bohart: west of the Rocky Mountains; Orange County: California to Oklahoma and south into Mexico.
- Wikipedia: adults "2 to 3 inches". UC IPM: up to about 2 inches; Orange County: most about 2 inches, two species near 3; Bohart:
  1 to 2 1/2 inches; Montana: body 35 to 50 mm (about 1 3/8 to 2 inches).
- UC IPM calls them "harmless to people" without a word on the bite; Montana and the Orange County district say a handled one bites
  hard. Orkin says they are "not prone to bite". Our page gives the bite and the first aid.
- Sources disagree on garden damage: Montana says they can be garden and crop pests on tubers such as carrots and potatoes; Bohart
  says they are not agricultural pests. Both are stated, attributed.
