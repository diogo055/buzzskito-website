## What they get wrong or leave unclear, and what our page says instead
- Wikipedia gives two species totals; our page uses Utah State Extension's "more than 3,300" and names its source.
- The Canadian Encyclopedia's "poison glands" with no word on people reads as a warning; our page gives the
  extension view: venom for prey, too small to hurt people.
- None of the three says what to do; our page says no treatment is needed (Kentucky, Iowa State, Wisconsin) and
  pesticides are not recommended (Penn State).
