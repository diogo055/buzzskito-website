## What they get wrong (our page states it correctly)
- **Alta:** "a little bleach down the drain should handle any eggs or larvae", and baking soda, vinegar and boiling water. **Clark:** drain cleaner or baking soda and vinegar. UC IPM (moth or drain flies) calls boiling water and bleach a common misconception that gives at best short-term control. Texas A&M (ENTO-050) says bleach or drain cleaner is largely ineffective against the film.
- **Better Termite:** "German cockroaches rarely live in wet bathrooms". UC IPM (cockroaches) says German cockroaches prefer food preparation areas, kitchens and bathrooms.
- **Better Termite humidity:** it says both "below 50% year-round" and "below 60% all year". EPA's mold and moisture guide says below 60 percent, ideally 30 to 50.
- **Clark:** "cockroaches and some rodents can come up through toilets" has no source. UC IPM describes American cockroaches foraging from sewers when pipes are damaged or drain traps are faulty, and Oriental cockroaches climbing up through damaged drain pipes.
- **Alta:** essential-oil "natural deterrent" with no evidence. Our page makes no repellent claim.
