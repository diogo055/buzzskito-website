# Mosquito Bits vs Mosquito Dunks: which page owns which search (Oct 23, 2026)

Two pages rank for the same BTI searches:
- `/blog/mosquito-bits-canada-vs-dunks` ("the Bits page")
- `/blog/mosquito-dunks-canada-guide` ("the Dunks page")

This map was written before either page was edited. The numbers come from two Search Console pulls:
- `queries-b.md`: Jul 23 to Oct 20, all countries.
- A 90-day pull for each page on Oct 23.

Format: impressions @ average position.

## The finding that changes both pages
Health Canada's pesticide registry was searched on Oct 23, 2026 through the registry's own API (`pest-control.canada.ca/pesticide-registry-api`).
- **Mosquito Bits:** no product of that name is registered in Canada, now or in the past. Summit's Bits carry a U.S. EPA number only (EPA Reg. No. 6218-86, U.S. specimen label).
- **Domestic BTI products that are registered:**
  - MOSQUITO DUNKS, Reg. No. 28888 (Summit).
  - AQUABAC 200G BIOLOGICAL LARVICIDE 10/14, Reg. No. 27374 (Clarke). These are granules.
  - MY-CROBE 200G BIOLOGICAL LARVICIDE (10-14), Reg. No. 34631 (Clarke). These are granules.
  - The other BTI registrations are restricted, commercial or technical class, or cancelled.
- **Not BTI, also domestic:** DOKTOR DOOM 90 DAY MOSQUITO LARVA KILLER, Reg. No. 34197 (novaluron).

So the Bits page must stop saying Bits are sold at Canadian chains. It also needs to say which granules are registered.

## How the two stay distinct
- **Titles:**
  - Bits page: "Mosquito Bits Canada: Not Registered + Legal BTI".
  - Dunks page: "Mosquito Dunks Canada: Registered + Where to Buy".
  - Neither title carries the sibling's head term.
- **Tables:**
  - Bits page: the table of every domestic mosquito larvicide in the registry (what to buy instead of Bits).
  - Dunks page: the Canadian label's dose-by-water-size table for Dunks only. It sends granule questions to the Bits page in one sentence.
- **Each page links to the other once** in the new text. Both already linked each other.
- **New FAQs do not repeat each other:**
  - Bits page: "Are mosquito bits banned in Canada?" and "What BTI granules are registered in Canada?"
  - Dunks page: "Are mosquito dunks available in Canada?" and "How much of a mosquito dunk goes in a rain barrel?"
