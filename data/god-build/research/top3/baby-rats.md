## What they get wrong or leave out (ours states it from the source)
- Critter Control: "sexually mature by six weeks". ICWDM: wild Norway rats at 3 months (Arkansas Extension agrees); 6 to 10 weeks is Merck's figure for pet males.
- Critter Control: fur and sight "by three weeks". ICWDM: roof rat pups have hair within a week and open their eyes at 9 to 14 days; the National Zoo: about 2 weeks for Norway rats.
- Critter Control: "6 to 12 pups every 28 days" (13 litters a year). ICWDM: 3 to 6 litters a year; UC IPM: 4 to 6.
- Coastline Pets: weaning at 4 to 5 weeks. Merck: weaning occurs about 21 days after birth.
- Rat Fan Club: release wild rats at 6 weeks. ICWDM: Norway rats are invasive in the US, so relocation is not recommended; CDC: avoid contact with rodents. Its antibiotic dose for pups has no veterinary source.
- None says that small young rats often will not set off a rat snap trap (UC IPM).
