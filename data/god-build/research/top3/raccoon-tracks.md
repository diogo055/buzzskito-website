## What they get wrong (and the source that says otherwise)
- Skedaddle: hind print "slightly larger" than the front. MU Extension G9453: hind print 3 1/4 to
  4 1/2 in, much longer than wide, front about 3 in and about as wide as long; MDC: front 2 1/2-3,
  hind 3 3/4-4. The hind print is clearly longer.
- Skedaddle: offers claw impressions as a way to tell raccoon from dog tracks. NY DEC Winter Tracks:
  dog-family tracks usually show claw prints too. The tell is five toes vs four.
- Skedaddle FAQ: snow shows claw and toe details "clearly". NY DEC: dry powdery snow records a
  less-defined print; Maryland DNR: melting snow makes tracks look larger than they are.
- A-Z Animals: claws "sometimes visible". MU Extension: five toes and claws visible on all feet; MDC:
  claws often leave marks; BioKIDS: you can usually see the claws.
- A-Z's front size (1-2.5 in) matches BioKIDS but not MDC (2 1/2-3) or MU (about 3): guides differ,
  so our page shows the spread instead of one number.
- Alderleaf and Skedaddle give one "across" size for the whole track; no front/hind length and width.
