## What they get wrong (our page states it correctly)
- Veseris: may not be able to bite people. Vetter & Isbister (Toxicon 2006, PubMed 16574180) report 8 verified bites
  plus 8 from the literature; MDC says the fangs can pierce human skin; CSU says it can bite if handled.
- Terminix: eight eyes; males just over 1/4 inch. Penn State: six eyes in an oval, males 9-10 mm (about 3/8 inch);
  UMN, MDC and USU: six eyes.
- Terminix: see a medical professional right away. MedlinePlus: most spider bites are harmless; seek treatment for small
  children and adults with severe symptoms; watch for infection over the next days.
- Veseris: residual insecticide. UMN: tolerate spiders, capture and remove a few; USU: jar or vacuum, moisture, prey, sealing.
