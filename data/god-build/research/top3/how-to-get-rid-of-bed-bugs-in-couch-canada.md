## Corrections made (against the primary source named)
- EcoRaider as a "PMRA-registered residual spray ... labelled for use around living spaces" (hero/quick answer, bullet, FAQ 1,
  FAQ 4, Step 4, description, schema description): registry has no ECORAIDER product. Replaced with the labels that allow
  upholstered furniture.
- "Residual keeps killing in cracks for weeks" for the couch spray: the couch-labelled cans are contact and spot sprays; the
  permethrin residual cans exclude furniture (Pro Max 34126).
- "Fit a narrow concentrator nozzle for the seams": UMN, "Do not use a pinpoint steam nozzle ... Pinpoint nozzles can blow
  the bed bugs away, and they may survive." Now the triangular or upholstery nozzle.
- "Amorphous silica or food-grade DE powder": now a DE product with a PCP number; CimeXa has no Canadian registration.
- "Only the spray is a pesticide": the dust is too; the devices' exemption cited (PCP Regulations s. 3(1)(a)).
- FAQ "Is it safe to sit on the couch during treatment?" renamed "Can I sit ...", answer tied to OnGuard's label.
