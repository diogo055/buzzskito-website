## What they get wrong (our page states it correctly)
- CVS calls nits "about the size of a pinhead". CDC gives the pinhead for nymphs, and CDC's DPDx measures a nit at 0.8
  by 0.3 mm (about 1/32 inch). (AAD also compares nits to a pinhead, so the page gives DPDx's measurement, no pinhead.)
- CVS: nymph to adult in "about 8 to 9 days". CDC: about 7 days after hatching.
- Cleveland Clinic: eggs hatch in "5 to 10 days" and survive off a host "about 10 days". CDC: hatch in 6 to 9 days; nits
  usually die within a week if not kept near scalp temperature.
- Lice Clinics of America: clothing and bedding "can harbor lice eggs and nymphs". CDC: an adult that falls off dies
  within 2 days; bedding is a less common route than head-to-head contact.
