- **Covers:** 8 oz "treats up to 2000", 30 oz "treats up to 7,500" (also "over 8000" on the same page), re-apply every 7–14 days, the fungus gnat "tea".
- **Safety wording:** "harmless to plants, pets, beneficial insects, honeybees, fish or wildlife".
- **Labels:** the U.S. specimen label (EPA Reg. No. 6218-86) is linked.
- **Canada:** no word on Canada.

- **Covers:** 8 oz / 30 oz / 20 lb sizes, "up to 14 days" in water with low to moderate organic content, fungus gnat directions, reviews.
- **Safety wording:** "harmless to other living creatures or plant life".
- **Canada:** no mention of Canada or shipping limits.

## What none of them has, and our page now does

### 1. The primary-source answer
Health Canada's pesticide registry was searched through its API on Oct 23, 2026:
- Product name contains "MOSQUITO" (295 products, current and historical).
- Active ingredient contains "H-14" (all 27 BTI registrations).

**Result:** no product named Mosquito Bits, ever. Summit's Canadian registrations are MOSQUITO DUNKS 28888 (domestic) and SUMMIT B.T.I. BRIQUETS 28995 (restricted).

The page now quotes:
- **Pest Control Products Act, s. 6(1):** "No person shall manufacture, possess, handle, store, transport, import, distribute or use a pest control product that is not registered under this Act".
- **Health Canada's import factsheet (Sep 2025):** "Online purchases of unregistered pesticides and their shipment to Canada are not permitted".
