## What they get wrong or leave vague
- **Terminix:** "sprays available to the public only repel mosquitoes and will not kill them". The registered Muskol can is labelled to "Kill Flies, Mosquitoes, Black Flies, Hornets and Wasps".
- **The Amazon.ca grid** sells U.S. fogging liquids. Health Canada's import factsheet says "Online purchases of unregistered pesticides and their shipment to Canada are not permitted".

## Checked and unchanged
- **RVD2023-06 quotes** on the page are verbatim (opened Oct 23):
  - "Domestic handler applications using handheld and stationary foggers".
  - The prohibition on "space spray uses on domestic-class liquid product labels".
- **Deltamethrin** (Health Canada fact sheet): authorized in 2017 for adult mosquitoes in residential and recreational areas, applied "by fogging using ultra-low volume sprayers".
- **Permethrin RVD2019-11:** total release fumigating foggers and attic mistblower use cancelled; indoor residential use limited to perimeter, spot and crack-and-crevice.
- **Black Flag:** no product in the registry. EPA's own record lists Black Flag Fogging Insecticide Formula 2 as EPA Reg. No. 53853-2. The page's "1021-1866-53853" appears on the U.S. safety data sheet; left as written.
