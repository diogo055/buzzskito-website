## Corrected (false against the primary source)
- TERRO "Canadian-label product", "sold with a Canadian label", "non-insecticidal lure rather than a pesticide",
  "can sit legally on a kitchen counter — no PMRA registration issues" (Quick Answer bullet, FAQs 1 and 4, Step 2,
  key facts) -> TERRO sells a Canadian version, T2502CANB, through Woodstream Canada (woodstreambrands.ca), but
  Health Canada's registry has no TERRO fruit fly product (TERRO's Canadian registrations are ant and insect products
  of Woodstream Canada Corporation); a trap sold to attract and kill insects is a pest control product (PCPA s. 2;
  Regulations Schedule 1 item 2 keeps devices "to attract or destroy flying insects" out of the device exemption).
  The registered domestic fruit fly lure traps are RESCUE! FRUIT FLY TRAP[S] 34819 (acetic acid 6.0%, Sterling) and
  OnGuard Fruit Fly Trap 35381 (acetic acid 5.12%, UR-CAN); sites include compost bins, garbage disposals, kitchen
  trash, pantries and sinks.
- Green Gobbler Fruit Fly Goodbye as "an enzyme or microbial drain gel ... the widely available one in Canada" (FAQ
  6, Step 3, table row "Enzyme drain gel", key facts) -> the maker's page ("Fruit Fly Killer - Drain Fly Treatment")
  says "It kills fruit flies, drain flies and sewer flies" with "natural Citronella Oil and Cinnamon Oil"; no enzymes
  or bacteria; Health Canada's registry: 0 Green Gobbler rows.
- "Enzyme drain gels ... there is no Canadian regulatory problem" and "raises no registration question" (body, FAQ
  11) -> the PCPA test is the claim; a drain gel sold to kill flies needs a PCP number; no home-use spray in the
  registry names fruit flies (the only current domestic products naming them are the two lure traps and three light
  traps: STEM 35296 and 35801, Raid Essentials 35297).
- "Mosquito Bits are the granular product labelled for fungus gnat use ... larvae die off within days" and FAQ 9 "treat
  those with BTI granules like Mosquito Bits" -> Summit labels Mosquito Bits for fungus gnat larvae in the US;
  Health Canada's registry has no Mosquito Bits record, Mosquito Dunks 28888 list mosquitoes only, and the domestic
  products that name fungus gnats are three light traps.
- "One two-pack, opened in mid-August, covers the entire Canadian spike season. Place one by the fruit bowl and one
  by the sink" (self-contradictory) -> Woodstream Canada: "90 day lure supply - each trap lasts for up to 45 days".
- "The best ready-made option in Canada ... sold at most Canadian hardware stores" -> "Our ready-made pick ... sold
  at Canadian hardware stores" (RONA, Home Depot Canada and others list T2502CAN/T2502CANB).
