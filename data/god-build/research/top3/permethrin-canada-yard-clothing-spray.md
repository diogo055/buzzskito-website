## The three, taken apart
- **Public Health Agency of Canada, "Permethrin-treated clothing"** (published 2020-08-07, modified 2020-08-14; short). The
  primary source: "Permethrin products in the form of liquids or sprays for consumers to treat their own clothing are not
  approved for use in Canada"; treated clothing for people over 16, pregnancy, nursing, children, pets, the PCP number. No
  wash counts, no where-to-buy, no products, nothing on yards.
- **Canadian Rockies Trail Guide, "Ticks, Permethrin and Canadian hypocrisy"** (about 2017, Brian Patton). An argument, not a
  guide: says treated clothing could not be bought in Canada (outdated since the 2018 Mark's line), that people dilute 10%
  livestock permethrin (it gives no warning that this is off-label), no cat or fish warning.
- **Wikipedia, "Permethrin"**. Uses, mechanism, cat and fish toxicity; its only Canadian line is an over-the-counter status
  citing a lice-treatment listing. Nothing on the clothing-spray rule.
- Also opened: the Trail Guide's 2018 "Permethrin-treated clothing arrives in Canada" (Mark's Wind River line, the PMRA lining
  condition, no tick claims at launch) and Urban Nature Store's Garden+ Protector page (12.5 g/L permethrin concentrate; the
  retailer page shows no PCP number and no label text).
