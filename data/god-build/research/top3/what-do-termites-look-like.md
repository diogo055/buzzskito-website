## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- Sizes: Dodson says "less than half an inch" yet lists drywood at 3/8 to 1 inch and subterranean at 1/8 inch; Terminix and
  PestWorld give 1/8 inch or 3 mm to 1 inch unsourced. Texas A&M: workers 1/4-3/8 inch; Penn State: soldiers and adult workers
  about 1/4 inch, kings and queens 3/8-1/2; MSU: swarmers 3/8 (eastern), 7/16 (drywood), 1/2 inch (Formosan) with wings.
- Terminix lists Formosan apart from subterranean (UF/IFAS EENY121: the Formosan subterranean termite; MSU's table gives it mud
  tubes) and has brown soldiers "seen outside the nest" (EPA: termites rarely leave soil, mud tubes or wood).
- Smithereen's FAQ: compound eyes, mostly nocturnal. MSU: workers are sightless, only primary reproductives have pigment and
  eyes; eastern subterranean swarms come by day (MSU, UF/IFAS, NC State), Formosan at dusk.
- Smithereen's drywood range ("Florida and the southern border"): USDA Wood Handbook, central California around the southern
  edge to Virginia, plus Hawaii. Season: PestWorld "spring", Smithereen "spring and summer"; UC IPM: desert subterranean
  July-September, California drywood summer and fall; UF/IFAS and MSU: Formosan late April to June at dusk.
