## What they get wrong (our page states it correctly)
- Terminix: the bait "sand fleas" are larger beach hoppers. UF/IFAS Extension (Escambia County): the bait sand fleas
  are mole crabs, the pompano bait dug from the swash zone; FWC's profile of the Atlantic mole crab agrees on the name.
- Terminix: active at dawn or dusk. Walla Walla University: the California beach hopper hides in a burrow by day and
  feeds on decaying seaweed at night.
- itchpet: beach hoppers "might bite". UC Riverside's Ebeling text: the sand fleas under beached seaweed are often
  blamed for bites but are completely harmless (Cleveland Clinic's softer "might cause bumps" is reported as such).
- EBSCO: parthenogenesis. CDC DPDx: only mated females burrow. EBSCO: pain no worse than a bug bite. Ebeling: causes
  great pain; CDC: inflammation and ulceration can become severe, secondary infections incl. tetanus and gangrene.
