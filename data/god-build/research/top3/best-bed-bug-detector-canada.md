## Watch-outs
- **Rutgers FS1117 changed.** The 2010 PDF still circulating (an NAA copy) says CO₂ monitors were less effective than a week of
  interceptors and that a zero catch never confirms absence. The live 2019 text (extension.rutgers.edu/fs1117) says CO₂ traps are
  equally or more effective than unbaited pitfall traps, detect faster, and one night can find low numbers in occupied or vacant
  rooms. Only the 2019 text is used.
- **EPA: "Sticky traps are not for bed bugs."** The page's "Best Passive Monitor" pick is a glue strip (BuggyBeds). The pick is
  unchanged; the new table states EPA's view. Owner's call.
