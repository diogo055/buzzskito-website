## What they get wrong (our page states it correctly)
- Pestnet: a recluse female "can make more than 250 sacs in a lifetime". Kentucky ENTFACT-631: up to 5 sacs
  in a lifetime, about 40-50 eggs each.
- Pestnet: baby recluses "do not have" the violin. Kentucky: the violin is sometimes less obvious in younger
  spiders (fainter, not absent); check the six eyes instead.
- Survival Freedom: "Baby spiders are poisonous if they're the offspring of a poisonous mature spider".
  MDC: juvenile widows pose no risk because their fangs are too small to pierce skin; Kansas State: immature
  widows do not produce the same toxic venom as females.
- Survival Freedom: a baby spider bite is "a small, red bump ... with fang marks" (no source). UC IPM: skin
  lesions are often blamed on spiders with no proof; MDC: a bite is confirmed only if the spider is seen
  and identified.
- A-Z Animals calls baby wolf spiders harmless; MDC says wolf spiders can bite if provoked.
- Biology Insights: molting "often monthly" (no source). Kentucky: recluses molt 5 to 8 times over about a
  year.
