## What they get wrong or leave unsupported (our page states it from sources)
- **AI Overview:** silverfish "up to an inch long". NC State gives adults as about 1/2 to 3/4 inch.
- **AI Overview humidity:** "below 50%" for all bugs. EPA's guide says below 60 percent, ideally 30 to 50. Penn State gives below 50 percent specifically for booklice. Our page gives each at its own source's strength.
- **Mosquito Squad and the AI Overview:** essential oils (peppermint, citrus, lavender) as basement bug repellents, with no evidence offered. Our page makes no repellent claim and gives the extension fixes instead.
- **Terminix and PestWorld:** they list the pests but never say which one the reader has. No sizes beyond leg counts, so a house centipede and a millipede, or a ground beetle and an Oriental cockroach, cannot be told apart from their pages.
