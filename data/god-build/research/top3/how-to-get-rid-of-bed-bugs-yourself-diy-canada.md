- "use food-grade DE" (FAQ 4) and "food-grade diatomaceous earth as a cheaper alternative" (FAQ 3, Step 3): EPA says pool or
  food grade DE should not be used; in Canada a dust must carry a PCP number (Health Canada). Now: DE with a PCP number.
- "silica-gel dust is the modern first choice" / "a silica product" (Step 3, FAQs 1 and 3, Quick Answer "silica or DE"): no
  silica-gel dust is registered for home use in Canada (registry: silica aerogel products are commercial; CimeXa 0 rows).
