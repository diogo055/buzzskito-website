## What they get wrong (and who says otherwise)
- PestWorld: "can fit through an opening as small as 3/8 inch". UF IG082: adults fit cracks of only about 1/16 inch and PREFER
  about 3/8 inch; UC IPM: an adult can hide in a crack 1/16 inch wide. Our page gives both, correctly.
- PestWorld: "up to six generations per year", unsourced. Extension figures differ (Penn State's estimate assumes two a year;
  UF IG082 says "several"); we give the sourced figures and no generation count.
- Orkin: "4 to 6 egg cases" per female. Penn State says 4 to 8, Rutgers and NC State 5 to 8.
- Orkin: one illustration, no photo; no sources; no bait guidance (the extension sources rank bait first).
- Wikipedia: its lead and table give different adult sizes (1.1-1.6 cm vs 13-16 mm); "egg to adult 50-60 days under ideal
  conditions" vs NC State 70-100 days and UF/Rutgers about 100 days.
