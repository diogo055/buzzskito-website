## Claims corrected on the page (primary sources)
- "Only three widely stocked consumer DE brands carry PMRA registration": false. Registry API, Oct 28: 33 current
  DOMESTIC products contain DE (32 DE-only), e.g. Ortho Bed Bug B Gon Diatom 32674, Wilson Bed Bug Out 26679, King Eco-Way
  29561, Insect Stop 21038, DX 13 30973.
- FAQ "Is food-grade DE legal as a pesticide? No ... carries no PCP number": wrong as stated. Doktor Doom Be Green PREMIUM FOOD
  GRADE DE is registered (32561) and Knock Down 32665's label allows "Food Grade Diatomaceous Earth" as an optional statement.
  Rewritten: only with a PCP number; PCPA s. 6(1); Health Canada quote.
- "using an unregistered product as a pesticide is off-label": off-label is for registered products; now s. 6(1).
- "most widely stocked" (unsourced) became "stocked by Canadian chains such as Canadian Tire and Home Hardware" (their listings).
