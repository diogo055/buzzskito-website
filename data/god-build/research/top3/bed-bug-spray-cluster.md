## Registry facts that change what the cluster can say (PMRA database, Oct 23, 2026)
- No registration found for EcoRaider, Proof, any geraniol bed bug spray, any Harris product, or CimeXa (its maker
  holds two Canadian registrations, Aprehend and Boractin). No current Ortho Home Defense bed bug product; Ortho's
  Canadian bed bug cans are Bed Bug B Gon (PCP 33722, 32674).
- "Sleep Tight" is a family: the pyrethrin can (PCP 29339) is labelled for mattress tufts and seams; the permethrin
  cans (PCP 28973, 30096) say not to apply to the mattress, headboard, footboard or furniture. Doktor Doom Pro Max
  (PCP 34126) carries the same prohibition.
- KONK Bed Bug Killer (PCP 31110) is a pyrethrin contact aerosol labelled for mattress seams and tufts, not a
  residual frame spray.
- Owner items these facts raise are listed in each page's own research note.
