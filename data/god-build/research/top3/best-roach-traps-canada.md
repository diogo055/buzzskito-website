## What they all lack (and what this page now adds)
- **Which roach baits are actually registered in Canada, from the labels.** New table "Roach baits and traps
  registered in Canada: what each label says", read on Health Canada's Pesticide Registry on Oct 23, 2026:
  - Raid Ant Roach & Earwig Gel Baits, Reg. No. 25735, domestic (label 2017-1008): "GUARANTEE: BORIC ACID 2.0%";
    "CONTAINS 12 CHILD RESISTANT BAITS"; "Place at least 10 baits in the kitchen and 2 in each bathroom"; "Replace
    ALL baits every 4 weeks if ant, cockroach and earwig problems persists"; "Apply/use only in areas inaccessible
    to children and pets."
  - Homeowners DIY Roach Bait (Nisus), Reg. No. 30293, domestic paste (2016-5463): "BORIC ACID ... 33.3%";
    "APPLICATIONS OF THIS PRODUCT OTHER THAN AS A CRACK AND CREVICE TREATMENT ARE NOT PERMITTED."; "Contains the
    allergens, milk and wheat."
  - Ortho Ant B Gon Max Ant & Roach Killer Gel, Reg. No. 32560, domestic (2017-0029): "Disodium Octaborate
    Tetrahydrate 6.0%"; "Cockroaches will feed directly on the bait and die, but will not share the bait."; "Do
    not use sprays, foggers or any chemicals or cleaners near the bait placements."
  - Victor Roach Trap, Reg. No. 28723, commercial glue trap with a lure (2025-5872): "0.03% German Cockroach
    Extract"; "Keep traps in one location for three to four days. If they remain empty, move them to other
    areas."; "replace after 35 days"; "Use of sprays or insecticides on or near traps may reduce the effectiveness
    of the product."
  - Combat Roach Killing System, Reg. No. 26519 (hydramethylnon): cancelled, last sale by retail May 31, 2006. The
    registry lists no current product containing hydramethylnon.
  - Raid Max Roach Bait 26645 and Raid Double Control Roach Baits 27731: cancelled, last sale by registrant
    Sep 1, 2020, last sale by retail Sep 25, 2025.
