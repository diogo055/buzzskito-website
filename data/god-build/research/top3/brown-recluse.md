## What they get wrong (and the source that says otherwise)
- Wikipedia: body "usually between 6 and 20 mm" (bettertermite.com repeats it). Vetter (UC Riverside; UC IPM): body
  about 3/8 inch, and a spider with a body over 1/2 inch is not a recluse.
- poisonhelp.org: the violin's neck points "toward the spider's head", and it leads with the violin. UC Riverside:
  the neck points backward toward the abdomen; UC IPM: the violin is not a reliable way to identify one.
- Univ. of Arizona News: brown recluses "inhabit every continent except Antarctica" (that is the genus). UC IPM and
  Vetter & Hedges (2018): the brown recluse is native to the south-central US; never found north of the 48 states.
- CDC PHIL 24369 caption: "found throughout North America". Penn State: established in 16 states only.
