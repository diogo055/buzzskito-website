- "University of Florida and Notre Dame ... 80–95% non-biting" and "~5% / only about 5%" (FAQ 2, FAQ 3, bullet, body, types table, GTA table, key facts) -> AMCA FAQ: Notre Dame, mosquitoes 4.1% and 6.4% of the daily catch, no significant difference between yards; UDel: 31 of nearly 14,000 zapped insects were biting flies.
- 'Female mosquitoes hunt by CO₂ and scent, not light' -> UDel's "most species of mosquitoes don't respond to black lights" (some do: photo of Aedes vexans at a UV light).
- BG-Mosquitaire as a "CO₂/octenol lure" trap, with "Flowtron" and a "sugar-yeast bottle" (FAQ 2, FAQ 5, bullet, types table, runner-up body, GTA table, key facts, price note) -> Biogents: BG-Sweetscent "mimics scents found on human skin", "up to two months", "+/- CO2"; no Biogents product in the registry; Flowtron Mosquito PowerTrap 28457 cancelled 2022.
- "Health Canada status: Traps are devices — no PCP pesticide registration" (key facts) -> PCP Regulations s. 3(1)(a) + Schedule 1 item 2 (devices to attract or destroy flying insects are not exempt); s. 6(1) of the Act; Schedule 2 item 6 (CSA-certified outdoor zappers labelled "This device does not control blackflies, mosquitoes or other biting flies.").
- "Mosquito Magnet line (Patriot Plus, Independence, Executive)" -> registry: Patriot Plus 32375 and Executive 29944 current; Independence 29943, Patriot, Pro, Pro Plus, Liberty, Defender cancelled (2024-11-12).
- "Octenol or Lurex attractant cartridges" -> octenol attractant 28440 current; no Lurex record.
- "measurably reduces the mosquitoes in your yard"; "a genuine investment that pays off" -> aimed at biting females; label "Ideal for large yards".
- Thermacell "70–95% in 4.5m zone" (GTA table) and "instant personal repellent zone" (smart stack) -> Patio Shield label 31998: "up to 4.5 metres from the product ... for up to four hours", "Effect is not immediate ... allow 30 minutes".
- "BTI dunks or bits — kills larvae before they hatch" -> Mosquito Dunks label 28888: "Kills Mosquitoes Before They're Old Enough to Bite" for up to 30 days, only where larvae are present in water that cannot be emptied weekly; no Mosquito Bits record.
