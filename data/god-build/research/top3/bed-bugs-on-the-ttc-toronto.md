## What was wrong on our own page (corrected Oct 28)
- "Permethrin clothing treatments/sprays sold in Canada are registered for ticks and mosquitoes" (FAQ 4, quick reference
  row, "What Not to Buy"): the Public Health Agency of Canada says permethrin liquids and sprays "for consumers to treat
  their own clothing" are "not approved for use in Canada"; only factory-treated clothing is registered (Health Canada
  register: 32757 NO FLY ZONE, apparel, mosquitoes and ticks). Fixed in all three places. **Owner note:** the sibling
  /blog/permethrin-canada-yard-clothing-spray should be checked against the same PHAC page.
