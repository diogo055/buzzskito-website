## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- Critter Control lists mothballs among raccoon smell repellents. Ontario: do not pour naphtha flakes or mothballs down
  a chimney; our page's mothball section stays.
- Hipster Homesteaders mixes vinegar and ammonia with no safety note, and sells the US products by link.
- Both describe US relocation law only. Ontario: within 1 km, within 24 hours, private land only with the owner's
  permission.

## Our own statements, checked against the sources (the user's rule for trapping and relocation lines)
- Quick Answer "Trapping is not an option" -> "Trapping rarely helps": trapping is lawful in Ontario under s. 31 of the
  Act; it is the 1 km / 24-hour release that makes it pointless.
- FAQ and section: "generally within 24 hours" -> "within 24 hours" (O. Reg. 665/98 s. 133: "not later than 24 hours
  after capture"); private-property permission added.
- "which is exactly why the rule exists" (rabies) -> Ontario's own fact: its latest raccoon-variant outbreak "started in
  2015 when an animal was inadvertently moved to Ontario from over 500 kilometres away".
