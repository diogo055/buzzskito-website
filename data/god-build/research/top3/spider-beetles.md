## What they get wrong (our page states it correctly)
- Extermital "They cannot fly" and DCPH-A "The adult beetles do not fly": Colorado State Extension says males (not
  females) of the whitemarked spider beetle fly and in other species both sexes may; UF says some species can fly.
- Extermital "They don't need water": WSU Pestsense says they "require easily accessible water for survival".
- Wikipedia and Extermital never say they are not ticks or that they do not bite; DCPH-A: no medical threat; UMN:
  pantry pests do not bite or sting people or pets; URI TickEncounter: no tickborne disease risk from look-alikes.
- Penn State (no longer live) gave the smooth spider beetle as Gibbium aequinoctiale; CSU's Colorado list still uses
  Gibbium psylloides (hump beetle). Our page names both as the sources do, no taxonomy of its own.
