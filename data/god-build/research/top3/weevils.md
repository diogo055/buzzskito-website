- **Wikipedia "Weevil"** (#1): ~1,400 words of description, ecology, taxonomy; ~11 images, mostly pinned specimens, no scale,
  none indoors. Rice and maize weevils in passing; nothing on removal, bites, root weevils indoors or acorn grubs. No table/FAQ.
- **Scotts Miracle-Gro** (#2): ~700 words from a pesticide maker; one unlabeled stock photo and three packshots; "1/8 to 1/4

## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- AI overview "most grow between 1/4 and 1/2 inch": pantry weevils are about 3/32 to 3/16 inch (Oklahoma State; UMN <3/16).
- AI overview, Orkin, Scotts: weevils infest flour. UMN: rarely in caked or crusted milled products such as flour;
  Mississippi State: maize weevils do not usually develop in flour or finely ground grain.
- AI overview: vinegar wipe, bay leaves, oven "120-140°F for 15-60 min" (no source). UMN: no evidence for bay leaves or
  spearmint gum, washing does not prevent re-infestation; Iowa State: 140°F for 15 minutes.
- Scotts: insecticide in pantry cracks. UMN, Iowa State: not recommended in food cupboards; UC IPM: spraying on or near food
  may cause greater harm than the pests.
- Facebook "weevils eat the house frame": Iowa State says they will not feed on the house structure; UMN, no building damage.
