## Why each wins
- Medical News Today (~1,250 words): a doctor-reviewed symptom page by mite: chiggers, Demodex, oak mites, scabies
  (symptoms, treatment each), dust mites, prevention, when to see a doctor; 13 references. Its "Pictures" section is
  empty in the capture; not updated since 2020.
- IDPH: the government list of every mite that affects people (chiggers, itch mites, bird and rodent mites,
  Cheyletiella, scabies, clover and dust mites), each in a paragraph. Authority.
- Loyola (~800 words, no sources): the 2024 cicada-year oak mite outbreak: why bites follow cicadas, what they look
  like, treatment, prevention, when to see a doctor.
