## What they all lack (and what this page now adds)
- **What the Canadian label actually allows.** Nobody quotes a label. New table "Registered cockroach sprays in
  Canada: what each label allows" quotes four current domestic labels, read on Health Canada's Pesticide Registry
  (pest-control.canada.ca) on Oct 23, 2026 (the older Pesticide Label Search, pr-rp.hc-sc.gc.ca, answered 503 all
  day):
  - Raid Ant Roach & Earwig Bug Killer 19, Reg. No. 30355 (label 2023-1858): "Pyrethrins 0.2%, Piperonyl Butoxide
    0.48 %, Cyfluthrin 0.05%"; "For Indoor Use at Buildings"; "DO NOT APPLY AS A SPACE SPRAY, PERIMETER, OR
    BROADCAST TREATMENT. Apply as a spot treatment only."; "DO NOT apply any product containing cyfluthrin more
    than once every 10 days."
  - Raid Ant Roach & Earwig Insect Killer 2, Reg. No. 32434 (2023-1994): "Pyrethrins 0.25%, Piperonyl Butoxide
    1.01%"; indoor only; its "perimeter" is "less than 0.3m wide along the edges of a room".
  - Doktor Doom Maximum Strength Residual Barrier Cockroach Killer, Reg. No. 30847 (2019-7129): "Permethrin
    0.25%"; "to be used to kill cockroaches inside buildings"; "crack and crevice or spot treatment only".
  - Ortho Ant B Gon Max Ant & Roach Killer Spray, Reg. No. 32118 (2019-5216): "Permethrin .... 0.35%"; cockroaches
    are listed inside buildings only; the outdoor list is "Ants, Crickets, Fleas, Spiders, Sowbugs and Brown Dog
    Ticks".
  - Also read: Ortho Home Defense Max Perimeter & Indoor 27521 (outdoors: ants only), Knock Down Spider Killer
    29689 and Konk Spider & Tick II 32619 (cockroaches indoors only). **Of the seven domestic labels read that
    list cockroaches, none lists them for outdoor use.**
