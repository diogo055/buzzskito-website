## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- PestWorld: "Region: Northern regions of the United States." UF: a major household pest in parts of the northwest,
  midwest and southern United States.
- Fox: lifespan "6 months to 2 years" in its fact box but adults "about 6 months" in its text; nymphs in "6-12 months".
  Penn State: nymph stages 24-130 weeks; Texas A&M: egg to adult 575-600 days, adults 1-6 months; UC IPM: 1-2+ years.
- Fox: look-alike beetles lack "fast movement". Missouri Conservation: most ground beetles are fast runners; Texas A&M:
  Oriental cockroaches do not move very fast even when disturbed. The speed test runs the other way.
- Fox: "68-84 degrees" with no unit, and Salmonella and E. coli stated flatly. Penn State: forms of gastroenteritis
  "appear to be" the principal diseases transmitted; we keep that strength.
- Wikipedia: females "wider and shorter than males". Texas A&M: females nearly 1 1/4 inches, males 1 inch (PestWorld
  agrees); we give Texas A&M's. Wikipedia and Fox state one origin; UF calls it uncertain. We leave origin out.
