## Who ranks (WebSearch, US, Nov 8; Google differs)
Skipped: keyword-spam subdomain pages, Quora, forum threads (UBC, Arachnoboards, frog forum), old blogspot pages.
1. **A-Z Animals, "8 Common Insects That Look Like Lint or Dust"** (a-z-animals.com, updated Sep 5, 2026). Wins the dust
   query on framing and freshness: woolly aphids, dust mites, whiteflies, grain mites, mealybugs, no-see-ums, snow fleas,
   cottony cushion scale. ~1,100 words, 9 stock photos, one source, no table or FAQ, no sizes for most bugs.
2. **Terminix, "What are Those Tiny White Bugs Around Your Home?"** (Aug 2025; 403 to our fetch today, teardown from Oct 28).
   Brand strength; 8 bugs including termites, clothes moths, springtails; one size; ends in a termite pitch.
3. **Leafy Place, "Types of White Bugs"** (Sep 2022) and Hawx (Feb 2025). Leafy has a "look like lint or dust" section
   (flour and mold mites, woolly aphids) and room sections, and names **silverfish in the bathroom**; several sizes are
   wrong ("0.4 to 0.8 inch" for 1-2 mm whiteflies). Bob Vila was also top 3 on Google on Oct 28.

## What they give the searcher that we did not
- The dust answer near the top. Ours was right but sat in the 9th of 11 sections.
- Whiteflies as the "flying dust" over a houseplant, said in the dust context (we had them only in the plant section).
- Silverfish (young ones are white) for the bathroom. We never mentioned silverfish.

## What we give that they do not
- Location sorter, quick-ID table with sourced sizes and how each moves, real credited photos where people find them,
  bed bug eggs and nits, dust mites correctly described as invisible, which ones bite, look-alike cards, 10 FAQs.
- Their errors stay out (Hawx: dust mites "resemble fine dust"; Leafy's unit conversions).

## What the searcher needs first
1. "Look like dust": mites on damp food; whiteflies if the specks fly up from a plant; booklice and springtails are single
   specks; dust mites cannot be seen. Now the second section on the page.
2. Where they are (kitchen, bathroom, bed, plant). 3. Do they bite. 4. What to do.
