## What they get wrong (our page states the sourced version)
- Terminix: 16-24 eggs per capsule. Penn State: average 13 per capsule; UF: 14 to 18; Ebeling: at most 18, 13.2 hatching.
- Terminix: adults live 3-6 months; Fox: 5-6 months. Ebeling (Gould and Deay): 131 to 315 days, average 206; Penn State:
  adult females 13 to 45 weeks; UF: over 200 days.
- Terminix: eggs hatch in 50-75 days. Penn State: 37 to 103 days by temperature; Ebeling: 49 days at 82°F, 95.6 at 72.5°F.
- Terminix: linked to "33 types of bacteria" (no source). Penn State says only that bacteria and protozoa causing
  gastroenteritis and diarrhea can be carried on cockroaches' legs and bodies.
- Fox: up to 13 egg cases per female. Penn State and UF: about 14.
- Wikipedia: "tan to light brown". UC IPM: males golden tan, females darker brown; UF calls the species dark brown.
- PestWorld: entered the US from Cuba in 1903 (no source). Ebeling: first US record Key West and Miami, 1903.
- Not wrong but missing everywhere: the room is only a clue. Terminix and Fox put it in bedrooms and dry spots; Ebeling
  (1975) found both species most abundant in kitchens, and mixed populations.
