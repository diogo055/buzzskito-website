## What they get wrong (our page states it correctly)
- **PestWorld: "up to 4,000 workers".** UC IPM gives 1,500 to 15,000 by species, and MDC 5,000 workers by September.
- **Fox Pest: queens "often close to 1 inch".** Its own fact box (1/2-5/8 inch) also contradicts its text (1/2 to 1
  inch). MDC gives queens about 3/4 inch; Penn State gives eastern queens up to 18 mm.
- **Britannica: "half a ton" nests, unsourced.** We give UF/IFAS's documented 8.2-foot southern nest (74 combs,
  possibly 2-3 colonies) and UC IPM, Mississippi State and Penn State on perennial nests, with no weight.
- **PestWorld: an unsourced ER figure.** Not repeated here. (UF Gardening Solutions misspells *squamosa* once.)
