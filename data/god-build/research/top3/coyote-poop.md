## What they get wrong (ours states it from the source)
- "Male droppings 6 to 12 inches" (Bob Vila): Minnesota DNR gives 3 to 6 inches; NY DEC and Iowa DNR say under an inch thick; wolf scat (Iowa DNR) is the one that runs 4-7 in and over 1 in thick.
- Heartworm tied to coyote feces (Bob Vila, Critter Control): the American Heartworm Society says heartworm is passed by mosquito bites; coyotes are carriers, their droppings are not the route.
- "All coyote poop is pretty much the same size, three to four inches" (A-Z): Minnesota DNR 3-6 in; the DNA study found wide size overlap between coyote, bobcat and gray fox scats.
- Tapered ends as the dog test (A-Z, several others): NY DEC says dog scat often lacks the taper but softer dog scats can show it, and the Arizona Extension guide describes dog scat with tapered ends; hair and bone (Iowa DNR, Alabama Extension) are the better test.
- Nobody names the one parasite agencies warn about (E. multilocularis) or says how people get it (swallowing eggs, CDC); cleanup is "call a pro" instead of gloves, a tool and hand washing (Kentucky DFWR, WSU).
