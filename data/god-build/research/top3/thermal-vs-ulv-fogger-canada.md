## Corrected on the page (against the primary source)
- Thermal droplets "0.5 to 10 microns" (Quick Answer, comparison table, FAQs 1-2, body): the makers of the consumer thermal
  foggers (Burgess and Fountainhead electric manuals) state an average of 15 microns; a Canadian label (Bugwacker 22563)
  calls for ULV equipment with 15-25 micron droplets. Now attributed; "thermal wins on raw fineness" removed.
- FAQ 7 "municipalities ... favour thermal" and "ULV foggers cover less area": the US EPA says mosquito-control ULV sprays
  are applied by aircraft or truck-mounted sprayers and used by state and local agencies. Table row reworded to handheld.

  product whose label allows it".
