# THE FINAL GOD LIST: 100 pages, 10 a day from Oct 27, 2026

Owner, Oct 26: "the ultimate God plan on the top 100 pages that we should make right now for the absolute most traffic possible in the next 12 months", skipping searches that are too hard to win.

**Built from:** Google Trends (US volumes calibrated to brown recluse = 383K/mo, plus winter factors), Google Keyword Planner checks, saved Ahrefs difficulty, and a page-one screen of every search (who holds the top 5, winnability 0-10, what it takes to reach the top 3). Data: `god-list-2026-10-26.json` (also `bench` = next in line, `dropped` = removed with the reason).

**Mix:** 64 Species ID, 18 Identification (sorters, look-alikes, eggs/babies, X vs Y), 13 Bites & stings, 5 Droppings & signs. **US searches a month across the 100:** about 4.2 million. **Dropped:** 11 (too hard or too small). **Bench:** 6.

Winnability: 7-10 = small sites/forums on page one (best odds); 4-6 = mixed; 0-3 = institutions/big brands (page one possible, top 3 unlikely). Every page still gets beat-the-top-3 research at build time, the photo-ID brief, an independent fact-check and real photos.
