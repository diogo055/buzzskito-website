## How to read the JSON

- `candidates` is sorted by `confidence` (high, medium, low), then `rankKey`. `rank` is the position.
- `usVolume`, `kd`, `trafficPotential`, `volumeKeyword`, `volumeSource`: the recorded Ahrefs US figures for the main search and the file they came from. `clusterVolume` adds the recorded volumes of the page's own variant searches. `needsVolume: true` means no recorded volume for the main search.
- `demandEstimate.usMonthlySearches` and `basis`: the number used for sorting and where it came from (with `range` for Trends). `relatedVolumes`: recorded volumes for variants and same-words searches (the latter are not counted).
- `gsc`: our 90-day Search Console numbers for the topic's queries (all countries and US), with the top queries. `gscQueriesOver200`: the proof-of-demand queries.
- `season` and `seasonBasis`; `overlapsWith`: existing pages (from `lib/article-index.json`) the page would sit next to, with why; `variants`; `sources`; `status` (planned date, taxonomy or research only).
- Flags: `ownerCall`, `gate`, `usEdition` (a US twin of a Canadian page: add the hreflang pair), `offCore`, `notes` (including the Oct 16 verdicts on queued pages).
- `dropped`: planned or taxonomy pages left out, with the reason. `droppedResearchTopics`: what was left out of the research files and why.

## Method

1. **Planned but not written:** every page in `plan.src.json` (335) and `taxonomy.src.json` (226) without a `content/guides` or `content/guides-scheduled` file, minus the state series. 132 plan pages and 11 taxonomy pages qualified; 48 were dropped (42 pure summer tick or mosquito topics, 5 already live, 1 duplicate of another planned page). Kept on purpose from the tick and mosquito clusters: dengue and chikungunya (travel and outbreak driven), winter tick (October-April) and deer keds (a fall pest, not a tick).
2. **Research files:** all recorded Ahrefs volumes were parsed into one table (737 keywords). Every topic named in the research files was checked against the 754 live pages, the 5 scheduled guides and the plan; 116 were new and were added by hand with their sources.
3. **Search Console:** the 90-day pull (single `query` dimension, `dataState` all; 97,860 rows) was used two ways: as proof of demand for every candidate (queries attributed as described in the JSON `method`), and to find queries with 200+ impressions whose topic has no dedicated page. Of the 847 queries with 200+ impressions, only 23 sit worse than position 15, and nearly all of those are local-service or product searches. The rest belong to pages that exist.
4. **Confidence, estimate, season:** as defined in the JSON `method`.

## Caveats

- Ahrefs volumes overstate what this site sees: about 0.26 Search Console impressions per Ahrefs search on page one (`path-to-50k.md`), 20-40x for whole topics (`next-50.json`). They rank topics; they are not forecasts. No Canadian Ahrefs volumes were used (skill rule 3).
- Search Console impressions for a topic are what we see at today's positions, mostly Canadian, often on a different page. They are a floor for demand, and they cannot show demand where we do not rank at all (snakes, lice, termites, scorpions show almost nothing).
- Competitor-traffic and Trends estimates are rough. Low-confidence pages are not "no demand": they are unmeasured.
- Overlaps were checked against titles, slugs and guide targets, not by reading every page. Before writing any page, run the usual collision check and the beat-the-top-3 step.
