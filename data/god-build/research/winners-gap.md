### How much to trust this

- **Estimates.** Ahrefs traffic is a model; on our own site it lands within x0.8-1.4 of Search Console. These are October numbers: summer pests run 1.3-2.2x higher in July.
- **Only their top pages.** The last page counted earns 231 (bettertermite), 118 (Fox), 190 (Bug Out) and 138 (Moxie) a month. Smaller winners are not in this list; the teardown's appendix lists more of bettertermite's.
- **"Covered" means we have a page aimed at the search**, checked against every live, scheduled and planned page and a text search of our pages. It does not mean our page ranks.
- **The score ranks; it does not forecast.** It multiplies their visits by how many of the four sites win the search, how well the page type does (bettertermite's hit rates), and the season. Topic and page type are mixed together in those hit rates (ops skill rule 10).
- **Single-page wins move fast.** bettertermite's biggest page on Oct 15 (Virginia house spiders, 8,514 a month, #1 for "house spider") is not in its top 80 on Oct 19.

- Fields: page address, traffic, top search and its US volume, plus the position for bettertermite and Fox. Every selected column costs at least 1 unit a row (sum_traffic and top_keyword_volume cost 10 each), so the first call's three extra columns cost 240 units; the last two calls dropped the position column to stay under the cap.
- Fox's location and brand pages were removed inside the query rather than after it, so all 80 rows are content pages for the same price. The same filter was used for Bug Out and Moxie. 15 rows were then skipped by hand: blog, pest, info and location indexes (7), service pages (3), a product-label page (1), off-topic or service-shaped posts (4).

**Our pages checked** (read from the repo on Oct 20): 148 live guides (`content/guides`), 358 hand-written blog pages and 5 hand-written Learning Centre pages (`app/blog`, `app/learn`), root pages such as `/pest-control-cost-canada`, 1 scheduled guide (`content/guides-scheduled`), the 311 pages in `plan.src.json` (with their release dates), the 202 pages and 26 upgrades in `taxonomy.src.json`, the 30 build-order pages, 77 candidates and the Oct 16 verdicts in `next-wave-research.json`, and the 50 pages of `next-50.json`.

**Coverage rules**
- **Covered:** a live page aims at the search (its keyword, extra target searches or title) or is about exactly that thing. For question searches, a section or FAQ that answers it counts. For identification searches (signs and life stages, look-alikes, X vs Y, species, bugs in a room) a section inside a page about something else does **not** count: the winners' formula is one search, one page (teardown: these types reach 100+ visits a month on 41-58% of bettertermite's pages, against 24-26% for questions and how-to pages and 2% for its templated species pages).
- **Planned:** a page in `plan.src.json` aims at it, or is about the same pest and the search is a question that page should answer (written as a spec note). Pages the Oct 16 verdicts dropped or deferred are marked "on hold".
- **Gap:** nothing live or planned aims at it. Near-duplicates were merged into one gap (bettertermite's two fly comparisons; Fox's two "nuke" pages; bettertermite's carpenter ant frass with Fox's ant poop and carpenter ant signs pages).

**Score** = their US visits a month × sites (1 site 1.0, 2 sites 1.5, 3 or more 2.0) × page type (signs and life stages, bugs in a room, look-alikes 1.0; X vs Y 0.7; species 0.6; question 0.45; how-to and product 0.4: bettertermite's hit rates divided by the best type's, with species set by judgment between bettertermite's 2% encyclopedia and Fox's strong species pages) × season (winter-strong 1.3, spring or fall 1.0, summer 0.8). Do-not-build pages are not scored. The order in the summary is the score order.
