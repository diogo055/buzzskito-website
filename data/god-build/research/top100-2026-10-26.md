# Top 100 pages to build: bites, identification, droppings (Oct 26, 2026)

Owner, Oct 26: "find the top 100 pages of this kind... make and push 10 a day over the next 10 days." Built from saved Ahrefs US volumes (pulled before the Oct 25 cancellation) and our own Search Console impressions. Machine-readable list with overlaps and variants: `top100-2026-10-26.json`. Scoring and ordering are explained in its `_about`.

**Mix:** 64 Species ("what is this ___"), 12 Bites & stings, 18 Identification (sorters, look-alikes, eggs/babies, X vs Y), 6 Droppings & signs. **US searches a month across the 100 head terms:** about 3,461K (11 pages have Search Console evidence instead of a saved volume).

**Release order:** biggest expected winter demand first (Google Trends Nov-Mar factor where saved). Summer-only pages come later and age through the winter for the May-August peak. Every page still gets beat-the-top-3 research, the photo-ID writer brief, an independent fact-check and real public-domain/CC0/CC BY photos. A page that cannot get a real photo of its subject waits and the next one on the list moves up.
