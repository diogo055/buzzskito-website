- Hamilton is in our service area, and the page is about recognizing and avoiding ticks; our page shows real photos of a blacklegged adult and nymph on a fingertip, a nymph on a fingernail, the life stages, an American dog tick and a lone star tick.
- A university link is about as strong as links get. Low odds, high value.
