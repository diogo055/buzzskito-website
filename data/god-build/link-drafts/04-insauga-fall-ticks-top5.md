> Ticks don't stop when summer ends. Public Health Ontario says blacklegged (deer) ticks can be active any day it's above 4°C. Adult blacklegged ticks are most active in September and October, and some are still out in November.

> Top 5 places ticks hide on a dog after a walk:
> 1. In and around the ears
> 2. Under the collar
> 3. In the armpits and groin
> 4. Between the toes
> 5. Under the tail
