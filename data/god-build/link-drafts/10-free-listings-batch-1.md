# 10. Free business listings, batch 1 (Bing, Apple, Yelp, YellowPages, 411, HomeStars, BBB, Hotfrog)

**Status:** draft, nothing created. Alex creates or claims each listing himself; Claude never creates accounts.
**Channel:** one-time listings (`../link-plan.md`, section 2). Two or three a week in November and December.

## Why do this

- Search found **no** BuzzSkito page on Yelp, YellowPages, HomeStars, BBB or 411 (Oct 21, 2026), and Ahrefs shows no listing sites linking to us. The April 2026 emails to directories left no trace.
- Most of these links are nofollow, so they will not push the US guides up. Their real value: Google, Bing, Apple Maps and AI assistants see the same business details everywhere, which helps the GTA service (the $1,222 customer), and some homeowners search these sites directly. They also make us look established to journalists who check.
- Expect 6-8 new linking sites from this batch.

## Before each one: two checks

1. **Search the site for "BuzzSkito" first.** If a page already exists, claim it instead of making a second one (duplicates hurt).
2. **Copy the details exactly as they appear on your Google Business Profile** (name, phone, website, hours, address or service area). If your address is hidden on Google, pick the "service-area business" or "hide my address" option where the site offers one.

## What Alex does

1. Do two or three a week, any day, in the 15-minute slot.
2. After each one, tell Claude "listed on [site]" and paste the listing's web address. Claude adds it to the link log and checks the link a few weeks later.
3. Keep a note of each login in your own password manager. Never share passwords with Claude or anyone else.

## Never

- Pay a "citation building" or "directory submission" service, or buy a "top 3" or "featured" spot for SEO reasons.
- Offer discounts, gifts or free service for reviews, or post reviews yourself. Every platform bans it.
- Create a second listing when one already exists.
