## Why it fits, and what it will not do

- It is Ontario's pest control trade association. Membership tells customers, Google and journalists that we are an established, licensed operator. Our old April outreach list already named it; it was never done.
- **Honest limit:** the "Visit Website" links in SPMAO's directory carry rel="nofollow", so the listing gives trust, not ranking power. Judge it on the business benefits (insurance discount, CFIB, contacts), not on the link.
- Open question for them: SPMAO is a "structural" association; we only treat outdoor mosquitoes and ticks. The email asks which membership type fits.
