## How to use it

- Each month, put the real numbers next to the goal: Google visitors (Search Console), Mediavine earnings, Amazon.ca and Amazon.com earnings. Ahead of the goal: keep going and consider the faster pace. Behind for two months running: find out why before building more of the same.
- Checkpoints that can move the goal: Oct 29-30 (photo upgrades read), Dec 15 (visits per new page: 20 = Realistic, 45 = Maximum, 100 = a 200K path), the US Amazon test (4 weeks after it starts), the first full month of Mediavine.
- The goal changes only on real data, never to look better.
