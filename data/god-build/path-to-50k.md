# Path to 50,000 Google visitors a month by July 2027

Written 2026-10-16. **Analysis only.** Nothing was built, scheduled, committed or edited apart from this file and `path-to-50k.json`, which holds the same data. Sources: Search Console (the owner's API pull to Oct 12 and the Ahrefs-connected history to Sep 11), the Ahrefs API (30,134 units used of the 50,000 allowed), and stored files under `data/`. Search Console figures are measurements. Ahrefs traffic figures are estimates (calibrated in section 2). Forecasts are models, not measurements.

## Summary for the owner

**Where we are:** 10,103 Google clicks in August, 10,154 in September, about 6,800 a month in October. Your middle forecast for July 2027, with everything already planned, is about 17,000. **The gap to 50,000 is about 33,000 clicks a month.**

**Is 50,000 realistic? Possible, but it is not the likely outcome.** If we run every step below at full effort, the middle outcome is about **36,000** in July 2027 (low 26,000, high 60,000). We only reach 50,000 in the high case. That case needs our new US pages to perform like the pages of small pest-company blogs that already get this traffic, and that takes two things together: a large number of new US pages, and links.

**It has been done from where we stand.** bettertermite.com is a small Virginia pest company. It grew from 1,081 visits a month (June 2025) to 56,156 (October 2025) and then 74,000-105,000, according to Ahrefs. Its authority while it grew was close to ours: about 410-450 referring domains (we have 374) and DR 16-17. It got there with about 280-315 pages in Google's top 10. Almost all of them are photo identification pages (roach poop, bed bug bite pictures, baby roaches, tiny black bugs in the bathroom, fleas in hair), and most have 0-3 links each.

**The single biggest lever is the number of US identification pages in Google's top 3.** We already have 270 pages in the top 10, which is about what bettertermite had. But ours earn about 30 visits each and theirs earned 150-230. Ours are mostly Canadian, mosquito and tick, or quick-fact pages sitting at positions 6-8, where only 0.6-0.9% of searchers click. Top-10 pages turn into traffic when they serve US demand and reach the top 3.

- **Step 4 per page:** 12, 25 or 60 clicks a month in July. 12 is this site's median new page. 25 is the July-2026 cohort's in-season mean of 40, discounted for US click-through (0.6% against 1.3% in Canada). 60 is what the comparable blogs earn per page (70-130, Ahrefs x1.1) after allowing for our lower authority.
- **To reach 50,000 at the middle yield** you would need about 1,050 new pages, which cannot be written and fact-checked by March. At 60 clicks a page you would need about 440. So links (step 5) and topic choice decide whether 50,000 happens. Page count alone will not get there.
- **Timing is strict.** On this site a new page earns 10% of its mature traffic in its first month, 35% in the second, 65% in the third and 85% in the fourth. A page must be live by about **March 15** to deliver most of its value in July. Pages published in May deliver about a third. Links must land by April and upgrades before May, when the season starts.
- **What July favours:** wasps, ticks and mosquitoes, flies, bites, fleas and ants all peak in summer, so July rewards them. Rats and mice, cockroaches, bed bugs and pantry pests hold steady all year and pay better for Mediavine's Q4. Build both. Do not build only for July.

### Five honest caveats

1. Every forecast here is a model. The 86 US guides and the 62 pages released on Oct 16 are the real test. If they average 20+ clicks a page a month by Dec 15, the middle case holds. If they reach 40+, the high case is in play. If they stay under 10, cut step 4 to the best families only.
2. AI Overviews are on almost every one of these results pages. Our measured click-through at position 3 is only 1.9-2.6%. The plan already uses those measured rates, not textbook rates.
3. Head terms (wolf spider, house centipede, spider) are not winnable now. Wikipedia, WebMD and .gov/.edu sites hold page 1 (section 4). Their huge volumes are left out of the plan.
4. Many independent pest-info sites collapsed to almost no traffic in 2024-2026: pestseek, pestsamurai, pestpolicy, insectcop, smithspestmanagement, pestpointers, whatsthatbug. Scaled thin content is the main risk to this plan, both with Google and with Mediavine's AI-content policy. Keep the fact-check and real-photo pipeline. Do not drop it to go faster.
5. Search Console data stops at Oct 12 (the API token has expired). The Ahrefs GSC connection stopped syncing on Sep 11, and its page and query tables are empty.
