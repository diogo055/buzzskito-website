# Courtesy copy to CDC (send after the first tick pages go live, Nov 16)

CDC's tick surveillance tables (ArboNET Tick Module) come with a data-use agreement. It allows
publications made from the data, provided they reference the ArboNET Tick Module (every page
does) and that "a final copy of the publications or presentations resulting from these data
will be provided to the CDC Division of Vector-Borne Diseases". This is that copy. The owner
sends it from support@buzzskito.ca; nothing is sent automatically.

**To:** ticksurveillance@cdc.gov
**Subject:** Publications using ArboNET Tick Module county data (buzzskito.ca)

Hello,

We publish free, source-cited tick information for readers in the United States and Canada.
Our new state pages use the ArboNET Tick Module county tables (Ixodes county status and
pathogens, lone star tick and American dog tick status, through December 31, 2025) to draw
each state's county map, and every page references the ArboNET Tick Module and states that a
county with no records is not a county without ticks.

As your data release guidelines ask, here are the pages (the hub links to all of them):
https://buzzskito.ca/learn/ticks-by-state

We do not redistribute the tables themselves. If you would like any change to how the data are
described, please reply and we will correct it.
