## How long it took (summer peak each year, Ahrefs monthly history)

- **fox-pest.com:** 2021 3K, 2022 14K, 2023 69K, 2024 56K, 2025 106K, **2026 223K**. Four summers of climbing after content started in 2022; roughly doubling a year from 2024.
- **bugoutservice.com:** 1-3K for 2019-2022, 2023 7.5K, 2024 22K, **2025 121K**, **2026 156K**. Two years from 7.5K to 156K.
- **bettertermite.com:** 1K (Jun 2025) to 74K (Nov 2025) to **106K (Jun 2026)**. The fastest: about a year.
- **orkincanada.ca:** 2021 94K, 2022 137K, 2023 162K, 2024 165K, **2025 239K**, 2026 127K (fell 47% this summer: even big brands swing).
- Winter months run about 30-50% of the summer peak on every one of these sites.

## What carries fox-pest.com at its peak (top pages, Jul 2026)

- About a third is people looking for a company: the New York locations page alone 50K ("pest control near me"), brand searches ~27K. **We cannot copy this part** (one company, GTA only).
- **Droppings family ~49K:** roach poop 16.1K, rat poop 10.8K, squirrel poop 7.6K, mouse poop 7.1K, bed bug poop 4.8K, mouse pee 2.7K.
- Species "pest files": little black ants 8.3K, carpenter ants 4.1K, voles 3.8K, mud daubers 3.6K, American / oriental / brown-banded cockroaches 2.5K / 2.3K / 2.0K.
- X vs Y and how-to: carpenter bee vs bumble bee 2.6K, sugar ants 3.1K.

## What it means for us

1. **200K has been done by pest companies:** Fox (223K, July 2026) and Orkin Canada (239K, July 2025); Bug Out (156K) and bettertermite (106K) are smaller companies on the way.
2. **What they had when they got there:** 900 to 2,200+ pages, DR 36 to 66, 600 to 3,000 dofollow linking sites, and 2 to 4 years of climbing. Pages: we are on track (681 now, about 1,200 by spring with the God plan). **Authority is our biggest gap: 55 dofollow linking sites against 600 to 3,000.** Time: our content push began in summer 2026.
3. **Fastest path seen:** about 100K in the first year (bettertermite), then doubling (Fox 106K to 223K). For us that reads as 50K to 100K next summer if the formula lands, and 200K in summer 2028 at the earliest if authority grows with it. Middle path: 36-50K in 2027, 100K in 2028, 200K in 2029.
4. **Build the droppings family first** (cockroach, rat, mouse, squirrel, bed bug, raccoon droppings, mouse urine): it carries about 49K a month for Fox and pairs with Amazon products (traps, gloves, cleaner).
5. A 200K summer month would be worth roughly $10,600-16,200 from ads and Amazon (four times the 50K month).
