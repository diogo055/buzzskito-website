## Why this is the right lever

The brief names this metric as decisive (§2):

> "The single variable separating a $2,600 outcome from a $5,000 outcome is what fraction of
> affiliate clicks land on high-ticket commercial pages rather than informational ones."

Routing beats building here because:
- The pages already exist — no new content risk, no new ranking bet
- It is purely additive internal linking — no affiliate element moves
- It works on traffic the site already has, today
- It does not require winning a KD 93 query

## The constraint that governs it

**A link only counts if a reader genuinely benefits.** A chigger-bite page does not need a
dehumidifier. A no-see-um page genuinely does need the finer-mesh screening page, because
no-see-ums pass straight through standard 16×16 window mesh — that is a real problem the
reader has just learned they have.

Every link added in this stage carries its reason in the sentence. Links that could not earn
a sentence were not added, and the verifier was instructed to strip any that read as a
stretch. The site's 10.4% conversion rate rests on recommendations being trustworthy; a
forced link trades that for a click, which is a bad trade.

## Applied 2026-09-03

Routing pass across the 13 unrouted top pages, at most 2–3 links each, each verified for
target existence, topical honesty, and zero movement of any affiliate element.

**What this does NOT do:** it does not lift the pages' own rankings, and it does not change
what Amazon pays per order. It changes which page a reader is standing on when they decide to
buy. That is the mix, and the mix is what the forecast is most sensitive to.
