# Cluster: cockroaches

## New pages in this cluster (link targets: use these exact addresses)

- `/learn/cockroaches` — **cockroaches**. The complete cockroach reference for US homes: the main species (German, American, Oriental, brown-banded, smokybrown, Turkestan, wood roaches) and how to tell them apart, biology and why populations explode, where they come from, health risks (allergens, asthma, pathogens), signs, prevention, the control sequence (sanitation, monitoring, gel baits, insect growth regulators, dusts), why sprays and foggers fail, apartments, and when to call a professional.

- `/blog/german-cockroaches` — **german cockroaches**. The German cockroach: identification (the two dark stripes behind the head), size in inches, biology and reproduction numbers, where they hide, how they arrive, why they are the hardest household roach, health effects, and the control program that works (sanitation, monitoring, gel bait, insect growth regulators) versus what fails (sprays, foggers).

- `/blog/what-do-baby-cockroaches-look-like` — **what do baby cockroaches look like**. Cockroach nymphs by species (German, American, Oriental, brown-banded): size, color, markings, how they differ from adults, baby roach versus bed bug versus beetle, what finding nymphs tells you about the infestation, and what to do next.

- `/blog/bugs-that-look-like-cockroaches` — **bugs that look like cockroaches**. The look-alikes: ground beetles, June beetles, crickets, giant water bugs, wood roaches (a real cockroach that does not infest homes), palmetto bug (a name for several large roaches), earwigs, bed bugs. How to tell each from a pest cockroach. A table.

- `/blog/water-bugs-vs-cockroaches` — **water bugs vs cockroaches**. The naming confusion: true water bugs (giant water bugs, aquatic and predatory, they bite) versus the cockroaches people call waterbugs (Oriental and American). Side-by-side identification, habitat, risk, and what to do about each. A table.

- `/blog/can-cockroaches-fly` — **can cockroaches fly** (also: do cockroaches fly). Which cockroaches fly (the page also answers 'do cockroaches fly'): species by species (American glides, smokybrown and Asian fly well, male wood roaches fly to lights, German almost never, Oriental cannot), why warm weather brings out flying, why they fly toward you, and what flying roaches mean for where they came from. A table.

- `/blog/do-cockroaches-bite` — **do cockroaches bite**. Cockroach bites: rare and associated with very heavy infestations, what is documented, what a suspected bite looks like and the more likely culprits, and the real health issues (allergens and asthma, bacteria carried on the body) with sources.

- `/blog/where-do-cockroaches-come-from` — **where do cockroaches come from**. How cockroaches get into a home: carried in (boxes, bags, used appliances and furniture), through shared walls and plumbing in apartments, up from drains and sewers, in from outdoors. Which species arrives which way, why clean homes still get them, and how to close each route.
