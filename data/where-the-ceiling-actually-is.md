# The ceiling is the commission rate, not domain authority

Written 2026-09-03, in answer to a direct question from the owner: *"we are at the ceiling of
our capability to make money through referrals right now without more domain authority?"*

**The honest answer is no — and I was wrong to imply otherwise earlier today.** The pushback
was correct.

---

## 1. There is no traffic ceiling. There is +52% sitting in CTR.

I first reported a "flat 5x CTR penalty at every position" against published benchmarks and
read it as a brand-recognition wall. Re-checking on the owner's challenge, that averaged over
a **bimodal** distribution and hid the real story.

Among Canadian query-page pairs ranking in the top 5 with >=100 impressions:

| | Pairs | Impressions | CTR |
|---|---|---|---|
| already at benchmark | 20 | 11,239 | **5-13.8%** |
| badly under | 52 | 13,462 | **<2%** |

The site already earns **13.8% at position 2.0** on "summit mosquito bits canada", 9.3% on
"mosquito bits canada", 8.4% on "worst areas for ticks in ontario". Those are normal,
healthy numbers. So there is no wall — some pages clear it today.

Sizing the gap against **the site's own 75th-percentile CTR at each position** (a fairer test
than an industry benchmark, because it only asks pages to do what sibling pages already do):

| Position | Impressions | Clicks now | Own p75 | Gain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3-5 | 24,969 | 718 | 4.51% | +408 |
| 5-7 | 81,614 | 1,500 | 2.78% | +767 |
| 7-10 | 142,504 | 966 | 1.28% | +861 |
| 10-15 | 43,755 | 120 | 0.40% | +55 |

**+2,091 Canadian clicks per 84 days — +52% — with no new pages and no ranking change.**

The losers are bare head terms ("permethrin", "chiggers", "horse fly", "june bug",
"picaridin") where the title answers a narrower question than the one typed. The winners are
specific and intent-matched. That is a title-and-snippet problem, which is why the title
rewrite programme was approved and is now running.

## 2. But +52% traffic is worth only ~$56/month, because of what a click is worth

At the blended **$0.0754 per blog click**, the entire recoverable CTR gap is ~$56/month at
full capture, ~$19 at a realistic third. That is the part worth sitting with.

## 3. Here is where the money actually stops

| | Value | Could it supply 6x? |
|---|---|---|
| blog clicks | 8,663 | +52% available. **Not 6x.** |
| click-out to Amazon | **32.7%** | No — already ~16x the ~2% industry benchmark |
| purchase conversion | **10.4%** | No — would need 64%. No affiliate site converts at 64% |
| **commission per order** | **$2.21** | **Yes — and only this one** |

$2.21 on a $46.53 basket is a **4.75% commission rate**. That is the ceiling. Not authority,
not traffic, not the funnel — the funnel is exceptional and has been for months.

To reach $4,000/month at today's traffic you would need **$13.53 per order**. Levanta's
measured Canadian rate on Harris bed bug killer is **CA$16.81 per conversion — 7.6x** what
Amazon pays (`data/levanta-catalogue-findings.md`). Premo Guard $22.49, Flowtron 2-acre $40,
Rugged Ranch $37.70.

**That single row is where the 6x lives, and it is measured rather than hoped for.**

## 4. What this means in practice

- **Domain authority is not the binding constraint.** It would raise rankings, but rankings
  feed a funnel that already converts at 10.4% into orders worth $2.21. Better rankings
  multiply the small number.
- **The binding constraint is that Amazon.ca pays ~4.75%** on a basket the site cannot easily
  raise — Stage G already established that its 72 high-basket pages underperform.
- **The unlock is brand-direct affiliate rates,** and it is blocked on one thing that only the
  owner can do: get the Levanta application approved, then ask each brand to activate the
  amazon.ca marketplace for Amazon Attribution.

Realistic stacked outcome: capture a third of the CTR gap (+17% traffic) and move the top
products to brand-direct rates (call it 3x blended, not the full 7.6x, since not every brand
will approve) lands around **$2,300/month**. Both terms are needed; neither alone gets close.

**Caveats.** The 7.6x is one measured product row, not a portfolio average — Levanta rates in
this niche run 3% (Summit Dunks, below Amazon) to 20% (Harris). The CTR headroom is an
upper bound that assumes every under-performing page can reach its siblings' rate; the title
programme now running will produce the real capture number by 2026-10-15.
