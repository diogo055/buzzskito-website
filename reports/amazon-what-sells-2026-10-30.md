- **Oct 29 was a real spike, not yet a trend:** CA$718.79 ordered, 16 items, 48 clicks. That is the best ordered day since Sep 20 (CA$974). The last 7 days (CA$1,700 ordered, 42 items) were still below the 7 before (CA$2,129, 56 items).

- **The motion sprinkler is the top seller in every period we could see** (Jul-Sep, Aug, Sep, Oct).
- **Most sales are not the product we linked:** 198 of 216 items in October.
- **Bed bugs holds through the fall** while mosquito falls away.
- **The US store is real but tiny:** about US$4.58 so far.

## Is Amazon the lever to 3-4x income?

No, not on its own. These rates are from August to October; none comes from the start of a season.

**Amazon pays about 7 cents per Canadian visit, and that has barely moved.**

| Period | Canadian sessions | Earned | Earned per session | Ordered per session |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Aug 16-31 | 5,000 | CA$252.62 | 5.1¢ | CA$1.46 |
| Sep | 8,247 | CA$574.86 | 7.0¢ | CA$1.32 |
| Oct 1-14 | 2,741 | CA$166.73 | 6.1¢ | CA$1.40 |
| Oct 15-29 | 2,937 | CA$227.23 | 7.7¢ | CA$1.51 |

Earnings count shipped items, so short periods are noisy. The rise in late October is worth watching but too early to credit.

**Where that money sits:** a Canadian product-page view earns about 10 cents, a remedy-page view about 6 and an information-page view under 1. There are only about 3,000 Canadian product-page views a month right now. New visitors from the US and from image search add almost nothing on Amazon: US readers made 73 clicks on 4,400 views in 30 days, which is worth about CA$12-20 a month even if all of them bought through the right store.

**What 3-4x takes.** October is about CA$420, so the target is CA$1,260-1,680.

- **Amazon alone:** 18,000-24,000 Canadian sessions a month at 7 cents. The best month so far is August's 9,216. The goal has Amazon.ca at CA$1,425 in July.
- **With ads on:** the Oct 29 forecast has about CA$950 in December, CA$600-700 in January and February, about CA$2,000 in May and about CA$3,100 in July. On that path, 3-4x arrives around April. All of it is an estimate until the first real Mediavine month.

**The Levanta correction.** The Sep 3 note called it 7.6x and the only real lever. That compared one order's commission, but 92% of October's items were not the product we linked. Later research found Levanta pays only on the linked product and needs about 22% of items to be that product, against about 8% here, so it loses money on most pages. The biggest example, Harris, is also not registered in Canada. Ignore the CA$40-125 in the table above.

**Correction, same day.** This section first said new product pages weren't worth doing, based on a median of $0.16 a month. That figure came from a data pull that misses most Canadian clicks. Re-measured, a new page earns about CA$30 a year on average and CA$8 at the median, so they add real money in tested batches. See `reports/Canadian Amazon earnings growth plan.md`.

**Not worth doing:** more Amazon boxes on information pages without a test (tried in September, no effect), or US shopping pages on this domain (measured Oct 21, they barely appear).
