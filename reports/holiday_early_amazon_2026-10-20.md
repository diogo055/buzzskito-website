**Goal:** before the holiday shopping weeks (Black Friday Nov 27 to Christmas), every Canadian winter page
that earns Amazon.ca money should show the reader a clear route to Amazon near the top. A click is what pays
(you earn on the reader's whole cart for 24 hours), and holiday carts are the biggest of the year.

  commit c86dbc38, which you approved for every page it fits).

## How to compare November 20 to December 31 with October

Amazon pays on the reader's cart, so measure **Amazon.ca earnings per 1,000 Canadian visits**. October ran
about **$68**.

1. **On or after January 15, 2027** (Amazon counts earnings when items ship, so late-December orders appear
   in early January), take two screenshots in Associates Central (amazon.ca): the Summary for
   **October 1 to 31** and for **November 20 to December 31**, showing **Clicks** and **Total Earnings**.
   Amazon.ca only; amazon.com is a separate account and a separate test.
2. Claude runs (reads Google Analytics, changes nothing):
   `python -I scripts/holiday-amazon-compare.py --oct-earnings <$> --oct-clicks <n> --hol-earnings <$> --hol-clicks <n>`
3. Read three numbers, October against the holiday weeks:
   - **$ per 1,000 Canadian visits**: the headline.
   - **Amazon clicks per 1,000 Canadian visits**: how often readers go to Amazon (this is what the box changes).
   - **$ per Amazon click**: what each trip is worth (this is where holiday carts show).
   The headline is the second times the third, so you can see whether a change came from more trips or
   bigger carts. The script also prints the 30 winter pages' clicks per 1,000 views (336 in October 1 to 19)
   and the top box's share of all Amazon clicks.
4. Keep in mind: October had the box only for its last 12 days. A change in clicks per 1,000 visits smaller
   than about a quarter cannot be told apart from normal ups and downs. If Mediavine ads start inside the
   window, note the date (ads can take some clicks). Canadian visits are not affected by the Oct 19 bot block
   (it only stops Singapore and China).
