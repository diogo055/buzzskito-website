## The short version

1. **You are closer than you think.** "12 to 15" on "mosquito control" is a Canada-wide average (Search Console: 11.0 last season). From GTA locations today you are on page 1 for 16 of 23 searches and in the top 3 plain results for 8. Only Mosquito Man has more (11).
2. **Your pages are already better than the pages beating you.** Mosquito Man's city pages run about 900 words, show no price and carry a broken sentence. Mosquito Hero's run about 500 words. Yours run 3,000 to 5,000 words with the price in the headline. More words will not move you.
3. **The winners win above the plain results.** Sponsored boxes top 18 of 23 searches. Mosquito Man is in 16 of them. BuzzSkito is in none. Next comes the map pack, where they have a separate listing for each city and you have one.
4. **Pages alone will not produce 2,000 leads.** All the page changes below together: about **+100 to +200 leads** on the roughly 400 I already forecast for spring 2027. That is 500 to 600. Section 4 covers the rest.

## How I checked, and the limits

- **Browser:** the built-in browser, signed out, with Google's personal history switched off.
- **Location:** my real location was Brampton (Churchville). For city searches I set the city in the Google address, and Google's footer confirmed each one.
- **Google blocked me.** After about 40 result pages, at 10:57 a.m., Google showed an "unusual traffic" robot check. I stopped and did not try to solve it. I also did not move to your own Chrome: it is the same network, and it holds your real Google account. **You may see that check on your network for a short while.**
- **Four searches I could not check on Google:** "is it worth spraying your yard for mosquitoes", "how much does it cost to spray your backyard for mosquitoes", "how much does it cost to get rid of ticks", "mosquito and tick control near me". I tried the WebSearch tool. Its results are US-based and not local, so I report no positions from it.
- **It is end of October.** Mosquito demand is low, so ads and rankings will shift. Re-check in late April.
- **Screen size:** results were read at desktop width. On a phone, ads and the AI answer push plain results further down.
- **Other data:** Search Console (Canada, Apr 15 to Jul 31, 2026), GA4 (Ontario, same dates), and public domain registration records. No Ahrefs. I clicked no ads and submitted no forms.
