# From 500 to 2,000 sessions a day: the plan from your own Search Console (Nov 3, 2026)

Built from 6 months of Google Search Console data (May 3 to Nov 1, 2026): 182,000 search-and-page rows, 42,800 Google clicks, 4.5 million impressions. Also last summer (Jun-Aug), the last 28 days, and your Google Business Profile searches.

## The honest headline

**2,000 sessions a day is reachable in June-August 2027, not this winter.**
- **The site already grew 5x in clicks since May**, from 61 Google clicks a day to 261-430. Last summer's peak ran at 326-338 a day, from a site a third this size.
- **Bug searches swing hard with the season**: clicks rose more than 5x from May to August last year (61 a day to 326), partly the season and partly new pages.
- **The winter work decides how high summer goes.** Upgrade the pages that are close, build the pages people must click, and get the summer pages written and aged before May.

**Where clicks go now** (last 28 days, by position):

| Position on Google | Share of searchers who click |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1.0% |
| 2-3 | 0.75-1.4% |
| 4-5 | 1.1% |
| 5-7 | 0.65% |
| 7-10 | 0.30% |
| 10-15 | 0.17% |

Even #1 gets about 1 searcher in 100, because Google answers most bug questions itself (AI answers, image rows). So "more pages" and "higher positions" each help only a little. What moves the number:
1. **Searches with huge volume where we sit at position 7-12** (moving to the top 3 roughly triples or quadruples their clicks).
2. **Page types Google cannot answer for the reader**: bites, pest identification with photos, "X vs Y", droppings, and product picks.
3. **The season**: be in the top 3 on summer searches before May.

## What I would be shouting at you

1. **Stop building pages Google answers by itself.** "What time of day are mosquitoes most active" sits at position 2.5 with 16,200 impressions and got 12 clicks. "How many ants are in the world" had 4,200 impressions and 6 clicks. Trivia questions are dead traffic.
   - **Bite pages:** 42 pages earned 1,297 clicks in 28 days, and the top tenth earn 81+ each.
   - **Identification pages:** the top tenth earn 55+ each.
   - **Droppings pages:** the best median of any family (6.5 clicks per page).
   - Build those, not trivia.
2. **Push the big searches we're already close on into the top 3** (table below). This is the biggest and fastest lever, and no new pages are needed.
3. **Canadians are telling you where they shop.** About 55,000 impressions over 6 months were for "[product] canadian tire" or "[product] home depot". Examples: "thermacell canadian tire" (11,700), "mosquito dunks canadian tire" (5,100), "canadian tire thermacell" (3,400), "mosquito bits canadian tire" (2,000) and "fruit fly trap canadian tire" (1,900 + 1,500). None of our page titles says "Canadian Tire". Product readers are your most valuable visitors (Amazon pays about 3x more per visit on product pages).
4. **Get last summer's winners ready by March, not May.** Laser mosquito killer review 1,945 clicks; mosquito bits vs dunks 1,914; permethrin 1,397; bugs that look like ticks 1,056; black flies Ontario 977; ground wasps 755; mosquito dunks 563; are ticks dangerous 463. They now sit at 4-158 clicks a month (season over). Update them for 2027 with photos and better titles so they start May higher than they ended August.
5. **Your money pages are invisible.** "mosquito control toronto" shows the Toronto page at position 28 (4,900 impressions in 6 months). The 121 service and city pages made 25 clicks in 28 days (off-season, but they barely ranked in summer either). Your Business Profile shows what buyers type: "mosquito spray" 166, "ontario mosquito control" 109, "mosquito control" 76, "tick spray" 48, "tick control for yard" 47, "backyard mosquito control" 22. Rebuild the service side around those words before March. One spray customer is worth about 80,000 image visits.
6. **Photos are the clicks AI can't take.** Image search sent about 1,500 clicks a month in October, and identification and bite pages with real, labelled photos win there.

## How this was measured

Search Console API (sc-domain:buzzskito.ca):
- query x page for 6 months, summer and the last 28 days; page totals; image search.

How the data was handled:
- Page totals add up each page's "#section" rows.
- Families come from page addresses.
- "No page made for it" means no live, indexable page's title, H1 or address holds all the search's words.

Limits:
- Only about 45% of impressions come with their search words; Google hides the rest.
- The CTR table is October's.
- Summer is one season of data.

Scripts and output are in the session scratchpad (`godplan/`).
