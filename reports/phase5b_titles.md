## Honest expectation

8 of the 14 old titles ALREADY opened with the dominant query's words (most were rewritten in an earlier pass - see the comment blocks in the larvae, thermacell and spray-safe files). Their CTR is still 0.1-0.5% at positions 5-9, which means the remaining gap on those pages is mostly SERP features (AI Overviews, People-Also-Ask, image packs on the biology and health queries), not title mismatch. The site's 3% curve is a site-wide average that those query types will not reach. Expect the biggest lift where the old title genuinely missed the phrasing:

- **what-blood-type-do-mosquitoes-bite** - demand says "hate", title said "bite".
- **flea-bites-on-humans** - demand is the question "what do flea bites look like"; title never used it, and the description was 199 chars (truncated).
- **thermacell-canada-where-to-buy** - "where to buy" now leads; retailer-named queries deliberately not targeted.
- **how-long-do-ticks-live** - the "in a house" and "without a host" sub-intents (~4.2K impr, 6 clicks) were absent from the title.
- **can-ticks-fly-jump-swim** - "drown" and "survive in water" now lead.
- **male-vs-female-mosquito** - the hook was the AI-overview answer ("only females bite"); replaced with the identification promise.
- **mosquito-larvae-identification** - adds the exact "what they look like" phrasing (6.2K impr at pos 6.5-7.2, where the click is winnable; the 37K-impr head term sits behind an image pack at pos 9.7).

Smaller, deliberate tweaks (already-matching titles): why-do-mosquito-bites-itch, ultrasonic-pest-repellers, how-long-does-mosquito-spray-last, deer-flies-horse-flies, why-do-mosquitoes-bite-my-ankles, best-fruit-fly-trap.

## Two owner decisions

1. **is-mosquito-spray-safe-kids-pets** - rewritten for compliance, not CTR. The dominant query is literally "...safe for dogs", which is banned in titles; the old title and description both carried it, and the old description answered "Yes". The new pair defers to the label. Expect some CTR cost in exchange for a defensible snippet.
2. **how-long-does-mosquito-spray-last** - this is a service page. The old title had query + "in Ontario"; the new one swaps geo for the "type by type" differentiator because both do not fit in 48. Geo-in-title is the measured lead lever, so if you would rather keep "in Ontario", keep the old title and apply only the new description.
