## How the engine works

1. **Topics** come from what already gets searched: Search Console and Trends.
2. **Scripts** use only facts from our fact-checked pages, with a real source behind each one.
3. **Pictures:** real public-domain or CC0 photos (495 of the 720 in the ledger), real job footage, and one AI clip per video at most.
4. **Build:** the existing content factory renders them for free, with its Health Canada wording guard.
5. **Approval:** a weekly batch lands in Ready to Post, which syncs to the owner's phone. Nothing is posted without approval at the start.
6. **Posting:** scheduled on each platform once the owner connects the accounts. How much can run hands-off on each platform still has to be confirmed.
7. **Measure:** booked jobs and quotes first, then link clicks, watch time and views. Do not judge a video style on fewer than 1,000 views.

## Rules

- **AI shows atmosphere, scale and the problem.** Every frame with mist, gear or the technician is real. Turn on the platform's AI label and disclose in the caption.
- **Never use AI for a bug, bite or droppings shown as real.** A wrong picture on an identification video is false information from a licensed operator.
- **No AI person posing as a technician or customer.** No fake reviews or testing claims.
- **No customer footage in Higgsfield,** because its terms grant training rights.
- **No templated mass uploads.** [YouTube judges whole channels](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/youtube-clarifies-policies-around-ai-slop-and-upsetting-videos/) on generic, repetitive content. Each video needs its own script and shots.
- **No automated Reddit posts or dropped links.** Reddit [reportedly removes about 100,000 bot and spam accounts a day](https://ppc.land/reddit-bots-threaten-ad-quality-heres-how-the-platform-fights-back/), and each subreddit's moderators set their own rules.
- **Amazon:** list any social account that carries an affiliate link in the Associates account first, with the required disclosure.
- **Off limits as before:** Health Canada's banned wording, efficacy promises and competitor names.

## What Hormozi's free material changes (added Nov 1)

Read from transcripts and summaries of his free podcast and videos, not the books, so details may be imprecise. Some examples come from other industries.

- **Check the limit first.** Are we short of customers or of van time? At 5 stops a day, 6 days a week, one van fills at about 60 bi-weekly customers, about what the 2026 season had (62). The stops-per-day answer decides how much new demand we can serve.

- **Offer something free before asking for a quote,** named after the result. He says this can bring 3-5 times more contacts. Test the yard report this way in spring.
- **Proof beats promises:** lead with the reviews.
- **Partners:** give landscapers and pool companies one specific product to sell.

## What would prove this wrong

Read after the first 30 videos. If Shorts and TikTok average under about 200 views with no standout hits, cut the volume and put the time into long demos. If one topic takes off, make more of that topic.
