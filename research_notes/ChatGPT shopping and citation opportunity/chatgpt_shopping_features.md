# ChatGPT shopping and commerce features, and how products and sources get chosen (state as of Oct 21, 2026)

Method note: OpenAI's own pages (openai.com, help.openai.com, chatgpt.com/merchants, developers.openai.com) were read directly in a browser on Oct 21, 2026. Amazon's robots.txt files were fetched directly on Oct 21, 2026. Items marked "(search summary)" come from a search-engine summary of the page, not from opening the page itself, so treat their exact wording with more caution.

## 1. What shopping features exist in ChatGPT, when they launched, where (US vs Canada), and how they work

### Takeaway
ChatGPT has product carousels in search answers, a "shopping research" buyer's-guide mode (Nov 2025), virtual try-on and saved favourites (Oct 2026), and a merchant product-feed program built on the Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP). Instant Checkout (in-chat buying, Sep 2025) was wound down in March 2026: purchases now finish on the merchant's own site or app. OpenAI says the feed-powered shopping experience is "currently live in the U.S." only. Ads (separate from product results) have run in Canada since Apr 16, 2026 and in 89 countries as of Oct 21, 2026.

### Inferences
- For a Canadian reader in October 2026, the confirmed pieces are: ordinary ChatGPT answers with citations, ads (Free/Go only), and probably shopping research and try-on (no country limit stated). The feed-powered product-discovery experience that OpenAI is investing in is officially US-only, so US readers see far more product cards and merchant buttons than Canadian readers do.
- OpenAI's money-making has moved from a transaction fee (4% on Instant Checkout) to selling ads (CPC/CPM, product-feed campaigns). Organic shopping clicks to merchant sites carry "no fees," so OpenAI currently earns nothing per organic sale and has no need for an affiliate layer.

### Gaps
- No OpenAI page states plainly whether Canadian users see product carousels, merchant lists or "Shop at" buttons today. Testing would require running shopping queries from a Canadian account (not done: it would mean submitting prompts in the owner's account).
- The exact date Instant Checkout stopped for all merchants is not published; OpenAI's own help article still mentions it.
- Whether product-feed ads are sold in Canada is not stated anywhere I found.

## 2. How ChatGPT chooses which products and sources to show; what OpenAI says about structured data, feeds, publisher reviews and crawlers; whether shopping answers link to publishers or straight to merchants

### Takeaway
Product cards come from structured product data (merchant feeds, Shopify/Etsy catalogs, third-party data providers) and link to merchants; publisher review pages appear as cited sources in the text and inform review summaries, but are not where the product cards and "Shop at" buttons send people. To be cited at all, a site must allow OAI-SearchBot. Independent tracking shows product cards shifting sharply toward merchant feeds in July 2026 and concentrating on big retailers.

### Cited Findings
**What OpenAI says (help article, read Oct 21, 2026)**
- "Product results are selected independently by ChatGPT and are not ads, nor influenced by any OpenAI partnerships." — [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-shopping-with-chatgpt-search)
- ChatGPT considers: "Structured metadata from first-party and third-party providers (e.g., price, product description) and other third-party content"; "Model responses generated by ChatGPT before it considers any new search results"; and "OpenAI safety standards and product policies." It also uses the user's query and context "(such as Memory or Custom instructions)," and may weigh "available options, price, reviews, and ease of use." — [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-shopping-with-chatgpt-search)
- Review summaries "are based on reviews from public websites... Reviews and ratings are not verified by OpenAI." Labels like "Budget-friendly" are model-generated and "may include third party data." Prices come "from third-party providers"; the first price shown "may not be the lowest available price." — [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-shopping-with-chatgpt-search)
- Merchants for a product are "ranked based on factors like availability, price, quality, and whether they are the maker or primary seller of that item." — [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-shopping-with-chatgpt-search). In Sep 2025 the list also included "Instant Checkout availability." — [OpenAI release notes, Sep 29, 2025](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)
- Merchant page pitch: "Richer data helps your products surface with more accurate information when shoppers compare options" and "Send shoppers to your site or app by default." — [chatgpt.com/merchants](https://chatgpt.com/merchants/)
- Shopping research: "trained it to read trusted sites, cite reliable sources, and synthesize information across many sources"; "Results are organic and based on publicly available retail sites—reading product pages directly, citing sources, and avoiding low-quality or spammy sites"; final guide includes "up-to-date information from reliable retailers." — [OpenAI, Nov 24, 2025](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-shopping-research/)
- Shopping research is "trained to avoid sites that may be low quality, spammy or overly commercial"; Isa Fulford (leads OpenAI's Deep Research and ChatGPT agent teams) defined low-quality sources as "sites where there are pop-up ads" or "clearly advertisements"; the tool has "a focus on organic content, including review sites and even Reddit"; recommendations are "ranked primarily by relevance to the user's query." — [Digiday/Modern Retail, Dec 1, 2025](https://digiday.com/media-buying/openais-new-chatgpt-shopping-tool-promises-in-depth-research-just-not-for-amazon-products/)

**Crawlers (OpenAI documentation, read Oct 21, 2026)**
- OAI-SearchBot: "used to surface websites in search results in ChatGPT's search features. Sites that are opted out of OAI-SearchBot will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers, though can still appear as navigational links." Changes take "~24 hours." — [OpenAI crawlers overview](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)
- GPTBot is for training; settings are independent ("allow OAI-SearchBot in order to appear in search results while disallowing GPTBot"). — [OpenAI crawlers overview](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)
- ChatGPT-User fetches pages for user actions; "robots.txt rules may not apply"; "not used to determine whether content may appear in Search." — [OpenAI crawlers overview](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)
- New: OAI-AdsBot visits ad landing pages to check policy and relevance; its data "is not used to train." — [OpenAI crawlers overview](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)
- Fulford on respecting site rules: "Anything that allows us to access their site, our product will access, and anything that we don't, we won't access." — [Digiday/Modern Retail, Dec 1, 2025](https://digiday.com/media-buying/openais-new-chatgpt-shopping-tool-promises-in-depth-research-just-not-for-amazon-products/)

**Independent studies of where products come from (third-party, observational)**
- Peec AI (published in Search Engine Land, Mar 2026): of 43,000+ carousel products, 83% strongly matched products in Google's top 40 organic Shopping results (Bing: 11%); 60% of matches came from Google's top 10. The "Google Shopping pipeline" reading is the vendor's interpretation. — [Search Engine Land (search summary)](https://searchengineland.com/new-finding-chatgpt-sources-83-of-its-carousel-products-from-google-shopping-via-shopping-query-fan-outs-470723)
- Profound (reported by SEJ, Sep 9, 2026): across 1,757,723 tracked July prompt runs, "feed-integrated" recommendations jumped from 8.26% to 61.54% on July 10, 2026, and reached about 65% by Sep 3; top-10 merchants' share of references rose from 22.5% to 41.8%; unique merchants fell from 13,524 to 10,607; about 35% of July feed retrieval related to Shopify (estimate). OpenAI has not confirmed the July change. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/chatgpt-shopping-results-lean-hard-on-product-feeds/589000/)

**Do shopping answers link to publishers or merchants?**
- Product cards show "links to sites where you can learn more or purchase," and clicking a product shows a list of merchants. — [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-shopping-with-chatgpt-search)
- "Shop at" buttons (Oct 10-13, 2026): 56% went to big-box retailers (the analysis groups Best Buy, Walmart, Target and Amazon there), about 5% to the brand's own store, 39% to competitor brands and specialist retailers; publisher/review sites were not analyzed. Answers linking to a big-box store rose from 3.7% to 19.2% of 250 prompts after Oct 10. When the brand's product page was among the cited sources, 34% of its buttons went to the brand's store vs 6% otherwise. Sample is small and provisional. — [Wislr](https://www.wislr.com/articles/chatgpt-shop-at-buttons-big-box/)
- Publishers as citations: in a Sep 23, 2026 sample of 17,524 AI answers to 2,000 "best product or service" questions, 61% of ChatGPT product answers cited at least one affiliate review site (all engines: 70%; Perplexity 99.4%, Google AI Overviews 79.3%, AI Mode 70.1%, Gemini 54.7%); electronics lowest at 43.7%. Most-cited across engines: Forbes 14.4%, Wirecutter 10.3%, PCMag 9.4%. — [Suff Digital study, Sep 24, 2026](https://www.suffdigital.com/resources/data-studies/ai-product-answers-affiliate-sites)
- Tracking: from Oct 8-15, 2026 ChatGPT outbound links lacked both a referrer and the usual utm_source=chatgpt.com tag; the tag returned Oct 15 (OpenAI reportedly confirmed to Adobe's Cedric Huesler it was a bug in the GPT-6 rollout); no referrer was passed in the Oct 16 test; one store's recorded ChatGPT arrivals fell 66% in that window. — [Wislr](https://www.wislr.com/articles/chatgpt-shop-at-buttons-big-box/)

### Inferences
- There are two separate ways to win. Product cards and buttons are a merchant game (feeds, price, availability, primary-seller status). Text citations and "which products get named" are where a review publisher can appear, through OAI-SearchBot access and the "other third-party content" and "model responses generated... before it considers any new search results" inputs.
- The July 2026 move to feeds plus the concentration on the top-10 merchants means product cards increasingly point to large retailers' own listings. A publisher's page shapes the answer text but rarely gets the click on the card.
- Fulford's "pop-up ads" / "clearly advertisements" definition of low-quality sources is a risk for any page heavy with interstitials or ad clutter (relevant to a site running display ads). This is an inference from one interview quote, not a documented ranking rule.
- Because ChatGPT links stopped carrying utm_source for a week in October 2026 and pass no referrer, ChatGPT traffic is undercounted in GA4 (shows as direct) at times. Treat ChatGPT referral numbers as a floor.

### Gaps
- OpenAI publishes no criteria for which publisher pages get cited in shopping answers or shopping research beyond "trusted," "reliable" and "high-quality" sources.
- Who the "third-party providers" of product metadata are is not disclosed (the Google Shopping link is a vendor inference).
- No study found that measures how often ChatGPT shopping answers link to publisher review pages vs merchants, by click share.

## 3. Amazon and ChatGPT shopping: does ChatGPT show or link to Amazon, the Amazon-OpenAI relationship, and whether Amazon affiliate links on a cited page still earn

### Takeaway
Amazon blocks OpenAI's search, user and training crawlers on both amazon.com and amazon.ca (verified Oct 21, 2026), so ChatGPT cannot read live Amazon product pages, and Amazon is absent from OpenAI's list of feed and ACP partners. Amazon's $50B investment in OpenAI (Feb 2026) is a cloud and equity deal with no reported shopping component. Amazon is pushing its own assistant instead (Rufus, renamed Alexa for Shopping on May 13, 2026). An Amazon Associates link on a publisher page that a ChatGPT user clicks is credited the same way as any other click; nothing found says otherwise.

- No source found describing any ChatGPT shopping, catalog-sharing or crawler-access term in that deal, and the crawler blocks remain in place (above).

**Amazon's own assistant**

**Do Amazon affiliate links earn when the reader came from ChatGPT?**
- Amazon Associates credits a click on a tagged link for 24 hours; items added to the cart in that window still count if ordered before the cart expires ("usually after 90 days"); commission is credited after purchase, delivery and payment. Same rules on Amazon.ca. — [Amazon.ca Associates help (search summary)](https://associates.amazon.ca/help/node/topic/G9SMD8TQHFJ7728F)
- No source found that Amazon excludes or treats differently purchases by visitors who reached the publisher page from ChatGPT. A vendor notes Amazon's affiliate tooling "assumed a human browsing a website" — [ChatAds blog (search summary)](https://www.getchatads.com/blog/amazon-affiliate-marketing-for-ai-apps/)

### Inferences
- When ChatGPT's own product cards or buttons go to Amazon (if they do), no publisher tag is involved, so the publisher earns nothing. A publisher earns only when the reader clicks through to the publisher page and then clicks the publisher's own tagged Amazon link. That path works normally, because Associates attribution depends on the tagged click, not on where the visitor came from.
- Because ChatGPT cannot read Amazon pages, a publisher page that names a product and links to it on Amazon is one of the few ways a ChatGPT user is pointed to Amazon specifically. But ChatGPT's own buttons steer the same shopper to Walmart, Target, Best Buy or the brand, which competes directly with the publisher's Amazon link.
- Possible leakage (unmeasured): readers who copy a product name from ChatGPT and search on Amazon directly; in-app browser sessions on phones (cookies set in ChatGPT's in-app browser may not carry over if the reader switches to the Amazon app); and agent or browser automation, which Amazon blocks.

## 4. Is there any program for publishers or affiliates to earn from ChatGPT shopping directly?

### Takeaway
No. As of Oct 21, 2026 there is no OpenAI affiliate, revenue-share or commission program for publishers or review sites, for either shopping or ads. OpenAI pays only selected large publishers under content-licensing deals with undisclosed terms. Merchants pay OpenAI through ads; organic clicks carry no fee.

### Cited Findings
- Jun 2, 2026: OpenAI's VP of media partnerships Varun Shetty, asked whether OpenAI would share ad revenue with publishers whose content appears next to ads: "Not at this point." He said traffic is not the "core value" for publishers in ChatGPT search. Named licensing partners include The Washington Post, News Corp, The Guardian, Financial Times, People Inc, Schibsted, Axios, Time, Future, Hearst, Condé Nast, Vox Media, Le Monde and Axel Springer (terms not disclosed). — [Press Gazette](https://pressgazette.co.uk/platforms/openai-not-planning-to-share-advertising-revenue-with-publishers/)
- Comparison points in the same report: Perplexity shared ad revenue with publishers from late 2024 but later removed advertising; ProRata AI says it shares 50% of ad revenue with publishers. — [Press Gazette](https://pressgazette.co.uk/platforms/openai-not-planning-to-share-advertising-revenue-with-publishers/)
- Merchant page: "There are no fees on purchases that start in ChatGPT." — [chatgpt.com/merchants](https://chatgpt.com/merchants/)
- Digiday's read: OpenAI is pursuing advertising budgets rather than "taking a cut of transactions"; cost-per-action ad models are "in development." — [Digiday, May 12, 2026](https://digiday.com/media/openai-makes-it-easier-to-run-shopping-ads-in-chatgpt/)
- Affiliate-industry view (Apr 28, 2026): no OpenAI affiliate or revenue-share program confirmed; affiliate attribution is "the first casualty" when platforms compress discovery into one interface. Criteo data (Feb 2026, 500 retail clients) showed ChatGPT-referred users converting about 1.5x other referral channels (paid context). — [Affiliate Summit](https://www.affiliatesummit.com/blogs-1/chatgpt-turned-pay-per-click)
- Wislr (Oct 2026) found "no announced retailer partnership or affiliate program" behind the new "Shop at" buttons. — [Wislr](https://www.wislr.com/articles/chatgpt-shop-at-buttons-big-box/)

### Inferences
- For an affiliate site, ChatGPT's only economic value is referral traffic from citations (then the site's own affiliate links and display ads earn). Nothing is paid for being cited, and nothing is paid when ChatGPT's own cards convert a shopper who learned from the site's content.
- The ads platform gives retailers and brands a paid route into the same answers. Publishers can only buy ads like any advertiser; they get no share.

### Gaps
- Whether OpenAI will add CPA or affiliate-style payments (the "~2% Altman" figure is unsourced) or a publisher program is unknown; nothing announced.

## 5. Who benefits (merchants vs publishers/affiliate review sites), and what it means for an affiliate content site

### Takeaway
The 2025-2026 changes mostly benefit large merchants and platforms: feed and ACP partners (Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Home Depot, Shopify and Etsy sellers) get product cards, merchant listings, "Shop at" buttons and an ads channel. Publishers get citations in the answer text and some referral traffic, but no payment and a shrinking share of the click. Amazon has opted out, which removes Amazon's own listings from ChatGPT but also removes the one retailer whose affiliate program review sites rely on most.

### Cited Findings
- Merchants: "For merchants, it brings higher-intent shoppers who are closer to making a decision." — [OpenAI, Mar 24, 2026](https://openai.com/index/powering-product-discovery-in-chatgpt/); "Send shoppers to your site or app by default, with no fees." — [chatgpt.com/merchants](https://chatgpt.com/merchants/)
- Concentration: top-10 merchants' share of ChatGPT Shopping references rose from 22.5% to 41.8% after July 10, 2026; 450 of 687 tracked brands lost at least a third of their Shopping visibility. — [Search Engine Journal (Profound data)](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/chatgpt-shopping-results-lean-hard-on-product-feeds/589000/)
- Retailers' referral share: ChatGPT drove 20% of Walmart's referral clicks and over 20% of Etsy's in Aug 2025, versus under 3% of Amazon's. — [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/chatgpt-is-now-20-of-walmarts-referral-traffic-while-amazon-wards-off-ai-shopping-agents/)
- Publishers remain heavily cited: 61% of ChatGPT "best product" answers cite at least one affiliate review site (Sep 2026). — [Suff Digital](https://www.suffdigital.com/resources/data-studies/ai-product-answers-affiliate-sites)
- OpenAI positions ChatGPT against the "best of" list: when you're deciding, the web "often means jumping between tabs, reading the same 'best of' lists"; "ChatGPT solves that." — [OpenAI, Mar 24, 2026](https://openai.com/index/powering-product-discovery-in-chatgpt/)
- Shopping research says it cites sources and focuses on "organic content, including review sites and even Reddit," but avoids "sites where there are pop-up ads." — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-shopping-research/); [Digiday/Modern Retail](https://digiday.com/media-buying/openais-new-chatgpt-shopping-tool-promises-in-depth-research-just-not-for-amazon-products/)
- To be eligible for citation at all, a site must allow OAI-SearchBot; GPTBot can be blocked without affecting search inclusion. — [OpenAI crawlers overview](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)

### Inferences
- For an affiliate content site, the realistic opportunity is being a cited source (traffic, then earning through the site's own Amazon links and display ads), not product-card placement, which goes to merchants with feeds. Affiliate pages compete with ChatGPT's own buyer's guides and merchant buttons for the same click.
- For Canadian readers (amazon.ca audience), the product-card experience is officially US-only, so a Canadian ChatGPT user is more likely to get a text answer with citations. That is relatively better for a cited publisher than the US experience, where cards and "Shop at" buttons pull clicks straight to retailers. US-facing guides with amazon.com links face the full card and button experience.
- Products sold only or mainly on Amazon are the weakest spot in ChatGPT's coverage, because ChatGPT cannot read Amazon. Pages that name specific products and give clear, sourced detail may be what ChatGPT cites in their place, though no data was found to confirm this.
- Display-ad-heavy layouts (pop-ups, interstitials) could count against a page under shopping research's stated quality filter. That is a risk to weigh when choosing ad settings on pages meant to be cited. It is an inference from one OpenAI researcher's quote.
- Measurement: ChatGPT referrals may be undercounted (no referrer; utm tag missing Oct 8-15, 2026), so citation value is probably understated in analytics.

### Gaps
- No public data splits ChatGPT shopping clicks between publisher citations and merchant links, so the size of the publisher opportunity cannot be quantified from public sources.
- No Canada-specific data on ChatGPT shopping behaviour or citation patterns was found.
