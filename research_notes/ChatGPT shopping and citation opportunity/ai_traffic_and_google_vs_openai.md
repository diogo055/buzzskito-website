# AI assistant traffic to content and affiliate sites, and what is really happening between Google and OpenAI (state as of 21 Oct 2026)

Scope note: researched 21 Oct 2026. Every bullet carries the date of the data or report. Labels: **[Primary]** = the organisation that produced the data or the official document; **[Vendor]** = an SEO/analytics vendor's own panel (real data, but self-published methodology); **[Secondary]** = a write-up of someone else's data that I could not open at the source; **[Reported]** = a single outlet's reporting, not confirmed by the companies; **[Speculation]** = opinion or motive-guessing. Where a page could not be opened (403/timeout), that is stated.

## A1. How much traffic do ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot and Google's AI features send to websites, and how does that compare with Google organic?

### Takeaway
On broad 2026 panels, all AI assistants together send roughly 0.2-0.35% of website visits (Ahrefs: 0.28% in March 2026 across 74,752 sites), about 100 times less than Google search. ChatGPT supplies roughly 75-80% of those AI referrals. ChatGPT referrals jumped in May 2026 after it made brand names clickable, but about 60% of its clicks now land on homepages. In the news-heavy Chartbeat network, AI was only 0.01% of pageviews in 2026 and falling, while Google Search traffic fell 40% year on year.

### Cited Findings
- **[Vendor, Ahrefs, published 15 May 2026]** In March 2026 all AI chatbots combined sent about 3.5 million visits, or **0.28% of total traffic across 74,752 websites**. Channel mix that month: Direct 40.10%, Search 31.39%, Paid 17.17%, Social 7.34%, Unknown 5.73%, AI assistants 0.28%. By assistant (March 2026): ChatGPT about 2.7M (roughly 10x any other), Perplexity about 230K, Gemini about 230K, Claude about 102K. The April 2026 dashboard also shows Meta AI at 521.1K. Google sent 345.2M visits. The article gives Google's share inconsistently (28.12% in one place, 30.53% in another) and says it fell from 35.11% (June 2025) to 30.53% (March 2026). Only visits that carry a referrer are counted, so some chatbot clicks are missed. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-chatbot-traffic/)
- **[Vendor, Ahrefs, May 2026]** Month-on-month growth, March 2026: ChatGPT +0.4% (average +1.4% a month over the tracked period), Gemini +21.2% (average +12.8%), Claude +153.5% (average +30.7%), Perplexity average +2.9%, Meta AI -14.5%. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-chatbot-traffic/)
- **[Vendor, SE Ranking, published 9 Jul 2026]** Sample: 101,574 GA-connected websites, January 2025 to May 2026. ChatGPT's share of all website traffic worldwide rose from 0.23% (April 2026) to **0.32% (May 2026)**, up 36.7% in a month and the highest yet (previous peak 0.29%, October 2025). January to April 2026 had been flat (+1.5%). May 2026 month-on-month change: EU +42.7%, UK +38.7%, US +23.2%. The US level was about 0.22% in April 2026. ChatGPT's share of all AI referrals: 79.74% (2025) to 75.96% (2026) worldwide; US 75.78% to 72.41%. — [SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/chatgpt-referral-traffic-may-2026/)
- **[Vendor, Similarweb via SE Ranking, May 2026]** ChatGPT referral visits grew about 150% comparing the week before 7 May 2026 with the week after. Three things changed that week: brand names became clickable in answers (about 7 May), GPT-5.5 Instant became the default model (5 May), and the self-serve Ads Manager beta launched (5 May). SE Ranking calls any causal link a hypothesis. — [SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/chatgpt-referral-traffic-may-2026/)
- **[Vendor, Similarweb, byline 29 Jul 2026]** Homepages' share of ChatGPT referral traffic rose from about 26-29% before the May 2026 change to **about 62-63% by late May 2026**, and stayed there. The share of ChatGPT answers containing citations (US prompts) rose from about 1.6% (June 2025) to about 6.8% (May 2026), varying by category: Travel about 23%, Automotive about 20%, Professional Services under 4%. Similarweb publishes no sample sizes. — [Similarweb](https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/gen-ai-stats/)
- **[Vendor, SE Ranking, Jul 2026]** 60% of AI-referred traffic lands on homepages, against 17% of organic search traffic. — [SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/chatgpt-referral-traffic-may-2026/)
- **[Primary data, Statcounter, September 2026]** Share of AI chatbot traffic, worldwide: ChatGPT 80.48%, Gemini 10.91%, Perplexity 5.85%, Microsoft Copilot 1.79%, Claude 0.95%, DeepSeek 0.02%. The page does not state its methodology. — [Statcounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/ai-chatbot-market-share)
- **[Vendor, Similarweb, Jul 2026] Usage is not the same as referrals.** Gemini's share of visits *to* AI chatbot sites rose from under 9% (June 2025) to about 27-28% (May 2026), while ChatGPT's fell from about 76% to about 53%. Statcounter still puts Gemini at only about 11% of referrals *out* to websites (above). — [Similarweb](https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/gen-ai-stats/)
- **[Primary data, Chartbeat via Search Engine Journal, 17 Mar 2026]** Across "thousands" of mostly news client sites, December 2024 to December 2025: ChatGPT referrals grew more than 200%, yet chatbots were still under 1% of publisher page-view referrals. Over the same period Google Search page views fell 34% and Discover 15%. Over two years, search referrals fell 60% for small publishers (1,000-10,000 daily page views), 47% for medium (10,000-100,000) and 22% for large (over 100,000). — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/search-referral-traffic-down-60-for-small-publishers-data-shows/569959/)
- **[Primary data via secondary, Chartbeat 2026 Publisher Playbook, reported 24-25 Sep 2026]** Google Search traffic to publishers fell 40.2% year on year (July 2025 to July 2026; the prior year's fall was 21.9%) and Discover fell 34.3%. Google Search's share of pageviews went from about 9% to about 5% (July 2024 to July 2026). AI referrals grew 8x over two years but were **0.01% of traffic in 2026**, and fell in 2026 for all five providers measured (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot, Gemini). The network handles about 31.1 billion pageviews a month. — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/seo/chartbeat-google-search-publisher-traffic-down-40-percent/)
- **[Vendor, TollBit Q2 2025 report via Search Engine Land]** Google sent **831 times more visitors** than AI systems; AI apps supplied 0.102% of referrals; about 135 AI scrapes produced one human referral. — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-more-visitors-ai-systems-report-462905)
- **[Secondary, Similarweb via TechCrunch, 2 Jul 2025; search snippet, not opened]** ChatGPT referrals to news sites rose from just under 1M (January-May 2024) to over 25M (January-May 2025). Over the same period, the share of news searches ending without a click rose from 56% to nearly 69%. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/02/chatgpt-referrals-to-news-sites-are-growing-but-not-enough-to-offset-search-declines)
- **[Secondary roundup, unverified at source]** Other panels: Contentsquare puts AI at about 0.2% of traffic (Q4 2025); Conductor's enterprise panel puts it at 1.08% of sessions (US, May-September 2025). — [Mission Growth](https://missiongrowth.io/blog/ai-referral-traffic-statistics)
- **[Primary data via secondary, Cloudflare] Crawl-to-referral ratios** (pages crawled per human visit sent back). Cloudflare's own post gives June 2025 figures: Google about 14:1, OpenAI about 1,700:1, Anthropic about 73,000:1. For July 2025, a secondary report gives OpenAI 1,091:1. Secondary July 2026 readings of Cloudflare Radar give OpenAI about 251:1, Anthropic about 1,917:1 and Google about 4.7:1. SEJ found seven different Anthropic figures attributed to Cloudflare within 13 months, so treat every ratio as approximate. — [Cloudflare post (mirror)](https://tagteam.harvard.edu/hub_feeds/3997/feed_items/14511335); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/ai-crawling-data-reveals-massive-imbalance-in-training-versus-referral-patterns/); [ecorpit](https://ecorpit.com/ai-crawler-crawl-to-referral-ratio-2026-geo-decision/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/everyone-is-quoting-the-same-number-and-getting-different-answers/588659/)
- **[Vendor, Similarweb, 2026]** Google's own AI features: AI Overviews appear on "43%+" of US searches (no exact date). AI Mode "grew quickly, then leveled off and began to soften" (no figures). None of these sources measures AI Overview or AI Mode clicks as a separate referral source. — [Similarweb](https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/gen-ai-stats/)

### Inferences
- In Ahrefs' March 2026 panel, Google sent about **99 visits for every 1 from all AI assistants** (345.2M vs 3.5M), and about 128 for every 1 from ChatGPT alone. TollBit's publisher data (Q2 2025) puts the gap at 831:1. On any reasonable reading, AI referrals are a rounding error next to Google in 2026.
- **Illustration only, using the project's own planning figures (not researched here):** at the goal of about 50,000 Google visitors a month, a 1:100 ratio means at most about 500 AI-referred visits a month. At the roughly $20 blended RPM per 1,000 sessions in the project's notes, that is about $10 a month in display ads before any Amazon clicks. ChatGPT is not a material income line on volume alone.
- AI's share swings by vertical: about 1% in enterprise/B2B samples (Conductor), 0.2-0.35% in broad panels (Ahrefs, SE Ranking, Contentsquare), and 0.01% in news (Chartbeat). Chartbeat also says practical how-to sites get more pageviews per AI referral than news sites (A3). A pest-information site probably sits in the broad-panel band.
- The May 2026 shift to clickable brand names sends most ChatGPT clicks to **homepages**. For an information site, the homepage is often its least-monetized page. For BuzzSkito specifically, the homepage is a service page with ads switched off and, as far as the project notes show, no Amazon links. A homepage click from ChatGPT therefore earns nothing unless it becomes a GTA lead.
- Two trends point in opposite directions: Chartbeat (news) shows AI referrals falling in 2026, while SE Ranking (general sites) shows a May 2026 peak. AI referral volume is unstable and depends on how ChatGPT formats its links, so it is not a dependable growth channel.

### Gaps
- No public study isolates pest-control, home-and-garden or affiliate review sites.
- A secondary summary attributed to Similarweb said brands ChatGPT recommends get 2.5x more site visits within seven days, 56% of them through branded search (US desktop panel). I could not find the primary Similarweb source, so it is excluded from the findings.
- A figure that ChatGPT sends 92.4% of trackable standalone AI referrals (attributed to Previsible) came only from a search summary and is not verified.
- I found no 2026 source that reports clicks from Google AI Overviews or AI Mode separately from ordinary Google organic clicks. Sites cannot see them as their own referral source in GA4.

## A2. How often do users click a cited source in an AI answer, and how much have Google AI Overviews and AI Mode cut clicks to publishers?

### Takeaway
People rarely click sources inside AI answers. Pew measured clicks on links inside Google's AI summaries on 1% of visits, and clicks on ordinary results fell from 15% to 8% when a summary appeared. The best causal evidence, a randomized experiment with 1,065 people in January-February 2026, found AI Overviews cut outbound organic clicks by 38% where they appeared and raised zero-click searches from 54% to 72%. AI Mode is worse (about 93% zero-click in vendor data). Google says total clicks are "relatively stable" but has published no numbers.

### Cited Findings
- **[Primary, Pew Research Center, 22 Jul 2025]** 900 US adults (KnowledgePanel Digital) with tracked browsing, 1-31 March 2025: 68,879 unique Google searches, 12,593 of them with an AI summary. Users clicked a traditional result on **8%** of visits with a summary vs **15%** without, and clicked a link *inside* the summary on **1%** of visits. Sessions ended on 26% of summary pages vs 16% of others. 18% of all searches produced a summary, and 88% of summaries cited three or more sources (median 67 words). Searches starting with a question word produced a summary 60% of the time, those of 10+ words 53%, and those of 1-2 words 8%. — [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)
- **[Primary research via SEJ, published 27 Apr 2026]** Agarwal and Sen (Indian School of Business and Carnegie Mellon) ran a randomized, pre-registered field experiment through a Chrome extension: 1,065 US desktop Chrome users (recruited on Prolific), January-February 2026, two weeks each. AI Overviews appeared on 42% of queries. Where they appeared, they **cut outbound organic clicks by 38%** (0.61 clicks per search with Overviews hidden vs 0.38 with them shown), and zero-click searches rose from 54% to 72%. Satisfaction was almost identical with and without Overviews. Users sent to AI Mode clicked out less, had more zero-click searches and were less satisfied. Caveats: unreviewed SSRN draft; attrition and extension uninstalls. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-overviews-cut-organic-clicks-38-field-study-finds/573145/)
- **[Secondary, conflicting attribution]** TechWyse describes a preregistered experiment "from UPenn and Northeastern" in which AI Mode cut publisher referrals by 18.8 percentage points and raised the share of users searching on a rival engine (Bing, DuckDuckGo) by 11.2 points. It is unclear whether this is the same paper as Agarwal and Sen; SEJ's write-up of that paper does not report the rival-engine effect. — [TechWyse](https://www.techwyse.com/news/search-seo/google-ai-mode-reduces-publisher-clicks-study-2026)
- **[Vendor via secondary, Ahrefs, December 2025 data, reported February 2026]** Click-through on the top organic result was about **58% lower** when an AI Overview appeared (300,000 keywords). Ahrefs' April 2025 version found 34.5%. — [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/02/223-google-ai-overviews-click-through-rates-58-study/)
- **[Vendor via secondary, Seer Interactive; search summary of SEJ coverage, not opened]** On AI Overview queries, CTR fell 61% from Q3 to Q4 2025 while clicks on those pages barely moved, because impressions rose. The 2026 follow-up: AI Overview organic CTR recovered from a 1.3% low (December 2025) to 2.4% (February 2026). Brands cited inside an AI Overview get 35% higher organic CTR and 91% higher paid CTR than uncited brands on the same queries. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-overview-ctr-fell-61-but-clicks-didnt-collapse/572993/)
- **[Vendor via secondary]** About 93% of AI Mode searches produce no outbound click. The figure traces to a Semrush dataset (September 2025) and a Seer analysis of 25.1M impressions. — [Pasquale Pillitteri](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/811/google-ai-mode-zero-click-seo-2026-en); [Nobori](https://nobori.ai/blog/google-ai-mode-zero-click-rate-100m-users-2026)
- **[Small study cited by Ahrefs, May 2026]** In Kevin Indig's usability study (48 participants, high-stakes purchases), 64% of AI Mode users clicked nothing, and 23% clicked through, mostly to confirm a decision already made. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-chatbot-traffic/)
- **[Vendor, TollBit]** Q2 2025: AI interfaces' click-through rate was 91% below top-10 organic results. An earlier TollBit report: Google search CTR 8.63% vs 0.74% for AI search engines and 0.33% for AI chatbots. TollBit's current page: AI chatbot referrals have 96% lower CTR than traditional search. — [Search Engine Land (Q2 2025)](https://searchengineland.com/google-more-visitors-ai-systems-report-462905); [Search Engine Land (earlier)](https://searchengineland.com/ai-scraping-ai-search-traffic-report-453160); [TollBit](https://tollbit.com/analytics)
- **[Vendor, Ahrefs, May 2026]** When AI assistants mentioned Ahrefs, they linked to the site only about 10% of the time; the best case was Perplexity at 51.6%. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-chatbot-traffic/)
- **[Primary claim, Google, August 2025; contested]** In an August 2025 blog post, Google Search head Liz Reid said total organic click volume was "relatively stable" year on year and that "quality clicks" (users who do not quickly return to the results) had increased. Google published no data then, and none in later interviews. — [TechWyse](https://www.techwyse.com/news/search-news/google-liz-reid-bounce-clicks-ai-overviews-traffic-data); [Press Gazette](https://pressgazette.co.uk/platforms/google-search-clicks-traffic-2025-ai-overviews/)
- **[Primary data via secondary, Reuters Institute "Trends and Predictions 2026", January 2026; search summary, not opened]** Chartbeat data for more than 2,500 sites shows Google organic search traffic down 33% globally (November 2024 to November 2025) and Discover down 21%. In the accompanying survey of 280 media leaders, lifestyle and utility publishers were more likely to report declines. — [Press Gazette](https://pressgazette.co.uk/publishers/digital-journalism/google-traffic-down-2025-trends-report-2026/)

### Inferences
- Being cited is not being visited: about 1% of people click links inside Google's AI summaries, and Ahrefs is linked in only about 10% of AI mentions. "Getting cited by ChatGPT" will produce few visits per citation.
- Overviews are most likely on question-style and long queries (Pew: 60% and 53%), which describes most pest-ID and "how do I get rid of" searches. The informational side of a pest site is in the most exposed segment.
- Seer's cited-vs-uncited gap (+35% organic CTR) means that once an Overview shows, it is better to be cited in it than not. Inside Google this is defensive value, not new traffic.
- Small sites are hit hardest: Chartbeat's 60% two-year fall for small publishers vs 22% for large ones.

### Gaps
- I found no primary 2026 measurement of what share of ChatGPT answers lead to a click. TollBit's CTR comparisons are the only proxy.
- It is unresolved whether the "UPenn and Northeastern" AI Mode study and Agarwal and Sen's study are one paper or two.
- I found no 2026 figure for AI Mode's share of Google searches.

## A3. Do AI-referred visitors engage and convert better, especially for shopping and affiliate content?

### Takeaway
For retailers, yes, as of 2026. Adobe (over 1 trillion US retail visits) has AI-referred shoppers converting 42% better than other visitors in March 2026 and 60% better by mid-2026, up from 38% worse in March 2025, with 59% more time on site and 33% lower bounce. Software companies report much higher sign-up rates from AI visitors. But **no public data covers affiliate content sites**: how often AI visitors click through to Amazon, or what they are worth in display ads. Meanwhile ChatGPT increasingly answers shopping questions itself.

### Cited Findings
- **[Primary data via Digital Commerce 360, published 19 Aug 2026]** Adobe Analytics: AI-referred traffic to US retail sites was up 62% year on year in July 2026 and up 1,219% since October 2024. AI visits converted **60% better** than other traffic and produced 53% more revenue per visit; this was the 11th straight month AI traffic converted better. 2026 year to date (from Adobe's June 2026 data): engagement +14%, time on site +59%, bounce rate -33%, add-to-cart +28%. Basis: over 1 trillion visits to US retail sites; more than 200 of the top 2,000 online retailers use Adobe ($836B in 2025 e-commerce sales). — [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/08/19/adobe-ai-referral-traffic-data-july-2026/)
- **[Primary data via secondary, Adobe Q1 2026 report]** In March 2026, AI traffic converted 42% better than other traffic, vs 38% worse in March 2025; revenue per visit was 37% higher. AI traffic to US retailers grew 393% year on year in Q1 2026. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/ai-traffic-us-retailers-jumps-160141756.html); [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-traffic-converts-42-percent-better-2026-channel-strategy)
- **[Primary, Adobe Q3 2026 report; PDF timed out, figure from search summary]** May 2026: AI-referred retail visitors converted 54% better than other traffic. In travel the gap narrowed rather than disappeared. — [Adobe](https://business.adobe.com/resources/sdk/.q3-ai-traffic-trends-report/q3-2026-ai-sourced-traffic-insights.pdf)
- **[Vendor and self-reported, cited in Ahrefs, May 2026]** Ahrefs' own site: AI search visitors were 0.5% of visitors but 12.1% of sign-ups, a conversion rate 23x organic search. Self-reported sign-up sources, March 2026: Claude 2,836, ChatGPT 1,978, Gemini 619, Perplexity 67. Buffer (via a LinkedIn post): AI conversion rate 20.15% vs 7.06% for organic. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-chatbot-traffic/)
- **[Primary data via SEJ, Chartbeat, March 2026]** News and media sites get the most AI page views but the lowest engagement per article. Practical sites (health or gardening advice, for example) get fewer AI referrals but more page views per article. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/search-referral-traffic-down-60-for-small-publishers-data-shows/569959/)
- **[Vendor via secondary, Peec AI and Semrush]** ChatGPT's shopping carousels come mostly from Google Shopping. In about 5,000 carousels (43,000 products), over 83% of products closely matched Google's top-40 shopping results, against 11% for Bing. Semrush (100 prompts) found ChatGPT's top product in Google Shopping's top 3 in 75% of cases. (Search Engine Land returned 403; figures from the search summary.) — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/new-finding-chatgpt-sources-83-of-its-carousel-products-from-google-shopping-via-shopping-query-fan-outs-470723); [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/chatgpt-searches-google-shopping/)
- **[Vendor, Similarweb 2026 Gen AI Landscape, cited July 2026]** About 26% of ChatGPT responses contain an ad, and about a third of ad impressions appear in a session's first response. — [Similarweb](https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/gen-ai-stats/)
- **[Secondary/analyst] Instant Checkout:** launched September 2025 with a 4% merchant fee, and removed from ChatGPT on 4 March 2026 "at least for now". — [Opascope](https://opascope.com/insights/ai-shopping-assistant-guide-2026-agentic-commerce-protocols/); [Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/power-couple-openai-amazon-may-have-just-won-consumer-agentic-commerce)

### Inferences
- Adobe measures purchases on the retailer's own site. For an affiliate site, the conversion that matters is the click out to Amazon within Amazon's 24-hour cookie. AI visitors arrive with a specific question, so it is plausible they click out at a higher rate on product pages. This is untested, and Adobe's figures cannot be carried over.
- Even a doubled conversion rate on 0.3% of traffic is about 0.6% of conversions. Volume, not quality, limits what AI is worth to a small affiliate and display-ad site in 2026.
- ChatGPT is pulling shopping inside the chat: Google-Shopping-sourced carousels, ads in about a quarter of answers, and direct merchant links. That reduces the need to click through to an affiliate review page for "best X" questions. Amazon blocks OpenAI's agents (A4), so ChatGPT's carousels largely lack Amazon listings. That could, speculatively, leave room for pages that link to Amazon to be cited; no data supports or refutes this.

### Gaps
- No public data on Amazon Associates click-out rates, earnings per click, or display-ad RPM for AI-referred visitors on content or affiliate sites. Mediavine and Raptive publish nothing on this.
- The Adobe Q3 2026 PDF timed out, so the May 2026 figure (+54%) rests on a search summary.

## A4. Do ChatGPT referrals reach affiliate links and display ads like normal visitors (referrer handling, utm_source=chatgpt.com)?

### Takeaway
A person who clicks a ChatGPT citation arrives as an ordinary browser visit: ads load, and the page's Amazon links work as for anyone else. I found no report that ChatGPT strips affiliate tags. The weak point is tracking. OpenAI has added `utm_source=chatgpt.com` to links since June 2025, but many visits arrive without a referrer. From about 9 to 15 October 2026, a bug after the GPT-6 release dropped both the tag and the referrer, so ChatGPT visits were counted as "Direct". Separately, Amazon has blocked OpenAI's search and user agents since November 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **[Secondary, Search Engine Roundtable, 14 Oct 2026]** OpenAI added UTM tracking to ChatGPT links in June 2025. After the GPT-6 release (about a week before 14 October 2026), links were opened with `noopener,noreferrer` and without `utm_source`, so analytics recorded the visits as direct (Cedric Huesler, Adobe). Huesler compared about 500 pages that get at least 5% of visits from ChatGPT, 9-13 October vs the previous week: ChatGPT visits fell by about 31k a day and direct visits rose by about 26k a day, and direct rose on two-thirds of the pages that lost ChatGPT traffic. On 15 October he updated his post to say it was a bug and tagging was back. There is no statement from OpenAI. — [Search Engine Roundtable](https://seroundtable.com/utm-parameters-missing-chatgpt-42299.html)
- **[Vendor tracker, LLM Pulse, page updated 20 Oct 2026]** 98.1% of ChatGPT answers with at least one cited link had a link tagged `utm_source=chatgpt.com`. A search snippet of the same page dated 16 October showed 38.73%, consistent with the dip, though I could not see the daily series. The page says many ChatGPT visits arrive without a referrer, so untagged visits count as direct or unattributed. — [LLM Pulse](https://llmpulse.ai/data-studies/chatgpt-utm-tracking)
- **[Vendor, Seer Interactive; search snippet]** UTMs are most likely on citation links, but a citation is not guaranteed to carry one. — [Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/are-ai-sites-like-chatgpt-sending-your-website-traffic)
- **[Vendor, Ahrefs, May 2026]** AI visits normally show as referrals from the chatbot's domain (for example chatgpt.com), but some, especially from mobile apps, show as direct. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-chatbot-traffic/)
- **[Primary, OpenAI crawler documentation, read 21 Oct 2026]** OpenAI's documentation (read 21 Oct 2026) describes four agents:
  - **OAI-SearchBot** finds pages for ChatGPT search. Sites that block it are not shown in ChatGPT search answers but can still appear as navigational links. OpenAI recommends allowing it in robots.txt and allowing its published IP ranges (openai.com/searchbot.json).
  - **ChatGPT-User** acts on a user's request. robots.txt "may not apply" to it, and it does not decide what appears in search.
  - **GPTBot** collects training data. Each setting is independent, so blocking GPTBot does not affect search.
  - **OAI-AdsBot** checks the landing pages of ads in ChatGPT.
  - Search-related robots.txt changes take about 24 hours to apply.
  — [OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)
- **[Secondary, Seller Labs, 10 Dec 2025]** ChatGPT Shopping Research launched on 24 November 2025, and that month Amazon blocked ChatGPT-User and OAI-SearchBot, so ChatGPT cannot use Amazon listings for recommendations. — [Seller Labs](https://www.sellerlabs.com/blog/amazon-blocked-chatgpt-shopping/)
- **[Reported, CNBC, 20 Mar 2026; search summary]** Amazon was still blocking dozens of AI agents, ChatGPT among them, despite its February 2026 strategic partnership with OpenAI (Amazon investing up to $50B). — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/20/open-ai-agentic-shopping-etsy-shopify-walmart-amazon.html)
- **[Secondary, citing The Verge, October 2026]** Amazon confirmed it keeps blocking AI shopping agents, including OpenAI's. — [Pasquale Pillitteri](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/25165/amazon-yelp-delta-jpmorgan-schwab-block-ai-agents)

### Inferences
- Amazon Associates pays on the click from the site's own tagged link. Where the visitor came from (ChatGPT, Google or direct) does not matter to Amazon, so a person sent by ChatGPT who clicks an Amazon button on the page earns the same as any other visitor. This follows from how the program works; I found no test of it.
- Display ads do not depend on the referrer, so ChatGPT visitors without one still earn ad revenue. Only attribution suffers: some ChatGPT value will show as "Direct" in GA4 and in Mediavine reporting, especially around ChatGPT releases.
- Site-specific (from the project notes, not researched here): BuzzSkito's robots.txt allows OAI-SearchBot and ChatGPT-User and blocks GPTBot. Under OpenAI's documentation, that keeps the site eligible for ChatGPT search while opting out of training.

### Gaps
- I found no reporting either way on whether ChatGPT strips or rewrites affiliate tags in links it cites. The search returned only tag-remover browser extensions.
- I found no data on whether purchases survive Amazon attribution when the click happens in ChatGPT's mobile in-app browser and the purchase is finished in the Amazon app.
- I found no data on how ad networks (Mediavine, Google Ad Manager) classify visits from AI agents (ChatGPT agent mode, Atlas agent browsing), for example as invalid traffic.

## B1. What has Google actually done that affects OpenAI, what has OpenAI done in response, and which reports are confirmed and which are rumor?

### Takeaway
**Confirmed:** Google has made scraping Google harder and more expensive: SearchGuard and the JavaScript requirement (January 2025), and disabling `num=100` (September 2025). It turned down OpenAI's 2024 request for access to its index, and in December 2025 it sued SerpApi, the scraper The Information reported OpenAI used for Google results. **Not confirmed:** that any of this targets OpenAI. Google has never named OpenAI, the core of the SerpApi case was dismissed on 20 July 2026 (an amended complaint is pending), and in September 2026 ChatGPT still appeared to draw on Google data through scraping vendors. The real "squeeze" is competitive: Gemini's share of AI chatbot use roughly tripled in a year and Apple chose Gemini for Siri. "Google is massively pushing out OpenAI" overstates it: a court has ordered Google to share index data with qualified rivals (under appeal), and on 21 October 2026 Google Cloud agreed to sell OpenAI's models.

### Inferences
- **Verdict on the rumor (as of 21 Oct 2026):** "Google is massively pushing out OpenAI" is **true as competition, unproven as targeted action, and false as total exclusion.**
  - **Competition:** Gemini's share of chatbot use roughly tripled in a year, helped by Android, Workspace and Search distribution and the Apple deal.
  - **Technical:** Google's anti-scraping steps raise the cost of using Google data for anyone who scrapes, including OpenAI's suppliers.
  - **Not targeted, as far as the record shows:** no Google action names OpenAI, and the lawsuit's main theory failed in July 2026.
  - **Not excluded:** ChatGPT still appears to use Google-derived results through vendors, and Google has just agreed to resell OpenAI's models on Google Cloud.
- For a small publisher, the practical effect is indirect. ChatGPT's source pool mixes Google-derived results, Bing, and OpenAI's own crawl. Ranking well on Google still feeds ChatGPT citations, and nothing in this dispute changes that yet.
- One risk to watch: if Google's amended claims (built on licensing agreements) eventually win, or SearchGuard keeps tightening, ChatGPT may lean more on its own index and on Bing/Web IQ. Being crawlable by OAI-SearchBot and indexed in Bing would then matter more.

### Gaps
- OpenAI has never officially confirmed using SerpApi or Google-derived results. How much of ChatGPT's retrieval comes from each source is unknown.
- The Google v. SerpApi docket after 1 September 2026 was not verified; the latest status rests on one secondary source (Crawlraven).
- I found no information on whether OpenAI has applied for, or been designated, a "qualified competitor" under the antitrust remedies. The Congressional Research Service summary returned 403, so the exact remedy terms come from secondary sources.
- I found no reliable source for any Google action aimed specifically at OpenAI's crawlers, or for a change in Google Shopping data access aimed at OpenAI, beyond the SerpApi suit (whose Shopping claims were dropped in August 2026).

## B2. Does ChatGPT search rely on Bing's index, and does a site's Bing indexing matter for being cited?

### Takeaway
Bing is a documented but shrinking part of how ChatGPT finds pages. Early 2025 data pointed strongly to Bing (Seer: 87% of SearchGPT citations were in Bing's top 20). Larger replications in 2025-2026 find low overlap with any single engine's top results. September 2026 network analysis shows ChatGPT mixing OpenAI's own index ("Labrador") with results from the scraping vendors Bright Data and Oxylabs and a SERP vendor, with Bing seen mainly in Deep Research. Being indexed in Bing is still worth having (Copilot, Deep Research, Microsoft's Web IQ), but the essentials for ChatGPT search are allowing OAI-SearchBot and ranking well on Google too.

### Cited Findings
- **[Secondary summaries of OpenAI's help text]** OpenAI says ChatGPT search "sometimes partners with other search providers", pointing to Microsoft's and Shopify's privacy documents, and never states the shares. — [eSEOspace](https://eseospace.com/blog/chatgpt-search-explained/)
- **[Vendor, Seer Interactive, February 2025]** 100 queries and 500+ citations: 87% of SearchGPT citations matched Bing's top results (top 20) vs 56% for Google. — [Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/87-percent-of-searchgpt-citations-match-bings-top-results)
- **[Vendor replication, 400 queries]** Bing results for the original question matched 3.0% of citations; Bing results including ChatGPT's fan-out sub-queries matched 27.4%; Google results for the original question matched 13.4%; 68% matched neither engine. — [AI Plus Automation](https://aiplusautomation.com/blog/bing-replication-chatgpt-citations)
- **[Vendor, Ahrefs]** Across 15,000 prompts, only 12% of URLs cited by ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot ranked in Google's top 10 for the prompt; overlap with Bing's top 10 was about 10%. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-overlap/)
- **[Vendor investigation, Peec AI, 4 Sep 2026]** Findings:
  - Bing appears mainly in Deep Research.
  - Microsoft's Web IQ lists ChatGPT among its users, so some Bing-looking retrieval may go through Web IQ.
  - A Search Console test indicates ChatGPT still queries Google.
  - ChatGPT serves cached copies of pages.
  - Peec's advice: stop treating Bing visibility as a stand-in for ChatGPT visibility.
  - No sample sizes are given.
  — [Peec AI](https://peec.ai/blog/chatgpt-built-its-own-search-index)
- **[Secondary, unverified]** A June 2026 study reportedly found every logged Deep Research search ran on Bing. — [Lighthouse Local](https://www.lighthouselocal.ai/blog/does-chatgpt-use-google-or-bing)
- **[Confirmed product, June 2026]** Web IQ is a grounding API built on the Bing index; coverage says ChatGPT uses this infrastructure for parts of its answers. — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/microsoft-releases-web-iq-powered-by-bing-but-designed-for-how-ai-agents-search-479194); [TechWyse](https://www.techwyse.com/news/platform-updates/microsoft-web-iq-grounding-api-ai-agents)
- **[Primary product docs via Adobe and Practical Ecommerce, 2026]** Bing Webmaster Tools' AI Performance report (public preview, free for any verified site):
  - **Coverage:** Copilot, Bing's AI summaries and some unnamed partners, but not ChatGPT.
  - **Metrics:** total citations, cited pages and "grounding queries".
  - **Export:** CSV only (no API yet).

  ChatGPT shares citation metrics only with publishers that license content to OpenAI. — [Adobe Experience League](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/brand-visibility-learn/tutorials/measurement/bing-webmaster-tools-ai-performance-report); [Practical Ecommerce](https://www.practicalecommerce.com/bings-improved-ai-performance-section)
- **[Primary, OpenAI]** ChatGPT search inclusion depends on allowing OAI-SearchBot (robots.txt and IP ranges); GPTBot is separate. — [OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)
- **[Vendor via secondary]** ChatGPT shopping carousels match Google Shopping (83%) far more than Bing (11%). — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/new-finding-chatgpt-sources-83-of-its-carousel-products-from-google-shopping-via-shopping-query-fan-outs-470723)

### Inferences
- Bing indexing helps but does not decide ChatGPT citations. ChatGPT draws on several sources (its own index, Bing/Web IQ, and Google-derived results from vendors), and the overlap studies show citations rarely match any single engine's top 10.
- Site-specific (from the project notes, not researched here): Bing has been refusing BuzzSkito's IndexNow submissions since September 2026 (403 "UserForbiddedToAccessSite", likely lapsed ownership in Bing Webmaster Tools). Re-verifying the site in Bing Webmaster Tools is cheap insurance for Copilot, Deep Research and Web IQ-grounded answers. It also unlocks Bing's AI Performance report, the only free first-party AI-citation report a small site can get.
- The core requirements for ChatGPT search are unchanged: OAI-SearchBot can crawl the site (it is allowed in the project's robots.txt), and the pages rank well on Google.

### Gaps
- OpenAI has made no official 2026 statement on how much each source contributes, and Peec's reading of "Labrador" as OpenAI's index is not confirmed by OpenAI.
- I could not open OpenAI's help-center page on "offline web search" (April 2026).
- I found no controlled test showing that dropping out of Bing's index removes a site from ChatGPT's citations, or that being in Bing's index adds it.
