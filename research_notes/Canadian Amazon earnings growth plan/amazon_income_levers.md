## Objective: the levers ranked, with cost, likely effect, time to work and whether this site can do it

### Takeaway
I found no documented case of anyone earning CA$10,000 a month from amazon.ca, in any niche, and no independent publisher anywhere at CA$100,000. The best evidence-backed levers are earned links from real data, long-form YouTube by the licensed technician, and a small batch of real product testing that feeds both. Together they plausibly reach about CA$1,500-4,000 in peak months by 2028 (my estimate), not five figures.

### Cited Findings
- Site baseline from the brief: CA$420-670 a month, CA$0.225 per Amazon click, about 10 cents per Canadian product-page view (up to about 46 cents on the best pages), 92% of items ordered are not the linked product.
- Amazon report for the 30 days to Sep 15, 2026: 3,126 clicks, 319 items, CA$12,891 ordered, CA$567.86 earned, a 4.4% blended rate; 316 of 319 items were not the linked product. [P, site's own report] `reference_amazon_indirect_earnings.md`
- This site's click-through by position, Sep 28 to Oct 25, 2026: Canadian buying or fixing searches get 3.0% at positions 1-3 and 0.5% at positions 7-10. [P, site's own GSC] `project_upgrade_effect_read.md`
- If all 126 not-yet-upgraded pages at positions 4-15 reached about position 3, the gain would be about 1,370 clicks per 28 days at October demand. [P, site's own analysis] `project_upgrade_effect_read.md`
- Earlier ceiling analysis: a realistic peak-season ceiling of $2,200-3,000 a month with 1,000 pages, upside $4,000-5,000 (currency not stated; probably CAD), and "Canadian search volume caps out first"; the US market is about 8.5x Canada on the keywords checked. [P, site's own analysis, Jul 2026] `project_affiliate_ceiling_analysis.md`
- Supporting evidence for each row is in the sections below.

## Is CA$10,000-100,000 a month realistic? Who has documented US$/CA$10,000+ from Amazon?

### Takeaway
A small number of US publishers and video creators report US$10,000+, but the site cases date from before the 2020 and 2026 rate cuts, some leaned on tactics Google now calls spam, and the video cases mostly come with something to sell. None is amazon.ca or pest control. The largest public pest-niche listing I found is about US$1,000-1,500 a month, and at this site's rates CA$10,000 needs about 44,000 Amazon clicks a month.

### Inferences
- **The arithmetic (my calculation):** CA$10,000 at CA$0.225 per click is about 44,400 Amazon clicks a month, against about 1,870-2,980 now, so 15-24x. At 10 cents a view that is 100,000 Canadian product-page views a month (21,700 at the 46-cent best). CA$100,000 is 444,000 clicks or 1,000,000 views, 149-238x. At the 4.4% rate, CA$10,000 means about CA$227,000 ordered a month (about CA$2.7M a year, about 5,600 items); CA$100,000 means about CA$27M a year.
- **Verdict on CA$10,000 from amazon.ca alone:** no evidence for it, and the site's own ceiling work says Canadian demand runs out first. I would not call it impossible over many years if a YouTube channel broke out, but nothing I found shows it happening.
- **Verdict on CA$100,000:** no independent publisher in the evidence. Only corporate publishers operate at that level, for example Ziff Davis at about US$90M of affiliate commissions tied to organic traffic in 2025 ([PA API Plugin citing its earnings call](https://paapiplugin.com/ai-search-amazon-affiliate-traffic-2026/), [T]).
- **Conversion is not the weak point.** CA$0.225 per click and about CA$100 per 1,000 Canadian product-page views sit at or above the thin benchmarks, including about US$34.50 per 1,000 all-page views at the Amazon-only pest site (different denominator, so a rough comparison). The constraint is the number of Canadian buyers reaching these pages.
- **The documented five-figure sites do not transfer.** They targeted large US head terms and pricier products (up to US$2,500 on Blears' site, about US$100 on average on Cunnington's earlier one), before Amazon cut furniture and home improvement from 8% to 3% in April 2020 ([Geniuslink](https://geniuslink.com/blog/?p=2380), [T]). Blears' early links were PBNs, which Google's spam policies rule out.
- **Opportunity cost of "forgetting everything else":** CA$120,000 a year from Amazon equals about 131 extra Standard Season customers at about CA$914 each ($994 less 10 visits at about $8 product, per CLAUDE.md). One such customer is worth about 1.5-2 months of today's Amazon income. Capacity limits apply, but this is a reason to be sceptical of dropping the service side.

### Gaps
- No audited or Amazon-published income data exists for any case. No Canadian site income report was found. Amazon does not publish Canadian net sales.
- A search excerpt attributes "an average of 18 months to reach US$5,000 a month" to a survey of 100+ influencers via Logie, but [the Logie page I opened](https://logie.ai/news/?p=3953) did not contain it, so I do not rely on it.

## Amazon Influencer Program and onsite commissions

### Takeaway
It appears open in Canada and can earn with no traffic from the site, but since April 14, 2026 onsite pays only on the exact product, at low rates. US creators report about US$1 per video per month on average. There is almost no data for amazon.ca and none for pest control. It merits a bounded test using footage from real work, not a plan to reach CA$10,000.

### Cited Findings
- **Canada:** Amazon.ca supports Canadian participants and pays in CAD on purchases through Canadian links; you need an active YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok account, and Amazon reviews followers and engagement. [T] [Money.ca via Yahoo, May 2026](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/one-construction-worker-earns-10-103000948.html)
- Amazon's Canadian help centre has an [Onsite Associates Program Commission Income Statement](https://associates.amazon.ca/help/node/topic/G4ARBJC7Z2NK48CA) with a fixed-rate "Table A" limited to Direct Qualifying Purchases and excluding Bounty Events, and a [page on availability outside Canada](https://associates.amazon.ca/help/node/topic/GZRTENJNR89PB6DY). [P, excerpt only; rate values did not show]
- A YouTube video on the Canadian programme, from about April 2024, says onsite commissions had started in Canada and asks whether uploading to .ca is worth it. I could not read its contents. [T, excerpt only] [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8lYHUx5Xv6A)
- Country lists conflict, and onsite Store IDs are created per locale, so earnings are tracked separately by country. [P, excerpt only] [Amazon help](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GV7VUCCSS8MQFSQT)
- **Rule change:** effective April 14, 2026, onsite commission applies "only to Direct Qualifying Purchases" of the same ASIN variant as the linked page; earlier, onsite also earned on other items. Offsite links were not changed. [T quoting Amazon] [Mentionlink, Apr 30, 2026](https://mentionlink.com/blog/2026/04/30/amazon-associates-april-2026-update/); [Aff Rate](https://affrate.com/news-analysis/amazon-associates-2026-what-changed-affiliates/)
- A secondary source says onsite placement review now needs at least 3 qualifying sales within 180 days of joining, excluding paid traffic, and that AI-generated people must be flagged. [T, single blog] [Panstag, Aug 2026](https://www.panstag.com/2026/08/amazon-influencer-program-rule-changes.html)
- **Rates (US):** onsite about 1%-4% ([Aveoreach](https://aveoreach.com/blog/amazon-influencer-program-guide-2026), vendor); 1.25% vs 4.5% offsite in one example ([Sozee](https://www.sozee.ai/resources/amazon-influencer-program-commission-rates/), vendor). [T]

[O]
- **Earnings per video (US):** "most videos make about $1/month on average", best of a first three about US$18 a month, a wallet review over US$100 in five months. [O] [Entreresource](https://entreresource.com/amazon-influencer-program/) A 2024 Reddit thread suggests an average of about US$300 (C$410) a month. [T] [Money.ca via Yahoo](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/one-construction-worker-earns-10-103000948.html)
- **Equipment:** iPhone, tripod with Bluetooth button; optional ring light, lavalier mic, CapCut. Approval took him 2 business days; approval is said to be possible under 1,000 followers though 1,000 is the "baseline"; three attempts. [O] [Entreresource](https://entreresource.com/amazon-influencer-program/)

[O]

### Inferences
- At US$1 per video per month, CA$10,000 is about 7,300 videos. Two independent self-reports line up with this: about 10,000 videos for about US$10,000 in the Peterson family's best month. That average predates the same-product rule.
- On a CA$40 item (this site's average, CA$12,891 over 319 items) at an assumed 2-4% rate, each onsite sale pays about CA$0.80-1.60, so CA$10,000 is about 6,200-12,300 exact-product sales a month.
- Fewer creators may upload to .ca, which could help (the Vancouver claim hints at it), but the store is also much smaller. My low-confidence estimate is CA$50-500 a month at 100-300 videos.
- Site fit: Facebook has about 30 followers (`reference_social_distribution_reality.md`), so build a YouTube or Instagram profile first, and film the first three videos carefully because rejections are limited. Upload only products the technician has really used, re-using test footage.
- A bounded test of about 30 videos over one winter would answer the amazon.ca question for little money. Extending beyond that should depend on measured earnings per video.

### Gaps
- Canada's onsite rate table; any amazon.ca per-video data; how crowded pest products are on amazon.ca; whether one creator can upload to both .ca and .com from one account. I found nothing on pest control as a low-competition category.

## Creator Connections, bonuses and bounties

### Takeaway
Creator Connections appears to be US-only, which fits the site's .com report showing the line and the .ca report not showing it. On amazon.ca the bonus route is fixed bounties, which are small.

### Cited Findings
- Brands must "Advertise in the United States through an Amazon Ads account based in the US, China, or Hong Kong"; the guide does not mention Canada. Minimum bonus 10% of net product price, often 20-30%+; creators need Bronze or higher in Creator Stars and must take part in the Influencer and Associates programmes. [T] [Hive, Oct 17, 2026](https://www.hivehq.ai/blog/amazon-creator-connections)
- A creator's guide says it is "limited to the US marketplace (Amazon.com)". [O] [Entreresource](https://entreresource.com/amazon-influencer-program/) Another review says an Associates or Influencer account is enough, and brands' budgets start at US$5,000 (conflicts with Hive on eligibility). [T] [Creator Marketplaces](https://creatormarketplaces.com/platforms/amazon-creator-connections/)
- Amazon's own December 2022 guide called it invite-only. [P, excerpt only] [Amazon PDF](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/Associates/Marketing/Creator_Connections_Creator_Guide_Confidential_December2022.pdf)
- **Canadian bounties:** C$3.00 for free-trial sign-ups such as Prime and Music Unlimited, and C$5.00 for a Baby Registry with at least one item. [P, excerpt only] [Associates Canada](https://associates.amazon.ca/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ) Bounty events do not count for onsite. [P, excerpt only] [Onsite statement](https://associates.amazon.ca/help/node/topic/G4ARBJC7Z2NK48CA)
- Milestone bonuses that rewarded high-performing publishers were eliminated in the US in 2026. [T] [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/amazon-associates-affiliate-rate-cuts-publishers/)

### Inferences
- For amazon.ca, treat Creator Connections as not available. On amazon.com it could add a bonus, but it is product-specific, while the site's US pick boxes are generic types and never a brand (CLAUDE.md), so using it would mean changing that rule.
- A plain Prime trial link on top pages before Prime Day and Black Friday costs nothing. 30 trials a month would be CA$90. Do not expect more.

### Gaps
- I found no Amazon statement on Creator Connections in Canada, and the current amazon.ca bounty list could not be read in full.

## Hands-on product testing

### Takeaway
Google asks for first-hand evidence but sets no required process. The only measured support is correlational, and HouseFresh shows that real testing does not protect a site. Test a small batch for the value it adds to links, video and photos rather than for the direct earnings.

### Cited Findings
- For "best" picks Google says to explain why "with first-hand supporting evidence", suggests visuals, audio or links to your own experience, and suggests "links to multiple sellers"; no specific testing process is required. [P] [Google, updated Dec 10, 2025](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews)
- The reviews system "primarily evaluates review content on a page-level basis", can look at the whole site if it has many reviews, is "improved at a regular and ongoing pace", and affected content "may recover over time". [P] [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/reviews-system)
- Across 50 sites (Aug-Dec 2023), first-hand experience correlated +0.333 with traffic change (p=0.018) and stock images -0.403. The author says "very small sample" and these are "not ranking factors". [P] [Zyppy](https://zyppy.com/seo/google-update-case-study/)
- Across 400+ sites around the March 2026 core update: offers a product or service 0.391 (70.2% of winners vs 34.6% of losers), allows task completion 0.381, proprietary assets 0.357 (92.9% vs 57.1%). Correlations on third-party traffic estimates. [T] [Scalevise on Zyppy Signal](https://scalevise.com/resources/proprietary-assets-google-march-2026-core-update/)
- **Counterexample:** HouseFresh buys every unit it reviews and still lost nearly all search traffic after the March 2024 core update; partial recovery from Aug 2024, not back to earlier levels. [O] [Interview](https://the-unscripted-seo-interview-podcast.castos.com/episodes/gisele-navarro-on-rebuilding-housefresh-after-google-reddit-ai-bots-and-the-2-minute-visitor) It claims update rollouts generally favour testers; that is its own experience. [O] [HouseFresh](https://housefresh.com/david-vs-digital-goliaths/)
- **In-house staff vs freelancers:** Wirecutter and The Strategist barely changed in Jan 2025 while Forbes Advisor fell 83%. [T] [WSJ](https://itiger.com/news/2514696804) Later Ahrefs estimates show both falling (15.9M to 8.04M; 3.82M to 662K), attributed to AI Overviews by the author. [T] [Growtika](https://growtika.com/blog/publisher-affiliate-collapse)
- **This site's upgrades (not testing):** after upgrading 28 pages, no top-3 gain; median position moved +0.1; clicks +17% vs -16% for controls, half of the gain from one page. [P, site's own] `project_upgrade_effect_read.md`
- Which product is linked barely affects what sells here: tested picks would not change the 92%. [P, site's own] `reference_amazon_indirect_earnings.md`
- Google Images plus Lens brings about 150 clicks a week, and the site's research says unique original photos are what win there. [P, site note] CLAUDE.md

### Inferences
- **Cost for 30 products (my estimate; no published benchmark found):** products about CA$1,200 at the CA$40 average, more for traps and foggers; 120-240 technician hours at CA$21 an hour (CA$170 a day) is CA$2,550-5,100; plus 30-60 owner hours of write-up. About CA$4,000-8,000. Start with 10.
- **Direct return is small:** if those 30 pages earn half of today's income, a 10-30% lift is about CA$20-100 a month. It pays back only through what it enables: citable results for press, videos, original photos and Influencer uploads.
- **What fits:** mosquito and tick products from May to September 2027, the company's real trade. Tick is already the best-earning cluster at 4.6x. Pests the company does not treat are hard to test honestly.
- **What real testing requires:** a stated method, dates, photos and results, including products that fail. Only registered products used to the label (see Risks). Never add a testing claim to an untested page; the build guard and the owner's rule both forbid it.

### Gaps
- No controlled study isolates first-hand testing as a cause of ranking gains. No cost-per-review figure exists in the sources I found. Nothing shows whether Google can tell real testing from claimed testing.

## Backlinks and digital PR

### Takeaway
Links correlate with top positions and this site has almost none, so this is the largest ranking lever, but measured before-and-after evidence is weak. Paying for links does not make sense at this income: an average agency contract costs more than a year of current Amazon earnings. Earned coverage from the site's own data is the safe route.

### Cited Findings
- The #1 result has 3.8x more backlinks than positions 2-10; Backlinko calls this correlation. [P] [Backlinko, updated Apr 2025](https://backlinko.com/search-engine-ranking)
- Top-10 URLs average 112 referring domains, but the median is 6 (Semrush 2024, 16,298 keywords). [T via a link seller] [Rhino Rank](https://www.rhinorank.io/blog/link-building-statistics/) 25.3% of top-10 pages have no referring domains. [T, excerpt only] [Rankings.io](https://rankings.io/?p=3473) A different study says 96% of top-10 sites have 1,000+ linking domains at site level, over 200 commercial phrases. [T] [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/link-importance-google-top-10-study-443024)
- **Timing:** about 10 weeks per rank jump (Moz, 2016, 76 links); 3.1 months average (Authority Hacker 2022, 755 link builders); 38% of effective links peak in weeks 4-8 and 6% do nothing after six months (Andy Zhao, Apr 2026). [T] [Rhino Rank](https://www.rhinorank.io/blog/link-building-statistics/)
- **Before-and-after cases are all small:** a page moved #22 to #9 in 11 days from one link; a page at #34 showed nothing a month after six links. [O, via Rhino Rank] [Same](https://www.rhinorank.io/blog/link-building-statistics/) In a two-page test, links from pages with their own backlinks gave lasting gains while high-DR links without that faded. [O] [Isola, May 2026](https://joshuaisola.co.uk/link-qualification-test/)
- **Cost:** survey average US$597 per link and US$5,458 a month; US$350 US, US$461 "other", US$818 UK; journalist requests US$400, hero content US$800; 75% of contracts over six months; about 70 respondents, 54% UK, Canada not separated. [P survey] [BuzzStream](https://www.buzzstream.com/blog/digital-pr-costs/) Pricing of US$1,250-1,500 per unique PR link; about US$370 per link for one in-house case. [T] [Rhino Rank](https://www.rhinorank.io/blog/link-building-statistics/)
- **Canadian example:** a data study programme produced 34+ links with coverage in the Ottawa Sun and Vancouver is Awesome; no cost or ranking results stated. [O, agency] [Cedarwood](https://cedarwood.digital/case-study-digital-pr-casinobonusca-canadian-link-building/)
- **Google's rules:** spam includes "Buying or selling links for ranking purposes", advertorials "where payment is received for articles that include links that pass ranking credit", and "links with optimized anchor text in articles, guest posts, or press releases distributed on other sites". Paid links are fine with `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"`, which pass no ranking value. [P] [Google, updated Aug 28, 2026](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)
- **Site baseline:** DR 0.5, 86 backlinks, 83 referring domains (Jul 2026). [P, site's own] `project_affiliate_ceiling_analysis.md` Five data hubs shipped in June 2026; a note says press "already drove a 3.2x jump once" (not re-verified). `project_linkable_asset_hubs.md`

### Inferences
- **Value of the prize:** the site's CTR is 6x higher at positions 1-3 than 7-10 on Canadian buying searches. If all 126 pages reached about #3 at October demand, about 1,470 extra clicks per 30 days at 10-46 cents is about CA$150-675. Peak season could be 2-4x (weak evidence). A partial win gives +CA$100-900.
- **Paid links fail the arithmetic:** 30 pages x 3 links x US$597 is about US$53,700 (about CA$73,600), or 9-15 years of current Amazon income. One average contract month (about CA$7,500) equals 11-18 months of income. So do it yourself and put money into data and tests, not placements.
- **Safe methods for this site:** seasonal data pitches (tick and West Nile) to Canadian outlets, expert quotes from a licensed technician, citable test results. Target links from pages that themselves have links, on the small evidence above. Do not assume links to the data hubs pass value down to product pages: project memory found internal link counts do not predict rank here once controlled (`project_internal_linking_research.md`), so some coverage should point directly at the best product pages or test results.
- **Unsafe methods:** paid guest posts, link insertions, PBNs, optimised press releases. The older five-figure Amazon sites used some of these ([Blears](https://www.nichepursuits.com/?p=10198), [Empire Flippers case](https://empireflippers.com/1-9k-per-month-site-make-51k-in-10-days/)), so their results are not a guide.

### Gaps
- No measured before-and-after study for affiliate product pages moving from positions 6-12 into the top 3. No Canadian price data. The Oct 2026 referring-domain count was not re-checked because Ahrefs is cancelled.

- Both ad density and affiliates showed negative correlations in one small study: fixed footer ad -0.522, number of ads about -0.37, affiliate links -0.248 (not significant at its own threshold). [P, 50 sites, 2023] [Zyppy](https://zyppy.com/seo/google-update-case-study/)

### Inferences
- If Amazon extended the same-product rule to standard links, this site could lose roughly 90% of its items (92% are not the linked product). No source predicts this, but Amazon has already done it for onsite. This is the strongest reason not to bet everything on one programme.
- A 50% rate cut, if it reached Canada, would halve income. The reported cuts mainly hit premium rates and top-tier bonuses, so a small publisher at standard rates may be less exposed, but the same facts mean scale would not bring better terms.
- AI risk is real but less clear for this site's buying pages than for question pages: the only measured transactional result found no clear effect, on a small sample. "Best X" pages are the exposed group, and no one has measured their click loss.
- Testing does not protect against any of these on its own. In-house publishers held up better in the 2024-25 enforcement wave, but Ahrefs estimates suggest they later lost traffic too, and HouseFresh tested everything and was hit anyway.
- Linking to an unregistered pesticide could damage a licensed business. Checking the registration number on every pesticide pick is cheap and rare among affiliate competitors.

### Gaps
- Whether the 2026 cuts reached Canada is unknown; the site's account dates only from July 2026, so it has no before-and-after. No Amazon Rufus effect on affiliate clicks was researched. No Canadian measurement of AI Overview click loss.

## What else has real evidence behind it?

### Takeaway
A few further ideas have some evidence: Canadian retailers beside Amazon, buying a site, brand-funded links, and checking whether Mediavine ads hurt Amazon clicks on the best pages. Most of it is vendor data or seller claims, so treat them as small tests rather than plans.

### Cited Findings
- **Multiple sellers:** in a two-month vendor A/B test, Choice Pages more than doubled earnings per click on average, with one client up 2.6x; about 78% of shoppers comparison-shop. [T, vendor] [Geniuslink, May 2026](https://geniuslink.com/blog/?p=2380) Google also suggests "links to multiple sellers". [P] [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews) "thermacell canadian tire" had 7,330 impressions and 6 clicks here. [P, site's own] `project_affiliate_ceiling_analysis.md`
- **Buying a site:** US$110,295 for US$4,085 a month ([#40726](https://empireflippers.com/listing/40726/)); US$11,000 for US$428 a month ([ThePestInformer](https://flippa.com/12824340)). The buyer sets a new tracking ID and swaps links, and sales dip for days. [O] [Empire Flippers](https://empireflippers.com/1-9k-per-month-site-make-51k-in-10-days/) Buying and repurposing expired domains to manipulate rankings is spam. [P] [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)
- **Brand-funded links:** Levanta claims 3-8x Associates rates and a 14-day window, and links must not carry an Associates tag since Dec 20, 2024. [T, vendor] [Levanta](https://knowledge.levanta.io/amazon-associates-policy-update-faq) "You don't get paid for other items in the person's Amazon cart"; about 20% average. [T] [Making Sense of Cents](https://www.makingsenseofcents.com/2025/06/levanta-review.html) Levanta says it serves Canada. [T, vendor, excerpt only] [Levanta](https://levanta.io/levanta-expands-to-france-and-germany-driving-global-growth-for-amazon-sellers) Stacking on one click is not allowed. [T] [Geniuslink](https://geniuslink.com/blog/?p=2380)
- **Ad load:** the negative ad correlations above ([Zyppy](https://zyppy.com/seo/google-update-case-study/)) and the site's Mediavine estimate of about CA$20 per 1,000 sessions in Nov-Dec. [P, site note] `project_mediavine_rpm_estimate.md`
- **App links:** a blogger's 1.3% to 3.8% conversion and 41% more commission, 200-450% vendor claims; no independent data. [O, vendor] [urlgenius](https://app.urlgeni.us/blog/affiliate-publisher-how-to-triple-conversion-with-amazon-app-deep-linking); [Linktw.in](https://linktw.in/amazon-affiliates) Redirects that hide the Amazon URL are not allowed. [T] [UseBravery](https://usebravery.com/blog/amazon-associates-rules-complete-2026-guide)

[O] [Human Proof Designs](https://www.humanproofdesigns.com/increasing-amazon-affiliate-commissions-by-300-case-study/) The site has already done this (top box).
- **AI referrals:** AI-referred retail visits converted 42% better in Mar 2026 after 38% worse a year earlier, but they are a low single-digit share of most sites' traffic. [T, vendor] [PA API Plugin](https://paapiplugin.com/ai-search-amazon-affiliate-traffic-2026/)

### Inferences
- **Multiple sellers:** given Buy-Canadian sentiment and that query data, try a named second retailer on 5 product pages and measure total income per view. This is not amazon.ca income, and it could reduce Amazon sessions, so it needs a real comparison.
- **Buying a site:** CA$10,000 a month (about US$7,300) would cost about US$175,000-263,000 at 2-3x annual profit if such a site existed. Listed pest sites are mostly US traffic, so they would raise amazon.com and ad income, not amazon.ca. It buys income immediately but not the goal the owner named.
- **Levanta:** at 4.4% on all items versus about 20% on the linked product only, the break-even share of linked-product items is about 22%. This site is at about 8%, so it would lose money on most pages. A longer window helps only partly. It makes sense only on pages where most buyers take the linked product, which GA4 and the linked-product report can show.
- **Ads vs Amazon on the best product pages:** a product page view earns about CA$100 per 1,000 from Amazon against about CA$20 per 1,000 sessions from Mediavine (different denominators). If ads cut Amazon clicks by even 20%, they may lose money on the very best pages. Measure Amazon clicks per 1,000 views before and after ads go live on those pages. Agree any placement change with Mediavine first (90-day set-up period, CLAUDE.md).
- **Registration check as a selling point:** a visible "registration number checked against Health Canada's database" line on pesticide picks is a real difference from other affiliates and suits the licensed technician. I found no measurement of its effect.

### Gaps
- I did not verify which Canadian retailers run affiliate programmes, or their rates. No pest brands on Levanta were checked. Pinterest and Google Discover were not researched beyond the site's own image-search note. Selling the company's own products on Amazon.ca is outside this assignment.
