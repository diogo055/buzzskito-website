# Moving pages from positions 4-15 into the top 3, and affiliate product pages under Google's 2025-2026 systems

## Summary: tactics ranked by measured effect (size of the effect, marked down when the evidence is old, from one site, or uncontrolled)

1. **Enrich thin pages with facts that belong to that page (a spec or comparison table, related items, more depth):** about +20% organic traffic, with CTR rising from 3.43% to 4.27% while the control fell ([SearchPilot, Apr 2025](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/the-seo-impact-of-enriching-page-content)); +18% from page-specific copy, AI-written, on thin listing pages ([SearchPilot, Jun 2026](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/how-does-ai-content-impact-listing-pages)); Google calls deleting content a last resort ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-updates)). [C, G]
2. **Rewrite titles toward the searcher's own words and the format the query implies, but only as a controlled test:** 2024-2026 split tests range from +24% (dropping "Compare"), +11% ("best" first) and +8.5% (location first) down to -16% (airport codes) and over -20% (a first rewrite) ([SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/removing-compare-from-title-tags), [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/adding-location-to-start-of-title-tags-ctr), [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/expanding-your-title-tag-testing-strategy), [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/blog/when-seo-best-practices-backfire)); on this site, 9 titles that dropped "Canada/Ontario" earned 300 clicks against 158 expected ([project, Oct 29](C:/Users/buzzs/buzzskito-website/data/god-build/photo-upgrade-read-2026-10-29.md)). [C, P]
3. **Add a pros-and-cons table, and give specs their own section, on product and offer pages:** +50% organic with no featured snippet won (comparison site, 2023, older) and +5.5% at 90% confidence (retail, 2023, older) ([SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/adding-pros-cons-content-to-offer-pages), [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/pulling-specifications-into-standalone-section-improve-seo)). [C, older]
4. **Make freshness real and visible:** of about 15 variables tested on 3,000 ranking listicles, freshness was the one Indig found predicted a top-3 place over positions 8-10 (66.6% against 57.3% dated within two years; an old or unreadable date meant 56% lower odds) (Kevin Indig, Aug 2026, via [BizStack](https://bizstack.tech/what-3000-listicles-reveal-about-ranking-in-google-and-ai-search/) and [Index Lab](https://www.indexlab.ai/blog/listicles-still-dominate-but-rankings-churn)); a date changed without real changes is one of Google's own warning signs ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)). [R, G]
5. **Match the page's format to the query's wording:** queries that ask for a list put a listicle in the top 3 54.5% of the time, against 10.2% for implicit ones, and numbered lists beat unnumbered roundups in 56.8% of 6,114 head-to-heads (average best position 3.77 against 4.59) (Indig, Aug 2026, via [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/vendor-listicles-gain-38-median-traffic-since-googles-january-demotion/) and [Index Lab](https://www.indexlab.ai/blog/listicles-still-dominate-but-rankings-churn)). [R]
6. **Write FAQs as visible text, not markup:** +9.7% organic clicks from FAQs placed under footer copy on product listing pages ([SearchPilot, Apr 2026](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/will-adding-faq-content-to-footer-copy-improve-organic-traffic)); FAQ rich results stopped showing on May 7, 2026 ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/updates)); adding JSON-LD moved AI citations by -4.6% to +2.4%, which is noise ([Ahrefs via SEJ, May 2026](https://searchenginejournal.com/schema-markup-didnt-move-ai-citations-in-ahrefs-test/574568)). [C, G]
7. **Internal links from crawled, related pages:** +5% to +25% section traffic in controlled tests, with the receiving pages often flat, and a category-link module won at 95% in Jan 2026; on this site only the discovery effect is proven ([Oct 29 report](C:/Users/buzzs/buzzskito-website/reports/internal-linking-strategy-2026-10-29.md), [SearchPilot, Jan 2026](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/can-a-categories-accordion-boost-organic-traffic)). [C, P]
8. **Under AI Overviews, be the cited and recognized source:** cited brands got +120% clicks per impression against uncited ones, but still -38% against no overview ([Seer, Apr 2026](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-2026-update)); a recognized brand, .gov or .edu was picked first in 58% of cases where one appeared ([Indig UX study, May 2025](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ux-study-of-googles-ai-overviews/546580/)); Google claims links labelled Preferred get twice the click-through ([PPC Land, May 2026](https://ppc.land/google-brings-preferred-sources-into-ai-overviews-and-ai-mode-today/)). [R, G-via]
9. **Keep ad density and page experience in check while climbing:** across 4,000+ Raptive sites, ad density was one of the clearest site-level signals in the May 2026 core update; top performers also averaged 1,200+ words a page, and Raptive's update target is 7+ a month ([Raptive, Jun 2026](https://raptive.com/blog/googles-may-2026-core-update/)); Glenn Gabe tied a December 2025 drop to aggressive ads and autoplay ([Stan Ventures, secondary](https://www.stanventures.com/news/?p=6455)). [R, A]
10. **Measured as neutral or harmful, so don't:** a "key takeaways" block above a summary table, est. -6.5% Google organic ([SearchPilot/Omio, 2026](https://www.searchpilot.com/geo-a-b-testing-seo-and-geo-are-not-the-same)); photos alone, 174 web clicks against 176 expected here, though image clicks went from 16 to 135 ([project](C:/Users/buzzs/buzzskito-website/data/god-build/photo-upgrade-read-2026-10-29.md)); self-ranked "best" lists, dozens of sites down from Jan 2026 ([Lily Ray](https://lilyraynyc.substack.com/p/why-calling-yourself-the-best-could)); turnkey Amazon-data affiliate pages, 14K+ queries lost ([Glenn Gabe, Aug 2026](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/august-2026-google-spam-update-case-studies/)). [C, P, A]

## 1. On-page changes with measured ranking or CTR effects in 2025-2026

### 1a. Titles (and meta descriptions)

**Takeaway.** Titles are the fastest lever for pages already on page 1, and the riskiest. Controlled tests in 2024-2026 show wins of +4% to +24% and losses of -7% to over -20%, and "sensible" rewrites are often flat.
- **The wins share a pattern.** The title moves toward the words searchers actually use (or drops words they don't), puts the deciding word first (location, "best", the cost question), or shortens.
- **The losses add things the searcher didn't ask for:** codes, static prices, "(with Video)".
- **Google shows its own title about three-quarters of the time,** and since March 2026 has tested AI-written title links. Measure clicks against a control, not the tag.

**Measured, other sources**
- **[A] SEOTesting (page updated Mar 10, 2026).** On an affiliate site: adding the page's highest-impression queries to its title gave +27% clicks; adding month and year to a product review title gave +8.8% clicks on fewer impressions. No baseline numbers or control details — [SEOTesting](https://seotesting.com/blog/seo-test-page-titles/).
- **[R] John McAlpin, Apr 7, 2025: 24,886 titles across commercial and informational, mostly YMYL results** — [McAlpin](https://john-mcalpin-website.pages.dev/seo-data-study-how-often-google-changes-title-tags-and-why/).
  - Google showed a different title 76.04% of the time, rising to 79.23% for queries over 100K searches. Zyppy found about 61% in 2022-23. An Ahrefs estimate of 33.4% uses a different method (search summary only).
  - The brand name was the most common thing removed (63.0% of rewrites).
  - Titles shown as written averaged 44.47 characters, and 84.87% of them fell within 30-60 characters. 10.32% contained a year.
- **[P] This site, Oct 15-16 upgrades read on Oct 29** — [read](C:/Users/buzzs/buzzskito-website/data/god-build/photo-upgrade-read-2026-10-29.md):
  - 9 titles that dropped "Canada" or "Ontario": 300 clicks against 158 expected (+90%), impressions +65% against control. Mostly a recovery of what adding the word on Sep 17 had cost.
  - 10 titles that kept the word or never had one: +30% against control, within this group's normal swing.
  - The geo-title test of Oct 29 (6 changed, 5 held) is still unread — [plan](C:/Users/buzzs/buzzskito-website/data/god-build/geo-title-test-2026-10-29.md).

**Google and expert statements**
- **[G-via] AI-written title links.** On Mar 20, 2026, Google confirmed a small, limited test of AI-generated title links in web results, not only for news. A review of Ahrefs data by Brodie Clark found the scope still minimal. There is no opt-out and no marker on rewritten titles — [seo-kreativ, updated Oct 30, 2026](https://www.seo-kreativ.de/en/blog/google-ai-search-titles/).
- **[G-via, older] Click signals.** In 2023 trial testimony, Google's Pandu Nayak called Navboost (click-based) one of its strongest signals. **[O]** Cyrus Shepard (Oct 2026) reads the antitrust disclosures, patents and leaks as showing clicks matter more than SEOs assumed — [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/blog/winning-ai-citations-isnt-the-same-as-winning-in-search-with-cyrus-shepard). This is why a title change that wins clicks can also move rank, and why it is hard to separate the two.

**Inferences**
- For pages at positions 4-10, a title matching the dominant query's words and implied format is the cheapest test. The location-first result shows the gain can come from clicks alone, with no rank change.
- Roughly one title change in three loses in the table above. That is my count, not a published SearchPilot win rate. So title changes belong in a treatment-and-control batch like the Oct 29 geo-title test, never sitewide.
- The site's rule of 48 characters plus " | BuzzSkito" sits inside the length Google tends to keep, and the brand suffix is what Google drops most.
- Capitals have won three times in SearchPilot's tests (Feb 2024 +8.5%, Feb 2025 +14% mobile, Mar 2026 +17.5%). It looks odd and the mechanism is unknown, so try it only on a few product pages, if at all.
- Service side: location first (+8.5%) maps straight onto the city pages. That is a service-page change and needs the owner's approval.

**Gaps.** No 2025-2026 controlled title test on informational, health or pest pages: every win above is ecommerce, travel, healthcare clinics or editorial. SearchPilot publishes no overall title win rate.

### 1b. Refreshing and enriching content with new facts

**Takeaway.** The best-measured content change of 2025-2026 is filling a thin page with facts that belong to that page. Controlled tests show +14% to +20%, and that holds even for AI-written copy when it is specific to the page. Raptive's May 2026 data points the same way. Generic expansion and vendor "refresh" claims are weak. On this site, the Oct 15-16 upgrades lifted clicks by showing up for more searches, not by reaching the top 3.

**Measured**
- **[C] SearchPilot, Apr 11, 2025 (major retailer, nutrition-information pages).** It reused content from the product pages, added a spec table and a related-products list — [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/the-seo-impact-of-enriching-page-content).
  - Organic traffic about +20% over nearly six weeks.
  - CTR went from 3.43% to 4.27% on the variant while the control fell from 4.91% to 3.53%.
  - An earlier canonical test showed these pages serve a different intent from the product pages, so retiring them, which the client had considered, would have cost traffic.
- **[C] SearchPilot, Jun 26, 2026 (travel listing pages with little unique content).** Destination copy written with AI, rendered in the server HTML, gave +18% organic sessions — [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/how-does-ai-content-impact-listing-pages).
- **[C] SearchPilot, Nov 6, 2025 (ecommerce product pages).** A 3-5 sentence AI-written "product highlights" paragraph below the fold, ending with a note that AI wrote it, won and was rolled out globally. No percentage in the text — [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/what-happens-to-traffic-when-ai-content-is-added-with-a-disclaimer).
- **[C] SearchPilot, test date not given (Mar 2024 roundup).** Replacing templated content with unique human-written content gave +14% — [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/blog/10-seo-ab-tests-with-an-impact-of-over-10-percent).
- **[C] SearchPilot, Feb 7, 2025.** Removing an embedded expert-video carousel from brand listing pages gave +4.1%; class listing pages were inconclusive. The authors suspect speed and layout shift — [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/a-look-back-at-the-most-surprising-tests-of-2025).
- **[R] Raptive, May 2026 core update (4,000+ sites, 32 billion Google pageviews, ad network sample)** — [Raptive](https://raptive.com/blog/googles-may-2026-core-update/):
  - 70% of sites improved or held.
  - Holding up went with lower ad density, top performers averaging 1,200+ words a page, evergreen sites with under 6% of pages below 500 words, and a Semrush Authority Score above 35.
  - Raptive's targets built on the data: 7+ content updates a month, average content age under 24 months, top content refreshed at least yearly.
  - Top performers also averaged 10K+ email and 40K+ Pinterest pageviews a month.
- **[R] Cyrus Shepard, Apr 2026 (400+ sites, third-party traffic estimates).** Winners had proprietary assets 92.9% of the time against 57.1% for losers. First-hand experience and uniqueness of information showed no meaningful correlation — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/what-400-sites-reveal-about-organic-traffic-gains/571632/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-traffic-winners-in-2026-5-data-backed-traits-that-set-them-apart/).
- **[U, vendor] RepublishAI.** Updated pages lost 0.32 positions against 2.51 for never-updated pages over 76 days, and 31-100% more content was significant (p=0.026). The sample size is not disclosed (search summary only). HubSpot's often-quoted 106% refresh lift is old and has no control.
- **[A, vendor] SEOTesting / Better Collective.** Frequent updates to evergreen pages: clicks +614%, average position up 5+. No baseline, date or control described — [SEOTesting](https://seotesting.com/customers/better-collective/).
- **[P] This site, Oct 26 read of the Oct 15-16 "top 3" upgrades** — [memory](C:/Users/buzzs/.claude/projects/C--Users-buzzs-buzzskito-website/memory/project_upgrade_effect_read.md):
  - Head queries in the top 3: 3 before and 3 after, against 4 to 6 for the control.
  - Clicks +17% against a control at -16%, half of it from one page (no-see-um bites). The gain came from appearing for more searches.
  - The Nov 6-20 read of the Oct 23-24 set is pending.

**Google statements**
- **[G] Core updates documentation (section added Dec 2025).** Smaller, unannounced core updates run all the time, so improvements can show without waiting for a big one. Some changes take effect in days, others take months. Deletion is a last resort, though removing unhelpful content can help the rest of a site — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-updates).
- **[G] Generative AI content guidance (revised Oct 1, 2026).** Fact-check and review all AI-generated content by hand, including titles, descriptions, structured data and alt text — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-fact-check-ai-content-before-publishing/591782/).

**Inferences**
- For the 60 pages, add what only that page can carry: GTA season observations, label facts checked against PMRA or EPA, and a table of the options the page already discusses. More generic words are not the lever.
- In these tests AI drafting is not the problem; missing page-specific value is. Google's Oct 2026 bar is a human fact-check, and the Nov 3 audit found 0 of 255 guides with one.
- Measure the way SearchPilot does: treatment against control, 4-6 weeks, clicks and impressions, not rank alone.

### 1c. Original photos and labelled comparison images

**Takeaway.** The Oct 30 notes cover this in depth, and nothing new turned up. No 2025-2026 controlled test shows original photos lifting web rankings. On this site, photos alone did nothing for web clicks but multiplied image clicks. For product reviews, Google still asks for visual evidence of your own use. No test of labelled comparison images was found anywhere.

**Measured**
- **[P] Oct 29 read.** 10 photo-only pages: 174 web clicks against 176 expected. Image clicks on all 29 photo pages went from 16 to 135, against 1 to 0 for the control — [read](C:/Users/buzzs/buzzskito-website/data/god-build/photo-upgrade-read-2026-10-29.md).
- **[R] Indig listicles (Aug 2026).** Top-3 pages had 38.9% more images at the median, but this did not hold across adjusted models — [BizStack](https://bizstack.tech/what-3000-listicles-reveal-about-ranking-in-google-and-ai-search/).
- **[A] Sterling Sky (2025).** Swapping stock for unique photos on 5 pages changed rankings little. **[C, older] Reboot (2019):** unique images beat duplicates in one small test. Both are in the [Oct 30 notes](C:/Users/buzzs/buzzskito-website/research_notes/Google%20Images%20traffic%20growth%20plan/does_publishing_more_images_work.md).

**Google statements**
- **[G]** Reviews should show evidence such as visuals of your own experience — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews).
- **[G]** Relevant, high-quality images and video can appear in AI features (May 2026) — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).
- **[G]** A preferred thumbnail can be named through schema.org markup or og:image (Mar 2, 2026) — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/updates).

**Inference.** Photos serve Google Images and serve as review evidence; they are not a top-3 web lever. A real photo of the crew's own gear in use is the evidence type Google names for reviews, and it is untested here.

### 1d. Answer-first structure, FAQ, tables

**Takeaway.**
- **Answer-first blocks:** the one 2026 controlled test of an "answer first" block found it helped AI-assistant traffic but would have cost Google organic about 6.5%, so it was not shipped.
- **FAQs:** FAQ rich results are gone, but FAQ text still measured positive on ecommerce pages.
- **Tables:** the largest single content win in SearchPilot's archive is a pros-and-cons table (+50%, 2023).
- **Featured snippets,** the old target of answer-first writing, fell from about 15% to under 6% of results pages in the first half of 2025.

**Measured**
- **[C] Omio via SearchPilot, 2026** — [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/geo-a-b-testing-seo-and-geo-are-not-the-same); [SearchPilot, Jul 31, 2026](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/blog/lose-google-and-you-lose-ai-search):
  - Bulleted, quantitative "key takeaways" placed above an existing summary table: LLM traffic up, Google organic est. -6.5%. Not rolled out.
  - A brand-strengths module: +18% LLM traffic, Google organic inconclusive. Rolled out.
- **[C] SearchPilot, Apr 24, 2026.** FAQ content below footer copy on ecommerce listing pages gave +9.7% organic clicks (95%) — [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/will-adding-faq-content-to-footer-copy-improve-organic-traffic).
- **[C] Ahrefs, May 2026.** 1,885 pages that added JSON-LD, each matched to 3 control pages, difference-in-differences over 30 days: AI Overviews -4.6%, AI Mode +2.4%, ChatGPT +2.2%, all within noise — [SEJ](https://searchenginejournal.com/schema-markup-didnt-move-ai-citations-in-ahrefs-test/574568).
- **[C, older] Tables.** A pros-and-cons table on a comparison site's offer pages gave +50% with no featured snippet won (Jul 2023). Moving a spec table into its own H2 section gave +5.5%, significant at 90% but not at 95% (Oct 2023) — [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/adding-pros-cons-content-to-offer-pages); [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/pulling-specifications-into-standalone-section-improve-seo).
- **[R] Indig listicles (Aug 2026).** Schema markup, author bios and word count did not predict a top-3 position. Each standard deviation of extra headings went with 29% lower odds — [BizStack](https://bizstack.tech/what-3000-listicles-reveal-about-ranking-in-google-and-ai-search/).
- **[R] Ahrefs, Jul 22, 2025 (1M US desktop results pages).** Featured snippets fell from 15.41% to 5.53% of pages between January and June 2025, while AI Overviews rose from 3.93% to 27.43% — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/how-serp-features-have-evolved-in-the-ai-era).
- **[R] Indig AI Overview UX study (May 2025).** Median scroll depth inside an overview was 30%; most people stop after the top third — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ux-study-of-googles-ai-overviews/546580/).
- **[U] AI citations (search summaries only).** 44.2% of ChatGPT citations come from the first 30% of a page (Indig). CXL found 55% for Google AI Overviews on a sample of 100 citations, but its page returned 403.

**Google statements**
- **[G]** FAQ rich results stopped appearing on May 7, 2026, and their documentation was removed on Jun 15 — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/updates).
- **[G]** No need to split content into chunks for AI, no ideal page length; use paragraphs, sections and clear headings (May 2026) — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).

**Inferences**
- Put the deciding answer in the first lines of ordinary prose, since readers and AI both weight the top. Do not bolt a summary box onto existing pages without a control.
- Use FAQ sections as text where they answer real People Also Ask questions, and expect nothing from FAQPage markup.
- Use tables where a page compares options it already covers.

### 1f. Consolidating thin pages

**Takeaway.** The measured evidence favours enriching weak pages over retiring them, and merging only true duplicates. Google calls deletion a last resort. Mueller said in 2025-2026 that sitewide quality worries cut crawling and indexing, and that a site in a bad state may need to start over rather than edit pages. The one 2026 pruning case with numbers has no control, and its author doubts the cause.

**Measured**
- **[C] SearchPilot, Apr 2025 (section 1b).** Enrichment gave about +20%; a canonical test showed the pages served their own intent — [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/the-seo-impact-of-enriching-page-content).
- **[A, search summary; SEL returned 403] Search Engine Land pruning case (2026)** — [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/content-pruning-case-study-497056):
  - 198 of 600 URLs, which drew about 3% of clicks, were pruned.
  - Four months later clicks were +37%, impressions +49% and GA4 sessions +55%.
  - The author was unsure pruning caused it, and 278 redirected URLs still had 3,390 internal links pointing at them.
- **[A, older, search summaries only]** Seer (2023): an insurer was +23% year on year after retiring low-value pages. GoInFlow (about 2022): merging two similar pages gave +70% clicks a day over five weeks — [Seer](https://www.seerinteractive.com/work/case-studies/content-pruning-efforts-help-reverse-traffic-loss); [GoInFlow](https://www.goinflow.com/blog/content-consolidation-pruning-case-study/).
- **[P] This site.** The Nov 3 merges are live and unread: `best-ant-traps-canada` into `best-ant-killer`, basement dehumidifier into large-capacity. 50 template location pages went noindex the same day — [memory](C:/Users/buzzs/.claude/projects/C--Users-buzzs-buzzskito-website/memory/project_page_one_plan_nov2.md).

**Google statements**
- **[G]** Deletion is a last resort, for content that can't be salvaged; removing unhelpful content can help the good content perform better — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-updates).
- **[G-via] Mueller and Splitt, Jul 17, 2026.** When Google is seriously worried about a site's quality it crawls and indexes less. Quality includes the whole page experience, not only the text. Splitt asked why Google would index a page when others already cover the topic well — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-explains-seo-connection-of-site-quality-to-non-indexed-pages/582683/).
- **[G-via] Mueller, Nov 28, 2025.** A human rewrite doesn't make AI content authentic. A site in a bad state may need to start over, and recovery can be slower than on a new domain — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/googles-mueller-says-sites-in-a-bad-state-may-need-to-start-over/562109/).
- **[G] Canonical troubleshooting (clarified Jul 10, 2026).** After a fix, pages can stay grouped as duplicates for up to two weeks — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/canonicalization-troubleshooting).

**Inferences**
- Merge only where two pages answer the same query. `REGIONAL_PAIRS` are not duplicates.
- Try enrichment on a thin on-topic page before retiring it. Retire pages that are off-topic or cannot be fixed.
- The Nov 3 noindex of 50 location pages is the site's live pruning experiment. Read it before pruning more.

### 1g. Date and freshness signals

**Takeaway.** Freshness is the one variable that separated top-3 listicles from positions 8-10 in the largest 2026 study, and regular updates go with holding up in Raptive's May 2026 data. But:
- Google lists date-only changes among its warning signs.
- Google's AI Overviews do not favour newer pages.
- Artificial freshness appears among the traits of the self-promotional listicles demoted in January 2026.

Real updates with an accurate visible date work. Date bumps do not.

**Measured**
- **[R] Indig, Aug 31, 2026 (3,000 listicles, controls for vertical and query wording).** 66.6% of top-3 pages carried a date from the previous two years, against 57.3% at positions 8-10. Old or unreadable dates appeared on 8.2% against 15.1%, and went with 56% lower odds of a top-3 place — [Index Lab](https://www.indexlab.ai/blog/listicles-still-dominate-but-rankings-churn); [BizStack](https://bizstack.tech/what-3000-listicles-reveal-about-ranking-in-google-and-ai-search/).
  - The memo's subtitle says only freshness predicted a top-3 place, yet the same write-ups report numbered lists winning head-to-heads and more headings going with lower odds. "Only" probably means the variables that survived its controls in one model; the paywalled memo was not visible to check.
- **[R] Raptive, May 2026.** 7+ updates a month, average content age under 24 months — [Raptive](https://raptive.com/blog/googles-may-2026-core-update/).
- **[C] Dates in titles.**
  - SearchPilot (Mar 2024 roundup, test date not given): "Updated Daily" in a listings site's titles, an estimated +11% — [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/blog/10-seo-ab-tests-with-an-impact-of-over-10-percent). **[U]** A month-and-year-in-title test at +5% appears in another SearchPilot roundup (search summary only, not verified).
  - SplitSignal (2022, older): "updated 2022" in category titles was positive, which the authors read as a call to action rather than a freshness signal — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-split-test-result-updated-2022-title).
  - SEOTesting **[A]**: +8.8% clicks (section 1a).
- **[C, older] SearchPilot (2019 post).** A visible date changed daily gave +8%, and the author says not to fake dates — [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/blog/a-year-of-seo-testing-with-searchpilot).
- **[R] Ahrefs, Jul 2025 (17M citations).** AI assistants overall cite content 25.7% fresher than organic results, but Google's AI Overviews cite pages that are on average 16 days older than the organic results — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/do-ai-assistants-prefer-to-cite-fresh-content/).
- **[A] Demoted self-promotional listicles.**
  - Search Engine Land (Feb 2026; 403, search summary only) described them as ranking themselves first, often with "2026" in the title and little sign of real updates.
  - PPC Land's timeline lists artificial freshness updates and schema misuse among their shared traits — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/vendor-listicles-gain-38-median-traffic-since-googles-january-demotion/).
- **[P] This site, Nov 3.** At least 79 hand-written article titles contain "2026" in a `META_TITLE` constant (grep of `app/`; titles set elsewhere were not counted). From Jan 1, 2027 they show last year.

**Google statements**
- **[G]** Self-assessment question: are you changing page dates to look fresh when the content has not substantially changed? — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).
- **[G]** Show a visible, labelled date, and give `datePublished` and `dateModified` that match it — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/publication-dates).
- **[G-via, older]** Mueller (2021-2023): change the date when something is new or significantly changed; evergreen pages don't need it — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/googles-john-mueller-changing-dates-on-pages-wont-improve-rankings/401734/).

**Inferences**
- Tie every date change to a real content change. That is also the sitemap rule in CLAUDE.md.
- In January, re-date the "2026" titles only on pages that get a real update, and drop the year from the rest.
- Give each of the 60 pages a visible "Updated" line and `dateModified` when, and only when, it is genuinely updated.

## 2. Winning against AI Overviews: Google's 2026 guidance, and what gets a result clicked

**Takeaway.**
- **Google's guidance:** the May 2026 guide reduces "AI optimization" to ordinary SEO plus non-commodity content: a unique, first-hand perspective, no chunking, no llms.txt, no special schema, and product and local data kept current.
- **What gets clicked, from the measured studies:**
  - being cited inside the overview;
  - being a source people already recognize;
  - sitting on the first screen;
  - offering something the summary cannot finish: a decision, a tool, a product, a second opinion on a health worry.
- **Which page types still earn clicks** is covered in the [Nov 3 notes](C:/Users/buzzs/buzzskito-website/research_notes/Images%20and%20safe%20traffic%20growth%202026/search_clicks_late_2026.md). Navigational, transactional, shopping and local-service searches hold up; informational pages lose roughly 35-70% where an overview shows.

**Google's guidance and features**
- **[G] Optimization guide (May 15, 2026)** — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide); [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-optimize-generative-ai-mythbusting-41335.html):
  - AI features run on the core ranking systems, through grounding and query fan-out, so this is SEO.
  - Make non-commodity content. Google's example contrasts "7 Tips for First-Time Homebuyers" with a first-hand account of a sewer-line inspection.
  - Not needed: llms.txt or special markup, chunking, rewriting for AI, inauthentic brand mentions, structured data for AI's sake.
  - Keep Merchant Center feeds and Business Profiles current.
- **[G] Other documentation**
  - Spam policies now explicitly cover manipulating generative AI answers (May 15, 2026) — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).
  - AI features need no extra requirements. Their clicks count under the "Web" search type, and Google says they are higher-quality clicks — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).
- **[G-via] Preferred Sources** — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-brings-preferred-sources-into-ai-overviews-and-ai-mode-today/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-updates-preferred-sources-guide-doubling-publisher-click-through-rates/):
  - Since May 27, 2026, any site publishing fresh content is eligible, at domain or subdomain level only.
  - Chosen sites get a "Preferred" badge in AI Overviews and AI Mode.
  - Google claims these links are twice as likely to be clicked. More than 345,000 sources had been chosen.
  - Sites can link to `google.com/preferences/source?q=<domain>` or use a custom button (documented Aug 20, 2026).
- **[G] "Read more" deep links (Apr 20, 2026).** Eligibility rises when content is visible on load (not hidden in accordions or tabs), when no script forces the scroll position, and when hash fragments are kept — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/updates); [SEJ](https://beta.searchenginejournal.com/google-lists-best-practices-for-read-more-deep-links/572423/).
- **[G-via] Liz Reid (Jun 29, 2026 podcast).** Google wants great content to shine. In the interviewer's notes, an overview is not a substitute for the ten-minute article — [SER](https://seroundtable.com/google-we-want-great-content-to-shine-41582.html).
- More inline links placed higher in AI answers (Oct 15, 2026) are in the Nov 3 notes.

**Measured: what gets the click**
- **[R] Seer, Apr 2026 (53 brands).** Cited brands got +120% clicks per impression against uncited ones, and still -38% against no overview — [Seer](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-2026-update).
- **[R, older] Amsive, Apr 2025.** Average CTR -15.49% where an overview appears. Branded keywords with an overview were +18.68% (secondary sources only) — [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/ai-overviews-hurt-google-click-through-rates-39282.html); [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2025/04/22/research-googles-ai-eats-your-clicks/).
- **[R] Indig UX study (May 2025, 69 US users, about 350 overview observations)** — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ux-study-of-googles-ai-overviews/546580/):
  - Clicks inside the overview: 7.4% on desktop, 19% on mobile. Outbound clicks without an overview: 28% on desktop, 38% on mobile.
  - A recognized brand, authority, .gov or .edu was picked first in 58% of cases where one appeared. Links on the first screen were picked 71% of the time.
  - In 38% of overview sessions people opened a second organic result "to be sure".
  - Health searches got 52% scroll depth, against 34% for promo codes. About a third of the clicks an overview leaves behind go to Reddit, YouTube or forums.
- **[R] Ahrefs, Mar 2, 2026 (863K results pages).** 37.9% of URLs cited in AI Overviews rank in the top 10, against about 76% in July 2025. The parsing method changed, and AI Overviews moved to Gemini 3 in January 2026 — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-citations-top-10/).
- **[R] Shepard, Apr 2026 (400+ sites)** — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-traffic-winners-in-2026-5-data-backed-traits-that-set-them-apart/); [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/what-400-sites-reveal-about-organic-traffic-gains/571632/):

  | Trait | Winners | Losers |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | Offers a product or service | 70.2% | 34.6% |
  | Lets the visitor finish a task | 83.7% | 50.2% |
  | Proprietary assets | 92.9% | 57.1% |
  | Tight topical focus | 75.9% | 61.3% |
  | Strong brand | 32.6% | 16.1% |

  - Win rate: 13.5% for sites with none of the traits, 68.1% with four, 69.7% with five.
- **[R] Hasmord, Oct 17, 2026 (55 US shopping AI Overviews, hand-classified)** — [Hasmord](https://www.hasmord.com/ai-overview-sources-study):
  - Review sites and media were cited in 89%, YouTube in 85%, forums in 67% (Reddit 51%), brands in 49%, retailers in 27%.
  - 64% of cited sites were outside the organic top 10 for that exact question.
- **[C] Omio, 2026 (section 1d).** A block written for AI cost an estimated 6.5% of Google organic.

**Inferences for BuzzSkito**
- **Pest identification and bites.** The first click goes to a recognized health source. BuzzSkito's opening is the next step the overview can't finish: is it dangerous, what to do, who treats it locally.
- **The identifier tools fit "task completion",** the second-strongest trait in Shepard's data.
- **A "Preferred source on Google" link** on articles is a cheap lever Google claims doubles clicks, with no independent measurement. It only works for readers who choose it.
- **Don't add AI summary boxes untested.** The one controlled test says they can cost Google traffic.

**Gaps.** No 2026 study of what gets clicked under overviews on pest or identification searches specifically. No independent check of the Preferred Sources claim.

## 3. Affiliate product pages: what ranks now and what got sites demoted

**Takeaway.**
- **"Best X" results still often have a listicle in the top 3,** on about a third of queries, but Google has thinned them: the share at position 1 fell from 19-20% to 15-16% between January and August 2026.
- **What ranks:**
  - numbered lists that answer a query asking for a list;
  - pages kept genuinely current;
  - publishers more often than the brands selling the product.
  - Review media are the main source cited in shopping AI Overviews.
- **What got demoted:** thin programmatic affiliate pages (August 2026 spam update), self-ranked "best" lists at scale (from January 2026), and commodity content (January 2026).
- **Google's review guidance still asks for** evidence of your own use, measurements, comparisons, pros and cons from your own research, and links to more than one seller.
- **In Shepard's 400 sites, first-hand experience did not by itself separate winners from losers.** What did was having a product or service, a task the visitor can finish, and proprietary assets.

**Measured: what ranks**
- **[R] Indig, Aug 24, 2026 (5.32M results, 60K US desktop queries, 15 verticals, SE Ranking data)** — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/vendor-listicles-gain-38-median-traffic-since-googles-january-demotion/); [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/content-marketing/google-listicle-study-query-phrasing-august-2026/):
  - **Share of queries with a listicle:** 55.1% in the top 10, 32.3% in the top 3.
  - **By query wording (listicle in the top 3):** query asks for a list 54.5%; informational comparison 12.1%; implicit category 10.2%.
  - **January to August 2026:** listicle share at position 1 fell from 18.9-20.2% to 15.1-15.6%, and in the top 3 from 35.9-39.0% to 32.0-33.0%. Only 52.5% of queries kept the same position-1 URL.
  - **Publishers against brands:** publishers won 54.0% of 4,026 head-to-heads (average position 4.24 against 4.71).
  - **Forums and video:** Reddit or YouTube appeared on 92% of results pages that had a top-10 listicle. When Reddit was in the top 10, it was in the top 3 70.7% of the time.
  - **AI Overviews** appeared on 83.7% of these queries on average.
- **[R] Indig, Aug 31, 2026 (3,000 listicles)** — [BizStack](https://bizstack.tech/what-3000-listicles-reveal-about-ranking-in-google-and-ai-search/); [Index Lab](https://www.indexlab.ai/blog/listicles-still-dominate-but-rankings-churn):
  - Numbered lists beat editorial roundups in 56.8% of 6,114 head-to-heads (top 3 55.9% against 41.4%; average best position 3.77 against 4.59). Editorial roundups won in consumer tech, fitness and automotive.
  - Freshness predicted a top-3 place. Schema, author bios and word count did not.
  - Vendor-first lists grew 3.5 times faster raw, but only +4% after controls (p = 0.91). 62% of vendor listicles gained traffic after January (median +38%).
- **[C] SearchPilot product and comparison tests (2023-2024):** pros-and-cons table +50%; "best" or "The Best" in titles +10% to +11%; spec section +5.5% (sections 1a and 1d).
- **[R] Hasmord shopping overviews (section 2).** Review sites and media are the most-cited source type; brands 49%, retailers 27%.
- **[A, in the Nov 3 notes] HouseFresh** passed its pre-update (September 2023) visibility peak by October 2025, after product testing, design work and building a YouTube audience — [named sites notes](C:/Users/buzzs/buzzskito-website/research_notes/Images%20and%20safe%20traffic%20growth%202026/named_sites_wins_and_losses.md).

**Google's rules for review pages**
- **[G] Write high-quality reviews (current page)** — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews):
  - Show you know the product, with evidence (visuals, audio, links) of your own experience.
  - Give quantitative measurements, and say what sets the product apart.
  - Cover comparable products and which suits which use, with benefits and drawbacks from your own research.
  - Consider links to multiple sellers.
  - Back "best overall" or "best for X" picks with first-hand evidence, and make ranked lists useful enough to stand alone.
  - Quality and originality matter, not length.
- **[G] Spam policies: thin affiliation.** Merchant descriptions or reviews copied without added value are thin. Good affiliate pages add price information, original reviews, rigorous testing and ratings — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).
- **[G] Review markup.** Since Jul 24, 2026, fake or undisclosed incentivized reviews in review snippets can draw a manual action. Since Nov 12, 2025, mark what is reviewed in one way only — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/updates).
- **[G] Authorship (Oct 1-2, 2026).** Fabricated creator profiles (AI headshots, made-up names, false credentials) count as deception — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/seo/google-helpful-content-main-content-effort-originality-fake-authors/); [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).

**Measured and reported: what got sites demoted**
- **[A] Glenn Gabe, August 2026 spam update (Aug 18-21)** — [GSQi, Aug 31, 2026](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/august-2026-google-spam-update-case-studies/):
  - An Amazon affiliate site built programmatically from Amazon data lost 14K+ queries. Some of its categories had no product images, and the text at the bottom scored 97%+ AI on his detector. He doubts it can recover without rebuilding how the site works.
  - A product-information site with 1.5M URLs fell site-wide: 85% programmatic, many pages only a photo and information found elsewhere, aggressive ads.
- **[A] Lily Ray: self-ranked "best" lists** — [Lily Ray](https://lilyraynyc.substack.com/p/why-calling-yourself-the-best-could); [SER, Mar 13, 2026](https://www.seroundtable.com/is-google-algorithm-the-hitting-llm-tactic-of-self-promotional-listicles-41071.html):
  - **Timing:** around Jan 20, 2026, at least dozens of sites with hundreds or thousands of lists ranking their own brand first fell fast. The drop hit the listicle subfolders first, then whole domains, and accelerated in the May 2026 core update.
  - **Scale:** about 30 sites fit her criteria by March (SER); her later Substack analysis covers over 40. Secondary sources cite drops of 29-49%.
  - **In AI Overviews:** those lists were cited, but the brand behind them was left out of the recommendation 69% of the time (April-June 2026), and 83% in September (her X post, search result only).
  - **Counterpoint [R]:** Indig's controlled comparison found no average penalty for vendor-first lists.
- **[A] Gabe, Jun 10, 2026.** Sites hit in January for commodity content and self-serving listicles had not recovered, and some fell further in May — [GSQi](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/core-roars-back-google-may-2026-core-update-analysis/).
- **[R] Raptive, May 2026.** Deals was among the most volatile verticals. **[U]** A secondary list puts DealNews at -44% — [Raptive](https://raptive.com/blog/googles-may-2026-core-update/).

**Inferences for BuzzSkito**
- **Product pages: the strongest evidence the site can honestly show.** Photos of the crew's own gear in use (the owner confirmed on Nov 3 that the crew uses it), measurements the crew can really take, and a pros-and-cons table from that use. No invented testing (site rule 8).
- **Numbered titles** where the query asks for a list ("best wasp traps"), with real dates.
- **Links to more than one seller.** Google suggests it; the site is Amazon-only by design. That is the owner's commercial call, and no measured effect was found.
- **Risk flag: the 13 `/best-mosquito-control-companies-*` pages.** All 13 list BuzzSkito first in their ItemList markup and carry "2026" in their title lines; at least 5 also head the visible list with "1. BuzzSkito" (for example `app/best-mosquito-control-companies-toronto/page.tsx`). That is the pattern Ray documented. Indig's controls found no average penalty, so the risk is real but unmeasured here. These are service pages, so it is the owner's decision; the Nov 3 audit already lists it as open.
- **Shopping overviews cite review media and YouTube.** HouseFresh's recovery went with a YouTube audience. That is a co-occurrence, not a tested cause.

**Gaps.** No 2025-2026 controlled test of original product photos, of "where to buy" sections, or of buyer's-guide sections on affiliate rankings. No named affiliate site has published Search Console data from a 2026 recovery.

## 4. Publishing pace: anything new since the Nov 3 notes

**Takeaway.** Still no safe number. The new items say the same thing more firmly:
- Google spends crawl and index effort according to perceived quality.
- Scaled low-effort content is its growing concern.
- Updates to existing pages, not new pages, are what the 2026 "holding up" data counts.

**Findings**
- **[R] Raptive, May 2026.** The pace measure that went with holding up was 7+ content updates a month: updates, not new pages. The June 2025 figure in the Nov 3 notes was 1 new post and 5 updates a month — [Raptive](https://raptive.com/blog/googles-may-2026-core-update/).
- **[G-via] Gary Illyes, Search Central Live Barcelona, Oct 2, 2026 (attendee recap, no method given)** — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-says-new-pages-take-about-20-hours-to-be-found-some-never-are/):
  - New URLs are typically found in about 20 hours, and known URLs re-crawled in about 30 days.
  - Sitemap processing takes up to 14 days, or never on quality grounds. Indexing can take months, or never, for the same reason.
  - Scaled content is becoming a bigger problem than link spam.
- **[G] Oct 1, 2026.** Fact-check all AI-generated content by hand, metadata included. The scaled-content warning is unchanged — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-fact-check-ai-content-before-publishing/591782/).
- **[G]** Producing lots of content on many different topics in the hope some performs is one of Google's warning signs — [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).
- **[G-via]** Mueller (Jul 2026, Nov 2025): quality worries cut crawling and indexing, and a site in a bad state may need to restart (section 1f).
- **[U]** Reports of more pages dropping out of the index since April 2026 are unverified. Mueller reportedly called it nothing exceptional.

**Inference.** The Nov 3 pace decision (at most 10 new pages a day and 25 in any 7 days) is consistent with this. For the climb, the measured lever is updating existing pages, which the owner's rule leaves unlimited.

## 5. Internal linking: measured effects in 2025-2026

**Takeaway.** Nothing in 2025-2026 changes the Oct 29 conclusion. Links help pages get found and give modest section-level gains in big-site split tests. On this site, beyond discovery, more links showed no measurable effect. No controlled test isolates "links from high-traffic pages".

**Findings**
- **[C] SearchPilot, Jan 16, 2026.** A collapsible block of links to popular categories and services on store pages won at 95% (no percentage published) — [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/can-a-categories-accordion-boost-organic-traffic).
- **[C, older] SearchPilot, 2024.**
  - Removing the self-link from the last breadcrumb: -5.5% (Jul 2024).
  - A shorter link block with "show more": +10.2%, at 90% only (Apr 2024).
  - Sources: [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/removing-self-referential-breadcrumb); [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/resources/case-studies/seo-split-test-lessons-reducing-link-block).
- **[C] Earlier SearchPilot tests (Oct 29 report).** Nearby-location links +7%, category links +25%, a related-article block grown from 2 to 4 links +16% on the pages showing it with the receiving pages flat, and links to already-popular pages no change (2026) — [report](C:/Users/buzzs/buzzskito-website/reports/internal-linking-strategy-2026-10-29.md).
- **[P] This site.** Discovery is the measured effect (the 35 bed-bug pages). With page length held equal, doubling inbound links showed nothing (-4%). A 40-against-40 test is planned — [report](C:/Users/buzzs/buzzskito-website/reports/internal-linking-strategy-2026-10-29.md).

**Inference.** For the 60 pages, run the planned test: 1-2 links each from older, well-crawled pages, with anchors in the searchers' words. Expect 0-20%, not the top 3.

## 6. What this means for the ~60 pages (inference, built on the findings above)

- **The ceiling, measured here.**
  - On Oct 26, the 126 untouched pages at positions 4-15 would add about 1,370 clicks per 28 days if all reached about position 3, and the top 50 hold 1,046 of that.
  - CTR at positions 1-3 on this site: about 3.0% for Canadian buying or fixing searches, 1.7% for Canadian questions, 0.7-0.9% in the US (Sep 28-Oct 25).
  - Source: [memory](C:/Users/buzzs/.claude/projects/C--Users-buzzs-buzzskito-website/memory/project_upgrade_effect_read.md). Expect seasonal pages to be worth more in May-July than these October rates imply.
- **Order of work, by evidence.**
  1. Pages at positions 4-10: a title test first. It is cheap, and the gain can come from clicks alone.
  2. Pages at positions 8-15: enrich with facts specific to the page, plus a real update and a visible date.
  3. Product pages: a pros-and-cons table, a spec or comparison table, evidence of the crew's own use, and a numbered title where the query asks for a list.
  4. Links from 1-2 older, well-crawled pages, in the planned test.
- **Test design.**
  - Matched pairs and a coin flip, as in the geo-title test, with one kind of change per group.
  - Read at 4 and 8 weeks: Google re-crawls known pages in about 30 days, and SearchPilot's tests run 2-6 weeks.
  - Measure clicks and impressions against the control.
  - Never change pages that sit inside another read's window (Nov 6-20 reads; geo-title test).
- **Season.** Mosquito and tick pages can only be read in season. Their upgrades are already planned for February-March, which leaves one read before May.
- **Leave out.**
  - Summary boxes bolted on top of pages, and schema added for AI.
  - Photo-only upgrades aimed at web rank.
  - Date bumps without content changes.
  - Deleting pages before trying to enrich them.
  - Self-ranked lists.
- **Fixed date:** the 79+ "2026" titles in January 2027.
- **Ads.** Mediavine's ad density is now part of page experience on these pages. Raptive's data ranks ad density among the clearest site-level signals. Mediavine's 90-day set-up period limits changes, so agree any change with Mediavine first.
